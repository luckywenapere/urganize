# LinkedIn outreach

Outbound only: the first touch, not the sale. Every DM, reply and connection request is shown to Oyin and sent only after his yes. Likes need no approval.

**The goal is a person who actually uses Urganize, not just an account (since 27 Nov 2026, #1413).** A bare sign-up link does not work: of 36 people who got it from 3 to 26 Nov, 4 made an account and none of them used it; most said "will check it out" and went quiet. So when someone is interested, offer to set Urganize up with them for their artist on a short call (the approved line is in "When someone is interested"), then ask for their WhatsApp.

## What Urganize is (read before every draft)

**Urganize covers the FULL LIFE CYCLE of a music career, never just releases (Oyin, 27 Nov 2026, his 4th correction).** It is "the operating system for independent music careers": artists and managers run the whole career like a business there: the next record (songs, sessions, producers), releases, shows, pitching, press, distribution, money and the team. The hook is "A music career shouldn't run on a group chat."

- Never describe, offer or pitch it around "a release", "your next release", "you add a release, you get the plan" or "software to run releases". A release is one thing among many.
- What you may say: the AI plans with you, the team gets reminders on WhatsApp, and some plans include distribution. Quote no prices unless he says so.
- The assistant is called Chappi in anything a person reads. Never "UrgAns".
- Retired, never say: "where music teams work, from the idea to everything that comes after", "from idea to execution", "the team app for music release", "AI workspace for music executives", "a planning and coordination app" (10 Nov), "helps music professionals run their music businesses better".
- The memory note `urganize-positioning-current` is the authority. If it and this file disagree, it wins; say so to Oyin.

## Outbound run

2. For each, open `/in/<slug>/recent-activity/all/`. Find a post they wrote. A post that starts "X reposted this" doesn't count. Scroll past reposts. Their own words over a quoted post count.

6. Show Oyin ONE batch for the day, in chat (inbox replies, first DMs and connection requests together, each with name, link and exact text). Separate batches pile up unapproved.

## Opening lines

Before showing any DM or post draft, run its first line through the `writing-hooks` skill's 5 checks in short mode (one line, no 10 options for a DM). His voice rules below still win on wording.

## His voice

Write only in phrases he uses. From his DMs:
- "Hello good afternoon", "trust you're well?", "Very well thank you."
- "Oh nice that's exciting.", "Oh very nice", "Appreciate.", "Sounds good"
- "Who are you working with right now, if you don't mind me asking?"
- "What are you working on next with them?"
- "I'm the founder of a music tech startup and we're trying to solve problems for people such as yourself."
- "Anytime works, you can pick here https://call.urganize.app"

Short, loose punctuation, no polished marketing lines. Write like him from the first draft: he rejected polished drafts as "robotic". Reuse his phrases but never a product description from his old DMs.

Second replies stay short. If they say they'll check their calendar, "Sounds good. I'm flexible." is enough.

## When someone is interested

They say what they're working on, ask what Urganize is, or sound interested. Send the approved line (Oyin picked it, 27 Nov 2026):

> Oh very nice. Most managers I talk to run their artists' whole careers on WhatsApp group chats. Urganize is the place for that instead: the next record, shows, pitching, distribution and the team, all in one plan. Let me set it up with you for your artist on a 15 minute call, you can pick a time here https://call.urganize.app

Once they name an artist, ask: "Can I have your WhatsApp number? Easier to send you the plan there."

If they would rather sign up on their own, send https://urganize.app/login and say there are plans for one artist up to a full label and Oyin can help them pick. There is no Free plan (paid plans only since 10 Nov 2026). Never write "start free", "free for as long as you like" or "get started". Quote no prices unless he says so. Sign-up is open, so nobody waits to be let in.

Look up any artist a prospect names before replying. A big artist means Oyin onboards them himself: flag it, don't send the standard line.

## Names

- Use the first name from the profile. If it's unclear, say so when you show the draft. Examples: "Wilson Mary Oluwayemisi" (used Mary), "Tayebwa David" (Ugandan names often put the surname first, so used David), "rodricci ." (no real name, so no name).
- Never guess a name from a handle.

## Inbox replies

1. Open `/messaging/?filter=unread`. Opening a thread marks it read, so list every thread you open in case one gets missed. Threads you opened earlier in the session won't show as unread any more, so add them by hand.
2. Read each thread's last messages before drafting. If they asked a question, answer it. If they sound interested, use "When someone is interested". Calls already booked stay booked.
3. Skip chats that have ended ("Alright thank you", "Thanks"). Don't reply just to have the last word. Skip vendor pitches and ads.
4. Never guess a fact about Oyin or the company. If they ask something you can't find in the thread or memory, write your best guess, mark it as a guess, and ask him.
5. Old threads: say what you can't tell. E.g. a call agreed months ago with no follow-up: did it happen? Flag it when you show the draft.
6. Lay out drafts as: name, what they said (one line, their words), then the draft. Then wait for his yes.
7. Right before sending, re-read the last message in the thread. People reply while you work, and the draft may no longer fit.
8. Send, check each shows in the thread, then check the Unread filter again: new replies usually come in during the run.
9. When someone sends a phone number, add them to the WhatsApp follow-ups file straight away (name, number, who they are, date, what to say, Messaged? No). Never put numbers in the repo or the outreach log; the log only says "sent his number, Oyin to message". In your summary, tell Oyin to message them. When you ask someone for their number, add them under "Number asked".
10. LinkedIn only lets you edit a sent message for about an hour. After that, fix mistakes with a short follow-up, with his yes.

## Browser notes

- Open an existing thread: click the "Search messages" box, type the name, press Enter, then open the first result. Putting `?searchTerm=` in the URL does not run the search.
- Message a person: on their profile or activity page, click Message. Check the chat header shows their name before typing.
- Invites: click Connect (or More, then Connect), then "Send without a note". Check it in Sent invitations.
- Wait for each page and thread to load before clicking: the thread pane can still show the previous person for a second or two.
- Don't run long in-page scripts or load hundreds of conversations at once: the LinkedIn tab freezes. Read threads a few at a time.
- The safety check sometimes blocks a send once ("Real-World Transactions"). After Oyin's yes, retry once. If it blocks again, stop and tell him.
- After a send, the thread can take a few seconds to show the new message. Re-open the thread to check before you call it failed or send again.
