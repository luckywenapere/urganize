# Urganize: hook context

Read this when writing hooks for Urganize posts, DMs or emails.

## Who reads

First: artist managers running more than one artist, small management teams and small labels. Second: independent artists. Nigeria first, but posts reach anywhere. Sell to the manager, serve the artist.

## What the product is (say it this way)

"Urganize is an app that helps artists, managers and record labels plan new music and keep track of every task. It asks about your goals, then makes a plan that fits. You can also make your own. Use it solo or with a team. After every task, Urganize asks how it went, so you can see what worked when the release is out." (founder positioning, 9 Nov 2026; his "so it keeps learning from you" is not true yet, never use it). Short line: "Plan new music. Track every task."

Retired lines, never use: "From idea to execution", "keeps learning from you", "where music teams manage everything from idea to execution", "plan every release and deliver it on time", "the team app for music release", "AI workspace for music executives", "AI Music Business Manager", "Everything after the music", "Every release, on plan".

## Copy rules

- Urganize is a waitlist beta. Never "start free", "sign up", "get started".
- Say "deliver", not "ship".
- No em dashes. No hype, no startup cliches.
- "Interaction app" is internal only.

## Pains that make good hooks (real, from the product's thesis)

- One overworked person holding every release together.
- The plan lives in group chats, spreadsheets and someone's head.
- Nobody knows what is due this week, or who owns it.
- Cover art, metadata and the distributor deadline land in the last few days.
- Running several artists' releases at once with no shared view.

## Numbers

Only use a number Oyin has confirmed. Don't use user counts, revenue or growth figures unless he gives them for that post.
