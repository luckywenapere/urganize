# Urganize: hook context

Read this when writing hooks for Urganize posts, DMs or emails.

## Who reads

First: artist managers running more than one artist, small management teams and small labels. Second: independent artists. Nigeria first, but posts reach anywhere. Sell to the manager, serve the artist.

## What the product is (say it this way)

"Urganize is the operating system for independent music careers. It helps independent artists and managers run their careers as businesses: planning, coordinating and growing the career, not just keeping a task list." (founder positioning, 26 Nov 2026, issue #1405.) Headline: "A music career shouldn't run on a group chat." Short line: "Run your music career like a business." Never say "keeps learning from you": it is not true yet.

Retired lines, never use: "a planning and coordination app", "Plan new music. Track every task.", "From idea to execution", "keeps learning from you", "where music teams manage everything from idea to execution", "plan every release and deliver it on time", "the team app for music release", "AI workspace for music executives", "AI Music Business Manager", "Everything after the music", "Every release, on plan".

## Copy rules

- Sign-up is open (since 8 Nov): the button says "Start free", and "Free for as long as you like" under it. Never "get started".
- Say "deliver", not "ship".
- No em dashes. No hype, no startup cliches.
- "Interaction app" is internal only.

## Pains that make good hooks (real, from the product's thesis)

- One overworked person holding every release together.
- The plan lives in group chats, spreadsheets and someone's head.
- Nobody knows what is due this week, or who owns it.
- Cover art, metadata and the distributor deadline land in the last few days.
- Running several artists' releases at once with no shared view.

## Numbers

Only use a number Oyin has confirmed. Don't use user counts, revenue or growth figures unless he gives them for that post.
