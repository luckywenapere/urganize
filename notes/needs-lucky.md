- **Readable rollout URLs.** Rollouts only have `/dashboard?release=<uuid>` links, so a rollout cannot be bookmarked or shared as a readable address. Recommendation: add a slug route like `/dashboard/rollouts/<slug>` that redirects to the existing URL, keeping the uuid link working.
- **Retake the marketing product shots.** The press kit shot on the marketing site still reads "Made with Urganize, the operating system", a line the app dropped on 2026-09-24. Retaking shots (`pnpm --filter @urganize/homepage shots`) needs a signed-in headed Chrome on a seeded demo team, which the sandbox the marketing loop runs in cannot do. Recommendation: run it once from your machine and commit the new images and `content/product-shots.ts`. Until then the marketing loop hides that footer line by cutting the bottom off the frame in `FeatureGridSection.tsx` (`aspect-[780/1540]`); delete that class after the retake.
- **Real phone captures for three marketing shots.** On a phone the homepage's step shots (the activity list, the open activity, the Timeline) and the `/features` activity list are crops of the 1920px desktop captures (`apps/homepage/content/product-shot-crops.ts`), because there is no phone capture of them. The crops are tied to where things sit in the current images. Recommendation: when you run `pnpm --filter @urganize/homepage shots` for the retake above, add phone captures of those three screens to the script (like `catalogPhone` and `epkPhone`), switch those sections to `ResponsiveProductShot`, and delete the crop file. If you only retake the desktop shots, open the homepage at 390px afterwards and check the three crops still show the list, the assignee row and the week axis.
- **/pricing's bottom block promises a conversation the page does not have.** The last block on `/pricing` is headed "Not ready to talk yet?" with "Leave your email. We'll reach out when the time is right for your team." above a waitlist form. Nothing above it is a talk step any more: Solo, Manager and Label all say "Try Urganize" and go to the waitlist, and Group's "Let's talk" opens an email (the section that used to walk through a call and a demo was deleted 2026-09-09). So "Not ready to talk yet?" answers a question the page never asked, and "when the time is right for your team" sounds like a sales follow-up rather than a place in the queue. It is the headline of a page that sets the offer, so it was not changed here. Two things to decide: the words, and whether the card buttons should scroll to this form instead of leaving for the homepage (they all go to `/#waitlist`, the homepage's form, while this page carries its own form below them). Recommendation: change the heading to "Join the waitlist." and the line under it to "Leave your email. We'll reach out when your spot opens." (the same promise the form's second step makes: "so we can reach you when your spot opens"), and leave the card buttons as they are until you decide whether the pricing page should be a dead end for the waitlist.

## Sentry from the browser is blocked by CORS (F-09, QA1 Q-1-01)
Every page logs a CORS error to `o4512277649096704.ingest.de.sentry.io`. I read the setup and the code is not wrong: the browser SDK sends to the project's own DSN host, and next.config.js sets no tunnel and nothing in the app restricts connections. What to check in Sentry (project urganize-web): Settings, Security & Privacy, "Allowed Domains": it should be empty or list app.urganize.app. Also check the DSN in Vercel (NEXT_PUBLIC sentry DSN) is the urganize-web project's, and that the project is not over quota (an over-quota answer has no CORS header and looks like this). If both are fine, a browser ad blocker on the QA machine is the other likely cause; then add a `tunnelRoute` to withSentryConfig (costs one function call per event). No code change pushed.
