title: "Roles and permissions"
description: "What a Manager, Artist, Team Member and Viewer can do."
---

When you invite someone, you pick their role. The role decides what they can change.

## Roles at a glance

| Role | Best for | What they can do |
| - | - | - |
| **Manager** | Managers and label staff | Everything: make rollouts and plans, manage artists, invite people, handle billing. |
| **Artist** | The artist | Sees only their own rollouts. Can work on activities. |
| **Team Member** | Producers, designers, publicists | Sees the team's rollouts. Can work on activities. |
| **Viewer** | Anyone who only needs to look | Can see rollouts. Cannot change anything. |

The person who made the team is its **Owner**. The owner can always do everything.

## Good to know

- Only Managers can invite people. See [Your team](/account/team).
- When you invite an Artist, you also pick which artist they are. They only see that artist's rollouts.
- Only the person an activity is assigned to, and the team's Managers, can post in that activity's chat.
- **Billing** shows in Settings for Managers and for an artist who runs their own team.
