Signing up takes a few minutes. At the end you have a rollout with a plan, ready for your team.

## Sign up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the app">
    Go to [app.urganize.app](https://app.urganize.app).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say who you are">
    Pick one: Artist manager, Label, Solo artist, Artist team or Other. This decides what your menu shows first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer a few questions">
    Tell us what you are working on (Single, EP, Album or Music Video), where a rollout usually falls apart for you, and what is already done.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell us about this rollout">
    Type the artist's name. Add the title and release date if you have them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Use **Continue with Google** or your email. Got a referral code? Type it in here.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start your free trial">
    Pick a plan and tap **Start my free trial**. The trial lasts 3 days and needs no card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bring your team in">
    Invite teammates by email, or tap **Skip for now**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Your first plan

Urganize opens your new rollout and starts the planner. Answer the last few questions and tap **Build my plan**. It takes about half a minute.

<Tip>
  Your first plan is free. It does not use any UrgAns credits.
</Tip>

"Confirm your release date" sits at the top of your first plan. Do it first, because every date in the plan counts from release day.

## Invite one teammate

When you start scrolling your first plan, Urganize asks you to invite one teammate. Tap **Skip for now** if you are working alone. It asks again the next day, until you invite someone.
