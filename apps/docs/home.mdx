Home is where you land. It shows what needs you today.

## What you see

- **Opportunities**: new grants picked for your team, then the deals and grants you are chasing. Tap one, or **See all**.
- **Getting started**: a short checklist for new teams. Tap **Hide getting started** when you are done with it.
- **Up next**: what to work on next, with a button to open its rollout.
- **Next moves**: up to three things to do next, one tap each. Tap the cross and that one does not come back.
- **Rollouts**: every rollout in flight, with how far along it is.
- **Where the money went**: what each rollout has cost so far. Tap **Add spend** to log a cost.
- **Your team**: who has joined. A green dot means they are online now.

## Chat with UrgAns

On a computer, Home's chat sits beside your cards. **Chat** is one ongoing conversation. To start a fresh one, switch to **Sessions**.

Press **Home** again while you are on Home, or tap **UrgAns** in the top menu, to make the chat full width.

See [UrgAns](/urgans/overview) for what you can ask.
