Go to **Opportunities** in the top menu. Your newest opportunities also show at the top of Home.

## Add an opportunity

1. Tap **Add opportunity**.
2. Pick the type: **Deal**, **Grant**, **Sync**, **Brand**, **Partnership** or **Other**.
3. Give it a **Title**. Add **Who it is with**, what it is **Worth**, your **Next step**, a **Do it by** date and the date it **Closes on**.

Under **Where it stands**, move it along as it goes: **Idea**, **Reaching out**, **Talking**, **Negotiating**, then **Won**, **Lost** or **Passed**.

## Get grants suggested

Fill in your **Grant profile** once:

- **Based in**
- **Career stage**
- **Who applies**: the artist as a person, or a registered company or label
- **Genres** (up to 3)
- **Money for** (up to 5), like recording, touring or video

Tap **Save profile**. Each week we suggest up to three open grants that fit, with the award, the deadline and why it matched. You get a notification when new ones arrive.

Add a suggestion and it becomes a grant in your list, linked to the original call. Dismiss it and we will not suggest it again.

<Note>
  Suggested grants are part of paid plans.
</Note>
