## On a computer

The top menu has these parts:

| Menu | What it is for |
| - | - |
| **Home** | Your day: opportunities, what's next, your rollouts and your team. |
| **UrgAns** | The assistant, full width. |
| **Catalogue** | **Rollouts** and **Artists**. |
| **Relationships** | **People**, **Directory** and **Shows**. |
| **Opportunities** | Deals, grants, sync and brand work you are chasing. |
| **Audience** | Coming soon. |

When a part has more than one page, its pages show in a menu down the left side. Open a rollout and its own menu sits under **Rollouts**. Tap the fold button to hide the side menu.

On the right of the top menu:

- **Search or ask UrgAns**: find activities, rollouts and pages, or ask UrgAns.
- **Notifications**: the bell.
- **Your account menu**: **Settings**, your UrgAns credits, **Report an issue**, **Docs**, **Contact us**, **Community** and **Sign out**.

<Note>
  Your menu leads with what you need, based on who you said you are. Anything left out sits under **More**, and search still finds it.
</Note>

## On a phone

The bottom bar has **Home**, **Catalogue**, **UrgAns** and **More**. **More** holds Relationships, Opportunities, search, notifications, your account and help.

## Keyboard shortcuts

On a computer:

| Press | To |
| - | - |
| `G` then `H` | Go to Home |
| `G` then `U` | Go to UrgAns |
| `G` then `C` | Go to Catalogue |
| `G` then `R` | Go to Relationships |
| `G` then `O` | Go to Opportunities |
| `/` | Start typing to UrgAns |
| `C` | New activity |
| `⌘K` or `Ctrl+K` | Search |
