The plan is the list of activities that gets your release out, each with a date. UrgAns builds it from a proven playbook for singles, EPs and albums, then shapes it to your answers.

## Build the plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the planner">
    On a rollout with no plan, tap **Plan the rollout**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer the questions">
    UrgAns asks who the artist is, when it drops, what is already done, what matters most, your promo budget and what the plan should cover. It skips anything it already knows.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build it">
    Tap **Build my plan**. It takes about half a minute and lands on the rollout.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Not sure of a date? Tap **Help me pick**. You get three Fridays to choose from. Each one says how many weeks you get, whether there is time to pitch Spotify editorial, and whether another of your rollouts lands the same week.

When the plan lands, a short note says what UrgAns built, which activities to **Start with:**, and **Promo that fits:** your song.

<Note>
  Your team's first plan is free. Later plans use about 12 UrgAns credits. See [UrgAns credits](/urgans/credits).
</Note>

## Phases

Every rollout has three phases:

| Phase | When |
| - | - |
| **Pre-release** | Up to 8 days before release day |
| **Release** | From a week before to a week after release day |
| **Post-release** | From 8 days after release day |

Each phase shows its dates and how much is done. The phase you are in now is marked **Now**.

An activity gets its phase from its date. To move it, open the activity and change **Phase**.

## The timeline

Tap **See the timeline** to see every activity on a calendar, grouped by phase. Drag an activity to move its date. Tap it to open it. On a computer, use full screen for more room.

## Change anything

The plan is yours. Add, remove, rename or re-date any activity. If you move the release date, check that the dates still fit.
