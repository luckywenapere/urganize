## Talk to the team

Every rollout has its own chat. Open the rollout and tap **Talk to the team**.

- Type `@` and a name to tag someone. They get an email.
- Type `@everyone` to email the whole team.

Anyone who can see the rollout can read its chat.

## Plan content

Plan the rollout's social posts on a calendar. Tap **Plan content**.

1. Tap **New calendar**. Pick the **Platform** and add the account name.
2. Pick a day and tap **Add post**.
3. Add a **Title**, **Caption**, **Format** and **Status**. Paste the **Link to post** once it is live.

Release day is marked on the calendar. If you move the release date, your posts keep their dates, so check they still fit.

## Track spend

Log what you pay for, so you can see where the money went. Tap **Track spend**, then **Add spend**.

Each line has **What for**, **How much (₦)**, **Paid to**, **Date** and an optional task and note. Tap **Download CSV** to export.

Spend also shows on Home, under **Where the money went**.

<Note>
  Only the rollout owner and team managers can see spend.
</Note>
