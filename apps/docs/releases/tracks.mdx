description: "Upload the song and add cover art to a rollout."
---

## Upload the song

1. Open the rollout.
2. In the menu, tap **Upload the song**.
3. Tap **Choose file** and pick the master.

You can upload WAV, MP3 or FLAC, up to 40 MB. Only your team can hear it. Nothing here is public.

To add a new mix, tap **Upload another version**.

<Tip>
  You can also tap **Upload the song** in the AI chat. AI shows a button to pick the file.
</Tip>

## Add cover art

1. Open the rollout.
2. In the menu, tap **Add cover art**.
3. Tap **Choose file**.

Cover art must be a square JPEG or PNG, at least 3000 × 3000 pixels, up to 25 MB. That is what music stores ask for.

The cover shows on the rollout, the pitch kit and the press kit. To change it, tap **Replace**. Uploading a new image replaces the old one.
