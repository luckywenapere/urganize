title: "Fill in details"

---

**Fill in details** holds everything music stores need for the song. Open the rollout and tap **Fill in details** in the menu.

## Step 1: The song's details

Fill in what you know:

- **Genre** and **Subgenre**. Not sure? Tap **Predict from audio** after you upload the song.
- **Language**
- **Label name**
- **Featured artist**
- **ISRC**, **UPC** and **ISWC** codes
- **℗ line** and **© line**, like "℗ 2026 Some Label"
- Whether the song has explicit content

Tap **Save details**.

## Step 2: Collect everyone's credits

Make one link and send it to everyone who worked on the song. See [Collect credits](/credits/collect).

## Step 3: Their answers land here

Each person's answers show up here on their own. Tap **Download as spreadsheet** to get them all.

<Note>
  The distribution check reads these details. Fill them in before you order distribution. See [Distribute](/releases/distribute).
</Note>
