Urganize does not deliver music to stores itself. It checks that your rollout is ready, then hands you over to our distribution partner, **Therealmusic Distro**.

## Before you order

Open the rollout. The **Before you order distribution** checklist shows what is missing, like a genre or the cover art. Tap **Fix** next to an item to go straight to it.

Fixed items tick themselves off. You can tick a warning by hand once you have checked it.

## Order distribution

1. Open the rollout and tap **Distribute now**.
2. Tap **Sign up with Therealmusic Distro**.
3. Sign up and pay on their site. Read **Their terms** there.

You pay the partner directly. Distribution is not part of your Urganize bill.

If the checklist still has warnings, the button reads **Order anyway**. If something must be fixed first, you cannot order until it is.

<Tip>
  Order distribution early. Spotify editorial pitches need the song in Spotify's system weeks before release day. See [Pitch this!](/promote/pitch).
</Tip>

## Fan payments

Below the distributor sits **Fan payments**, from our partner **Fanlly**. Fans tip you through one link, by bank transfer or card, with no account. You keep 75% of every tip.

Tap **Sign up with Fanlly** to start. Fanlly does not put your music in stores, so you still need a distributor.
