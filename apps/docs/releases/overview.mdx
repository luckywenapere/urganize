A rollout is one release (a single, EP or album) and all the work around it. Every rollout has its own plan, team chat, song, cover art, credits and promo.

## Find your rollouts

Go to **Catalogue** > **Rollouts**. You see every rollout with its **Status**, **Release date** and **Progress**. Use the filters to show **Planning**, **In progress**, **Released** or **Archived** rollouts.

Tap a rollout to open it.

## Inside a rollout

The top shows the rollout's name, its artist, its release date and how many activities are done.

A new rollout also shows **Your first steps**: add the song, add the cover art and invite your team. Each step ticks itself off. Tap **Hide first steps** to hide them.

The rollout menu is grouped by job:

| Group | Menu items |
| - | - |
| **Plan** | **See the timeline**, **Work the plan**, **Talk to the team**, **Plan content**, **Track spend** |
| **Release** | **Fill in details**, **Distribute now**, **Add cover art**, **Upload the song** |
| **Promote** | **Pitch this!**, **Build your pitch kit**, **Find promo**, **Share to socials**, **Collect fan emails** |

On a computer the menu sits down the left side. On a phone, tap the menu button to open it as a sheet.

## Archive or delete a rollout

- **Archive rollout** hides a rollout you are done with. You can **Restore rollout** later.
- To delete a rollout for good, go to **Settings** > **Security & account** > **Delete rollouts**. This cannot be undone.
