title: "People"
description: "Keep your own list of the producers, DJs and editors you work with."
---

**People** is your own list of contacts. Only your team sees it. Go to **Relationships** > **People**.

## Add a person

1. Tap **Add person**.
2. Add their **Name**, **What they do**, **Email**, **Phone** and any **Tags**, like "Mixing, Lagos".
3. Save.

If they look like someone already on your list, Urganize tells you so you do not add them twice.

## Keep notes

Open a person to add **What they charge** and **Notes**. Tap **We spoke today** after a call. People you have not been in touch with for 60 days are marked, so you know who to check in with.

If the person is on Urganize, they show as **On Urganize**. Their own profile stays private. You only see what you wrote.

## Find someone

Search by name, role or tag, or filter by role and tag.
