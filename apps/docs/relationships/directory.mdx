---

The Directory lists music people who have worked on real releases: producers, engineers, directors, designers, managers and more. Go to **Relationships** > **Directory**.

## Search

Search by name, or filter by **Role**, **Profile type**, **City** and **Open to** (open to work, selective or not taking work).

Results are never ranked, and nobody can pay to be listed higher. When you have not searched, two short lists show at the top:

- **Featured**: picked by hand by Urganize each quarter.
- **On the rise this month**: people whose recent work helped a rollout come out.

## Ask someone about work

Tap **Ask … about work** on their card. Say what the job is and roughly when, then send. They see your name, the job and your note, and can say yes or no. A request with no answer in 7 days closes, and you can ask again.

From an activity, tap **Find someone** to see people who have done that kind of job.

## Invite your people

Bring your producers, engineers and directors in. At the top of the Directory, tap **Invite**:

1. Add their **Name** and **What they do**.
2. Add their email and tap **Send invite**, or tap **Get link** and share it on WhatsApp.

They get a profile only they can see until they publish it. You see who opened it and who joined. You can send up to 20 invites a day.

## Get listed yourself

1. Go to **Settings** > **Directory**.
2. Type your name and tap **List me**.
3. Add a photo, your profile type and what you are open to.

You are not listed until you say so, and you can close it again. Your public page lives at `app.urganize.app/p/your-name`. Work you delivered on Urganize rollouts fills in on its own.
