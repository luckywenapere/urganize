---
title: "What is Urganize?"
description: "Urganize is the operating system for independent music careers."
---

Urganize helps independent artists and managers run their music careers as businesses. It plans the work and keeps the team on it: releases, shows, pitching, press and distribution. You tell it your goals, it makes the plan, and every task gets one owner and one date.

The built-in AI assistant can plan a release for you, answer questions and make changes when you ask.

## Two words to know

- **Rollout**: one release (a single, EP or album) and all the work around it.
- **Activity**: one job inside a rollout, like "Shoot the cover art". Each activity has an owner and a date.
