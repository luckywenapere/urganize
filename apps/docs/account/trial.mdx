---
title: "Free trial"
description: "Try Urganize free for 3 days, with no card."
---

Every new team gets a free trial.

- It lasts **3 days**.
- You do not need a card.
- Your whole team gets the full app.
- You get **50 UrgAns credits** to try the assistant. Your first rollout plan is free on top of that.

You pick a plan when you sign up, but you are not charged. See how many days are left in **Settings** > **Billing**.

## Get more time

- **Share your rollout.** The first time your team shares a post from **Share to socials**, your trial gets 30 days longer. Once per team. See [Share to socials](/promote/share).
- **Invite a teammate.** If you start to cancel your trial, Urganize may offer 7 more days for inviting one teammate.

## When the trial ends

Urganize asks you to pick a plan and pay. Everything your team made is safe and waiting. See [Plans and prices](/account/subscription).

Leftover trial credits are removed unless you bought a plan or credits.

If you cancel your trial before it ends, your team becomes read-only when it ends. You can still see everything.
