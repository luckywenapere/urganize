---
title: "Settings"
description: "Where to change your details, team, notifications, billing and more."
---

Open your account menu (top right on a computer, **More** on a phone) and tap **Settings**. Settings opens over the page you are on. Use **Search settings** to jump to anything.

Some parts only show for people who can change them. For example, **Team** and **Billing** show for Managers.

| Part | What is in it |
| - | - |
| **General** | Your name, **Appearance** (**Light**, **Dark** or **System**), **Report an issue** and **Guided tours**. |
| **Notifications** | **Push notifications**, **After tasks** (the "How did it go?" check-in) and **Motivation**. |
| **Team** | **Teammates**, **Team logins** and **Invite another team**. See [Your team](/account/team). |
| **Artists & brand** | Team name and logo, your **Artists**, **Brand colors** and **Press kits**. |
| **UrgAns** | The **Team brief**. See [UrgAns](/urgans/overview). |
| **Directory** | **Your directory record**. See [Directory](/relationships/directory). |
| **Billing** | Your plan, UrgAns credits and cancelling. See [Plans and prices](/account/subscription). |
| **Connections & data** | **Connected apps** and **Your data** (**Download everything**, **Download activities**). |
| **Security & account** | **Password**, and the **Danger zone**. |

## Change your password

Go to **Security & account**. Type your **New password** twice and tap **Update password**.

## Report a problem

Go to **General** > **Report an issue** and tap **Report a bug** or **Make a request**. You can also use **Report an issue** in your account menu.

## Download your data

Go to **Connections & data** > **Your data**. **Download everything** gets all your data. **Download activities** gets a spreadsheet of your activities.

## Delete your account

Go to **Security & account** > **Danger zone** > **Delete account**. Type DELETE and tap **Delete account**.

<Warning>
  Deleting your account cannot be undone.
</Warning>
