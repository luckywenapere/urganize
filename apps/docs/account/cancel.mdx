---
title: "Cancel or pause"
description: "Cancel your plan or trial yourself, or pause instead."
---

Only the team owner can cancel or pause.

## Cancel

1. Go to **Settings** > **Billing**.
2. Tap **Cancel plan** (or **Cancel trial**).
3. Read the offers, or tap **Continue to cancel** on each screen.
4. Tell us why you are leaving, if you like.
5. Tap **Drop the mic** to cancel.

Your plan runs until the end of the time you paid for. After that, your team becomes read-only. Everything you made stays here for you to read.

## Pause instead

On a paid plan, you can pause for **2 weeks**, **1 month**, **2 months** or **3 months**. You pay nothing while it is paused, and your team is read-only. You can pause once every 6 months.

To come back early, go to **Settings** > **Billing** and tap **Resume now**. We remind you by email before a pause ends.

## Come back

After a plan has ended, go to **Settings** > **Billing** and tap **Update payment** to pick up where you left off.
