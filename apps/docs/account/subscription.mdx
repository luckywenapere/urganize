Prices are in naira. One price covers your whole team, not each person. Pay monthly, or once a year and get 2 months free.

| | Solo | Manager | Label | Group |
| - | - | - | - | - |
| **For** | An independent artist | A manager running two or more artists | Small labels and management teams | Distributors and multi-label groups |
| **Monthly** | ₦5,000 | ₦22,000 | ₦50,000 | Let's talk |
| **Yearly** | ₦50,000 | ₦220,000 | ₦500,000 | Let's talk |
| **Artists** | 1 | Up to 5 | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| **People on your team** | 1 | 4 | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| **Rollouts at a time** | 1 | 3 | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| **UrgAns credits each month** | 150 | 660 | 1,500 | Agreed with you |

Every plan includes distribution through our partner. You pay the partner for distribution directly. See [Distribute](/releases/distribute).

**Label** also includes help setting up your roster, and WhatsApp support answered the same day.

**Group** is priced with you, paid by invoice or bank transfer, and we set every label up with you. Email [contact@urganize.app](mailto:contact@urganize.app).

## Pay for a plan

You pay through Paystack, by card or bank. When your trial ends, Urganize shows **Pick your plan**:

1. Pick a plan.
2. Choose **Monthly** or **Yearly**.
3. Pay.

If you paid and the app has not caught up, tap **I have paid, check again**.

## Change your plan

Go to **Settings** > **Billing** and tap **Change plan**. Moving to a smaller plan starts at your next renewal.

## See your plan

**Settings** > **Billing** shows your plan, when it renews and your UrgAns credits. Only the team owner or a Manager can change it.

## When a plan ends

If a plan ends and is not renewed, your team becomes read-only. You can still see everything, and nothing is removed. Tap **Update payment** in **Settings** > **Billing** to start making changes again.

Want to stop or take a break? See [Cancel or pause](/account/cancel).
