One plan covers everyone on your team. Only Managers can invite people.

## Invite a teammate

1. Go to **Settings** > **Team**.
2. Under **Teammates**, type their email.
3. Pick a **Role**: **Manager**, **Artist**, **Team Member** or **Viewer**. For an Artist, also pick which artist they are.
4. Tap **Invite**.

They get an email to join. To share a link instead, tap **Copy invite link**.

See [Roles and permissions](/roles-and-permissions) for what each role can do.

## How many people

| Plan | People on your team |
| - | - |
| Solo | 1 (just you) |
| Manager | 4 |
| Label | Unlimited |

A pending invite takes a seat until it is accepted or removed.

## Team logins

Team logins let you make a login for someone without knowing their email or password.

1. Under **Team logins**, type who it is for, like "Tolu, publicist".
2. Tap **Generate login**.
3. Send the link on WhatsApp or by email.

They open the link, pick their own password, and they are in.

## Invite another team

Under **Invite another team**, copy your invite link and share it with a manager or artist who runs their own team. When they sign up and make their first payment, you get 100 AI credits. You can earn this up to 20 times.
