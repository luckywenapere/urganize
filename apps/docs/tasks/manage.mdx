title: "Work the plan"
description: "Add, assign, update and find activities."
---

Open a rollout and tap **Work the plan** to see its activities.

## The short list

A rollout opens on its key activities, the handful that matter most right now. Tap **Show all** to see the rest.

To keep an activity on the short list, open it and tap **Add to key activities**.

## Add an activity

1. Open the rollout.
2. Tap **New activity**. On a computer you can also press `C`.
3. Type a **Title**, pick a **Due date** and tap **Create activity**.

## Update an activity

Tap an activity to open it. Then:

- Change its **Status**, **Due date** or **Phase**.
- Assign it to a teammate. Not on the team yet? Choose **Invite someone new…** and type their email.
- Add **Notes**, or message the owner in **Chat**.
- Tap **Find someone** to look for a person in the Directory who has done this job before.

Tick the box next to the title to mark it done. Urganize may then ask "How did it go?". Answer in one tap or close it. You can turn this off in **Settings** > **Notifications** > **After tasks**.

To delete an activity, open it and tap **Delete activity**. This cannot be undone.

<Tip>
  You can also tell AI: "Mark the cover shoot as done" or "Move the pitch to in progress". Status changes like these use no credits.
</Tip>

## Find activities

- **Search this plan** finds activities by name.
- **Filters** narrows the list by **Status**, **Assignee** (**Me**, **Anyone** or **Unassigned**) or **Date**.
- **Group by** switches between **Phase** and **Status**.

## Export activities

Go to **Settings** > **Connections & data** and tap **Download activities**. You get a spreadsheet of every activity.
