---

An activity is one job in a rollout, like "Book the cover shoot" or "Pitch Spotify editorial". Your rollout's plan is a list of activities.

## Where activities come from

- **From the plan.** AI builds them when you plan a rollout. A few may be marked **Added by AI**. See [The plan](/releases/playbook).
- **From you.** Add your own at any time.
- **From other screens.** Adding a promo channel to your plan, for example, creates an activity for it.

## What an activity has

| Field | What it means |
| - | - |
| **Title** | What needs doing. |
| **Status** | Where it stands: **To Do**, **In Progress**, **In Review**, **Blocked**, **Done** and more. |
| **Due date** | When it should be done. |
| **Phase** | **Pre-release**, **Release** or **Post-release**. It follows the date unless you change it. |
| Owner | The teammate it is assigned to. |
| **Notes** | Context, links and details. |
| **Chat** | A conversation about this one activity. |

An activity marked **Locked** is waiting on an earlier one. It opens once that one is done.

## Progress

Each rollout shows how many of its activities are done, like "12 of 40 done". Cancelled activities do not count.
