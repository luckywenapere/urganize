---
title: "Fill in the credit form"
description: "For collaborators: how to fill in a credits link someone sent you."
---

Someone on a release team sent you a link to add your credits to a song. It takes a few minutes. You do not need an account.

## Have this ready

- Your legal name, as it is on your ID.
- What you did on the song.
- Your writing or recording share, if you have one.
- Your royalty society and its ID number, if you have signed up with one.

## Fill it in

1. **What did you do on this song?** Tick everything that applies.
2. **Legal name**: your real name, as on your ID. This goes on the paperwork.
3. **Stage / professional name**: the name you are known by, if you have one.
4. **Tell them what you did**: a sentence is plenty.
5. **Email**: so the team can reach you.
6. **Phone** and **Country**: phone is optional.

## Shares and publishing

These parts show only when they apply to you.

- **If you helped write the song:** add **Your writing share (%)**, your **Royalty society (PRO)**, **Your society ID (IPI / CAE)**, **Publisher name** and **Publisher ID (IPI)**. Write SELF as the publisher if that is you. Not sure of your exact share? Give your best number. The team can sort it out with you.
- **If you produced it:** add **Your recording share (%)**, also called the master share.

## About you (optional)

Add your **Instagram handle**, **X handle** and a **Short bio** if you like. The team may use them in a press kit or when they tag you.

## Send it

Tick **Everything I entered above is true and correct.** and tap **Send my credits**.

Sent it by mistake, or something changed? Send the form again with the same email. Your new answer replaces the old one.
