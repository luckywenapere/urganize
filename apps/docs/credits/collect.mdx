## Make the link

1. Open the rollout and tap **Fill in details**.
2. Go to **Step 2 · Collect everyone's credits**.
3. Tap **Create the link**.
4. Tap **Copy link** and send it to everyone who worked on the song, in one group chat or one by one.

One link works for everyone. Tap **Open form** to see what they see.

## See the answers

Answers show up under **Step 3 · Their answers land here** on their own. Tap **Download as spreadsheet** to get them all.

If writing or recording shares do not add up to 100%, Urganize points it out so you can sort it with the team.

If someone sends the form again with the same email, their new answer replaces the old one.

## Close or replace the link

- **Close link** stops new answers. The link still opens, and you can **Reopen link** later.
- **Regenerate** makes a new link. The old one stops working right away.

## Claim pages

When a rollout is out, each credited person gets a page of their own. Urganize emails them a link to claim it, and one reminder a week later if they have not.

You see this in **Fill in details**, under **Claim pages**. To send a link yourself, tap **Email** (it shows their first name), **Copy link** or **Send on WhatsApp** next to them.

<Tip>
  Send the credits link early, as soon as the song is finished. The details are freshest then.
</Tip>
