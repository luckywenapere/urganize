Credits say who did what on a song: who wrote it, produced it, sang on it and mixed it. Music stores and royalty societies need them, and they are easy to lose.

Urganize collects them with one link. Each collaborator fills in their own details. They do not need an Urganize account.

## How it works

1. You make one credits link for the rollout.
2. You send it to everyone who worked on the song.
3. Each person fills in a short form: what they did, their legal name, their shares and their publishing details.
4. Their answers land in the rollout. Download them as a spreadsheet any time.
5. When the rollout is out, each credited person gets their own page they can claim.

<Note>
  These are contributor credits. They are not the same as [UrgAns credits](/urgans/credits), which pay for the assistant.
</Note>
