---
title: "Claim your page"
description: "For collaborators: claim the page Urganize made from your credits."
---

When a song you worked on comes out, Urganize makes you a simple page from your credits. It shows your name, your role and the songs you are credited on. Until you claim it, it is marked **Unclaimed**.

## Claim it

1. Open the claim link from your email, or the one the team sent you.
2. Tap **Sign up to claim**, or **Log in** if you already have an account.
3. Confirm your email or phone.
4. Check your credits, add a bio and say if you are open to work.
5. Choose whether to show your page, then tap **See your page**.

Your page lives at a link like `app.urganize.app/p/your-name`. Share it with **Copy link** or **Send on WhatsApp** to get more work.

## Not you?

Tap **Not mine** on the claim screen. If a page about you should not exist, open it and use **Remove this profile**. It is hidden straight away.

## After you claim it

Your page is also your Directory profile. Change it any time in **Settings** > **Directory**. See [Directory](/relationships/directory).
