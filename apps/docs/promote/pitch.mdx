Every rollout has its own pitching page. Open the rollout and tap **Pitch this!**.

## Pitch a curator

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a platform">
    Tap **All**, **Spotify**, **Apple Music**, **Audiomack** or **YouTube**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a curator">
    Tap a curator's picture under **Pick a curator**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your song">
    Paste a link to the song. A private link from untitled, SoundCloud or similar is fine. Add a short note if you like.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it">
    Tap **Send**. Some curators are free. For a curator who charges, the button reads **Review and pay**, and you see the fee before you pay.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Paid pitches

You pay by card or bank through Paystack. You are paying for a listen and a written reply, not a playlist spot. No curator can promise one.

If the curator does not reply within 7 days, you get your money back. Your receipts are under **Payments**.

## Answers

Curators' replies show under **Answers**, like messages, with the curator's picture.

## Editorial pitches

Spotify and Apple Music pick songs for their own playlists. You pitch them yourself in Spotify for Artists and Apple Music for Artists, once your distributor has delivered the song. Urganize never sends the pitch for you.

When UrgAns builds your plan, it adds a **Pitch to Spotify editorial** activity. It comes after you order distribution and is due about four weeks before release day. Spotify needs the pitch at least 7 days before release day, and earlier gives you a better shot.

1. Open the activity.
2. Tap **Draft my pitch** to get a first draft of each field.
3. Tap **Open Spotify for Artists** (or **Open Apple Music for Artists**) and paste each field there.

<Tip>
  A pitch kit makes curators more likely to say yes. See [Pitch kit](/promote/pitch-kit).
</Tip>
