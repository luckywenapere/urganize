title: "Press kits"

A press kit is one page about an artist that you send to writers, bookers and curators. Each artist has one, and every rollout can use it.

## Open a press kit

- Go to **Catalogue** > **Artists**, open the artist and tap **Open the press kit** (or **Set up the press kit**).
- Or go to **Settings** > **Artists & brand** > **Press kits**.

## Fill it in

Start with the four must-haves under **Start here**: About, Bio, Photos and Contact. The header shows how many are done. Everything else sits under **Extras**. Tap **Next** to walk through.

- **Bio**: write a short one first. Tap **Add longer versions** for medium and long ones.
- **Photos**: tap **Add photos**. A kit without a photo gets skipped, so add one early.
- **Contact**: who writers should email.

Some parts fill in from your rollouts, credits and shows. When a rollout has something new, a **New from your rollouts** banner asks before anything changes.

AI can draft a section for you. Nothing changes until you tap **Use this**.

## Share it

1. Turn the link on. You get one link to send everywhere.
2. Tap **Copy link**.

Under **More options** you can choose **Who can find it**: **Only people I send it to**, or **Everyone, even on Google**. You can also ask writers to wait until a date, and say if the artist is open to interviews.

To stop sharing, use **Turn off the link**. It stops working until you share it again.
