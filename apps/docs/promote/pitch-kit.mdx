title: "Pitch kit"
description: "Everything a playlist or a blog needs to say yes to this song, in three steps."
---

A pitch kit tells playlists, blogs and radio why they should play the song. Open the rollout and tap **Build your pitch kit**.

## Three steps

1. **Tell the story.** How did this song happen? Why should people listen? A few sentences is plenty.
2. **Who made it.** Add who wrote it, made the beat and sang on it. Tap **Add a person**, or **Use what my team sent** to fill it in from the credits form.
3. **Share it.** Tap **Make the press kit**. Once the artist's press kit link is live, tap **Copy link** and send it.

Each step turns into a check when it is done. Your words save on their own when you click away.

## Let AI write it

Tap **Write it for me** to fill in what is missing. Tap **Give it a hint** first to steer it, like "we made it in one night in Lagos".

It only fills empty spots. Nothing you wrote is changed. It uses up to 25 AI credits, and the screen tells you before it runs.

<Note>
  Once a rollout is out, its pitch kit stays as it was on release day.
</Note>
