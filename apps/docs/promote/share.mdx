---
title: "Share to socials"
description: "Turn your rollout into posts for Instagram, WhatsApp, X and Snapchat."
---

Open the rollout and tap **Share to socials**.

1. Answer four quick questions.
2. Tap **Make my posts**.
3. Pick a post for **Instagram**, **WhatsApp** status, **X** or **Snapchat**, or a message you can send to anyone.
4. Share, copy or save it.

Urganize never posts for you. You choose what goes out and where.

## Get 1 month free

The first time your team shares, copies or saves a post from this sheet, we add 30 free days to your team.

- One free month per team, ever.
- The team owner or an admin gets it.
- On a trial, your trial gets 30 days longer. On a paid plan, you get 30 days after your paid time ends. You are still billed on your normal day.
