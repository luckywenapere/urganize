**Find promo** lists places that can push your song without a big budget: radio in Lagos, Abuja and the UK, music blogs, X communities and SoundCloud networks.

Open the rollout and tap **Find promo**.

## Find a channel

- **Good fits** come first, picked from your song's genre and country.
- Under **All channels**, filter by place (**All**, **Nigeria**, **UK**, **Online**) and by type.
- Each channel is marked **Free** or **Paid** when we know.

Tap a channel to see how to submit, who to contact, the lead time and the cost.

## Add it to your plan

Tap **Add to plan**. It becomes an activity, dated ahead of release day by the channel's lead time. A channel already on your plan shows a check.

## Suggest a channel

Know a station, blog or community we are missing? Tap **Suggest a channel**. We check every suggestion before it is listed.
