A capture page gives fans something, like an early listen, in return for their email. Share it from a bio, a story or a DM.

## Make the page

1. Open the rollout and tap **Collect fan emails**.
2. Tap **Create capture page**.
3. Fill in the **Headline**, **What they get**, the **Button** text and **The link they get**.
4. Turn on **Also ask for a name** if you want names too.

Your changes save as you type. The checklist beside the editor shows what is missing. When it says **Ready to send**, tap **Copy link** and share it.

## Your sign-ups

Emails land under the page as fans sign up. Tap **Copy as CSV** and paste them into a spreadsheet or your email tool.

- **Pause sign-ups** stops new sign-ups for now.
- **New link** makes a new link. The old one stops working, and your sign-ups stay.

<Note>
  Pre-save campaigns are no longer part of Urganize. Use a capture page to collect fans before release day.
</Note>
