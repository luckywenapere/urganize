Go to **Catalogue** > **Artists** to see every artist on your team, with their rollouts and shows coming up.

## Add an artist

1. Go to **Settings** > **Artists & brand**.
2. Under **Artists**, type a name in **Add an artist**.
3. Tap **Add**.

How many artists you can have depends on your plan. See [Plans and prices](/account/subscription).

## The artist page

Tap an artist to open their page. It shows:

- Their photo. Tap it to add or change it. You can drag and zoom to frame it.
- **Rollouts**: every rollout for this artist.
- **Shows**: shows coming up and past. Tap **Add a show** to book one.
- **Press kit**: tap **Open the press kit** or **Set up the press kit**. See [Press kits](/promote/press-kits).

## Brand colors

In **Settings** > **Artists & brand** > **Brand colors**, set each artist's **Background** and **Accent** colors. Urganize uses them on the posts it makes for that artist.
