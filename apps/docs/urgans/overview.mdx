UrgAns is Urganize's built-in assistant. Ask it anything about your rollouts, or tell it what to do. It plans, drafts and updates for you.

## Where to find it

- **On a computer:** the chat on Home, or the **UrgAns** tab in the top menu. Press `/` to start typing.
- **On a phone:** the **UrgAns** tab in the bottom bar.
- **Anywhere:** type a question into **Search or ask UrgAns** at the top.

## What you can ask

Tap a suggestion or type your own:

- **What should I focus on today?** What's due, what's late and what's blocked.
- **Plan a rollout.** Answer a few questions and get your plan.
- **Write an update I can send.** A paragraph on where a rollout stands.
- **Add activity**, or "Move the cover shoot to Friday".
- **Upload the song.**

Before UrgAns changes or deletes something that matters, it shows you what it will do and waits for your yes.

## Pick a rollout

Under the message box, tap **All rollouts. Choose one for this conversation.** and pick a rollout. Then "add an activity" lands on that rollout without you naming it.

## More ways to talk to it

- **Attach a photo or file** to ask about it.
- **Dictate** turns your voice into text in the message box.
- **Talk to UrgAns** starts a voice call. UrgAns answers out loud and can make plans, add activities and move dates after you confirm. Calls use 2 credits a minute.

## Chats and sessions

On Home, **Chat** is one ongoing conversation. Switch to **Sessions** to start a new chat or open an old one.

## Teach it about your team

Go to **Settings** > **UrgAns** > **Team brief**. Tell UrgAns about your **Roster**, **How we work**, **Positioning**, **Constraints** and **Vocabulary** once, and it stops asking. Tap **Walk me through it** for help.

## Use Urganize from Claude or ChatGPT

In Claude or ChatGPT, add Urganize as a connector. It brings you to Urganize to say yes. Connected apps can see and change your rollouts, activities and notes, but cannot delete anything. Disconnect one any time in **Settings** > **Connections & data**.

UrgAns runs on credits. See [UrgAns credits](/urgans/credits).
