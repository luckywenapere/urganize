---
title: "AI credits"
description: "How AI credits work, what things cost and how to get more."
---

AI runs on credits. Your team shares one balance. Credits never expire.

## What uses credits

Each request to AI uses some credits. Bigger jobs use more. The chat shows your balance as you go, and a button that uses credits tells you the cost first.

| Action | Credits |
| - | - |
| Your team's first rollout plan | Free |
| A rollout plan after that | About 12 |
| **Write it for me** in a pitch kit | Up to 25 |
| A voice call with AI | 2 a minute |
| Changing an activity's status, like "mark it done" | Free |

## Where credits come from

| You are on | You get |
| - | - |
| The free trial | 50 credits, once |
| Solo | 150 credits every month |
| Manager | 660 credits every month |
| Label | 1,500 credits every month |

Unused monthly credits roll over. If your trial ends and you have not bought a plan or credits, the trial credits left over are removed.

## Buy more

Go to your account menu and tap your credits row, or go to **Settings** > **Billing** > **See your AI credit history**. Under **Buy credits**, pick a pack and pay once, by card or bank.

| Price | Credits without a plan | Credits on a plan |
| - | - | - |
| ₦1,500 | 50 | 100 |
| ₦3,000 | 100 | 200 |
| ₦7,500 | 250 | 500 |
| ₦15,000 | 500 | 1,000 |

Subscribers get twice the credits for the same price.

## Earn credits

Share your invite link with another team in **Settings** > **Team** > **Invite another team**. When they sign up and make their first payment, you get 100 credits. You can earn this up to 20 times.

## Running low

Urganize warns you when you have fewer than 50 credits left. At zero, AI says "You are out of AI credits." Quick commands still work.

## No plan? Use credits only

You can keep using Urganize without a plan, as long as you have credits. Your team gets Solo features while it has credits.

Your credit history shows every credit added and spent.
