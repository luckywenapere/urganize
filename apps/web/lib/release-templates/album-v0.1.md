Default duration: 21 weeks (17 weeks pre-release + 4 weeks post-release follow-up)
Trigger to start: All masters are finalized, or finalization is within 3 weeks
Default roles: Artist, Manager, Marketing Lead, Designer, Distributor Contact

## What makes this different from the EP template

The album template extends the EP shape with **two staggered singles instead of one**,
and a longer tail. That is the whole difference, and it drives every offset here:

- **Single 1 drops at offset −84** (12 weeks before the album). Its job is to open the
  campaign and start building editorial relationships from nothing.
- **Single 2 drops at offset −42** (6 weeks before the album), alongside the album
  announcement and tracklist reveal. Its job is to convert the audience single 1 found
  into pre-saves for the full project.
- **The album lands at 0**, pitched to editorial on the combined evidence of both singles.
- **The tail runs four weeks, not three** (+1, +8, +15, +22, +29). An album has more
  tracks to give their own moment, and the long tail is where album streams actually
  accumulate.

The 17-week runway is not padding: two full single rollouts have to fit inside it before
the album's own 5-week editorial window even opens.

## Week 17 — Album Strategy & Narrative (Days 1–7) [offset: -119]

- Lock the tracklist and running order — Artist
- Lock all masters for every track on the album — Artist
- Define the album narrative: what the record is about and how it is sold — Manager + Artist
- Select the singles and their order (single 1, single 2, optional third) — Manager + Artist
- Confirm the album release date and lock it — Manager
- Map the full campaign timeline backwards from release day — Manager
- Choose distributor and confirm the account handles a staggered album campaign — Manager
- Set the rollout budget across both singles and the album — Manager
- Brief the marketing lead on the album — Manager

## Week 16 — Paperwork & Rights (Days 8–14) [offset: -112]

- Write metadata for every track — Manager
- Draft split sheets for every track and circulate to all contributors — Manager
- Confirm any sample clearances and features are documented — Manager
- Brief the designer on album cover art — Manager
- Plan the album content shoot (coverage for the whole record and both singles) — Manager
- [Conditional] If physical formats are part of the release: begin production now (8–12 weeks) — Manager

## Week 15 — Single 1 Delivery (Days 15–21) [offset: -105]

- Upload single 1 to the distributor — Manager
- Brief the designer on single 1 artwork — Manager
- Identify priority playlists and curators for single 1 — Marketing Lead
- Finalize split sheet signatures for every track — Manager
- Album content shoot executed — Artist

## Week 14 — Single 1 Editorial Push (Days 22–28) [offset: -98]

- Confirm the distributor accepted single 1 with no metadata errors — Manager
- Single 1 artwork final approved — Artist
- Submit the single 1 editorial pitch — Marketing Lead
- Begin curator outreach for single 1 with a private link — Marketing Lead
- Begin trimming shoot footage into promo edits, tagged by track — Marketing Lead
- Album cover art first draft delivered — Designer

## Week 13 — Single 1 Ramp (Days 29–35) [offset: -91]

- Set up the single 1 pre-save link — Manager
- Announce single 1 release date on socials — Artist
- Single 1 teaser content begins — Marketing Lead
- Follow up on single 1 curator outreach — Marketing Lead
- Set up Spotify for Artists / Apple Music for Artists access for analytics — Manager
- Album cover art final approved — Artist

## Week 12 — SINGLE 1 DROP (Days 36–42) [offset: -84]

- **Single 1 drops: post across all platforms — Artist [event milestone]**
- **Monitor the distributor for single 1 go-live across all DSPs — Manager [event milestone]**
- Compile single 1's first-48-hours numbers — Manager
- Thank-you content and early stats to the team — Artist
- Engage with fan posts and tags from the single 1 drop — Artist

## Week 11 — Single 1 Momentum (Days 43–49) [offset: -77]

- Review single 1 performance: markets, content, playlists — Manager
- Confirm single 1 playlist placements that came back positive — Marketing Lead
- Post follow-up content for single 1 — Marketing Lead
- [Conditional] Brief PR contacts on the album campaign — Manager

## Week 10 — Single 2 Delivery (Days 50–56) [offset: -70]

- Upload single 2 to the distributor — Manager
- Brief the designer on single 2 artwork — Manager
- Identify priority playlists and curators for single 2 — Marketing Lead
- [Conditional] Adjust the album plan based on what single 1 proved — Manager

## Week 9 — Single 2 Editorial Push (Days 57–63) [offset: -63]

- Confirm the distributor accepted single 2 with no metadata errors — Manager
- Single 2 artwork final approved — Artist
- Submit the single 2 editorial pitch with single 1's numbers as evidence — Marketing Lead
- Begin curator outreach for single 2 with a private link — Marketing Lead

## Week 8 — Single 2 Ramp (Days 64–70) [offset: -56]

- Set up the single 2 pre-save link — Manager
- Announce single 2 release date on socials — Artist
- Single 2 teaser content begins — Marketing Lead
- Follow up on single 2 curator outreach — Marketing Lead
- Prepare the album announcement assets — Marketing Lead

## Week 7 — Album Delivery (Days 71–77) [offset: -49]

- Upload the album to the distributor — Manager
- Upload album cover art to the distributor — Manager
- Confirm the album release date is locked at the distributor — Manager
- Schedule single 2 release-day posts — Marketing Lead

## Week 6 — SINGLE 2 DROP & ALBUM ANNOUNCE (Days 78–84) [offset: -42]

- **Single 2 drops: post across all platforms — Artist [event milestone]**
- **Monitor the distributor for single 2 go-live across all DSPs — Manager [event milestone]**
- Announce the album with the tracklist reveal — Artist
- Compile single 2's first-48-hours numbers — Manager
- Engage with fan posts and tags from the single 2 drop — Artist

## Week 5 — Album Editorial & Pre-save (Days 85–91) [offset: -35]

- Confirm the distributor accepted the album with no metadata errors — Manager
- Set up the album pre-save link — Manager
- [Conditional] Album pre-save campaign goes live — Manager
- Identify priority playlists and curators for the album — Marketing Lead
- Plan the post-release focus tracks — Manager

## Week 4 — Album Curator & Press Push (Days 92–98) [offset: -28]

- Begin album curator outreach with a private link — Marketing Lead
- [Conditional] Send the album press release to contacts — Manager
- [Conditional] Pitch long-form press: interviews, features, album reviews — Manager
- Confirm any release-day events — Manager
- Pitch to Spotify editorial, with both singles' numbers as evidence (after the album upload at -49; Spotify needs it at least 7 days before release day) — Manager

## Week 3 — Marketing Ramp (Days 99–105) [offset: -21]

- Album teaser content rollout begins — Marketing Lead
- [Conditional] Track-by-track teaser plan executed — Marketing Lead
- Follow up on album curator outreach — Marketing Lead
- Schedule album release-day social posts — Marketing Lead
- [Conditional] Tease the third single or focus track — Marketing Lead

## Week 2 — Final Prep (Days 106–112) [offset: -14]

- [Conditional] Listening party or album playback event planned — Manager [event milestone]
- Increase teaser content frequency — Marketing Lead
- Prepare release-day stories and posts (queued in scheduler) — Marketing Lead
- Confirm album playlist placements that came back positive — Marketing Lead
- Brief the team on release-day roles — Manager

## Week 1 — Release Week (Days 113–119) [offset: -7 / -3 / -1]

- 7 days out: countdown content begins — Artist [offset: -7]
- 3 days out: final push, ads start (if budget allows) — Marketing Lead [offset: -3]
- 1 day out: pre-save reminder to mailing list and fans — Manager [offset: -1]

## Release Day [offset: 0]

- **Release day: post the album drop announcement across all platforms — Artist [event milestone]**
- **Release day: monitor the distributor for go-live across all DSPs — Manager [event milestone]**
- **Release day: thank-you content, share early stats with the team — Artist [event milestone]**

## Week 0 — Release Week Wrap-Up [offset: 1]

- Compile first-week stats: streams, saves, playlist adds, top markets, per-track split — Manager
- Identify the standout tracks by streams and saves — Manager
- Debrief with the team: what worked across all three drops — Manager
- Identify the top-performing content from the rollout — Marketing Lead
- Thank-you posts to playlist curators, press, and collaborators — Artist
- Update the master release tracker — Manager

## Week +1 — First Follow-Up Push [offset: 8]

- Post follow-up content — Marketing Lead
- Push the standout track as the next focus track — Marketing Lead
- Pitch secondary editorial playlists with first-week stats as evidence — Marketing Lead
- Engage with fan posts and tags from release week — Artist
- Check for blog and press pickup and amplify anything that landed — Marketing Lead

## Week +2 — Sustained Momentum [offset: 15]

- Post follow-up content — Marketing Lead
- Pitch mood-based and genre-based playlists for the strongest tracks — Marketing Lead
- Review analytics: where listeners came from and which tracks hold them — Manager
- [Conditional] Review ads performance: spend efficiency, top creative, cost per save — Marketing Lead
- Identify the strongest market and consider geo-targeted content — Marketing Lead

## Week +3 — Deep Catalogue Push [offset: 22]

- Post follow-up content — Marketing Lead
- [Conditional] Release the third single or a focus-track video from the album — Marketing Lead
- Pitch the album to long-tail and catalogue playlists — Marketing Lead
- Review which tracks are being added to listener playlists — Manager

## Week +4 — Long Tail Setup [offset: 29]

- Post follow-up content — Marketing Lead
- Decide the next cycle: deluxe edition, remix, tour, or next project — Manager
- Document lessons learned in the master release tracker — Manager
- [Conditional] Submit to year-end / quarterly editorial and award considerations — Marketing Lead
