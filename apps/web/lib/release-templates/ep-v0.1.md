Default duration: 16 weeks (13 weeks pre-release + 3 weeks post-release follow-up)
Trigger to start: All masters are finalized, or finalization is within 2 weeks
Default roles: Artist, Manager, Marketing Lead, Designer, Distributor Contact

## What makes this different from the single template

Two things, and they are the whole reason an EP needs its own playbook:

1. **Multi-track logic.** Metadata, split sheets, and the content shoot cover *every*
   track, not one. They stay single tasks (one "write metadata for every track" rather
   than one task per track) — the tracklist is not known at template-authoring time, and
   the AI tailoring layer has no operation for adding tasks, so per-track rows would be
   either fabricated or missing.
2. **A staggered lead single.** The lead single gets its own compressed six-week rollout
   *inside* the EP runway, dropping 6 weeks before the EP (offset −42). Its traction is
   then the evidence used to pitch the EP to editorial. This is why the runway is 13 weeks
   rather than the single's 8: the front half of the calendar belongs to the single.

## Week 13 — EP Strategy & Tracklist (Days 1–7) [offset: -91]

- Lock the tracklist and running order — Artist
- Lock all masters for every track on the EP — Artist
- Pick the lead single — Manager + Artist
- Confirm the EP release date and lock it — Manager
- Choose distributor and confirm the account is active — Manager
- Set the rollout budget across both the lead single and the EP — Manager
- Brief the marketing lead on the EP: themes, story, target audience, sequencing logic — Manager

## Week 12 — Paperwork & Lead Single Delivery (Days 8–14) [offset: -84]

- Write metadata for every track — Manager
- Draft split sheets for every track and circulate to all contributors — Manager
- Upload the lead single to the distributor — Manager
- Brief the designer on lead single artwork — Manager
- Identify priority playlists and curators for the lead single — Marketing Lead
- Plan the EP content shoot (coverage for every track, not just the single) — Manager
- [Conditional] If physical formats are part of the release: begin production now (8–12 weeks) — Manager

## Week 11 — Lead Single Editorial Push (Days 15–21) [offset: -77]

- Confirm the distributor accepted the lead single with no metadata errors — Manager
- Lead single artwork final approved — Artist
- Submit the lead single editorial pitch — Marketing Lead
- Brief the designer on EP cover art — Manager
- Begin curator outreach for the lead single with a private link — Marketing Lead
- Finalize split sheet signatures for every track — Manager

## Week 10 — Content Production & Pre-save (Days 22–28) [offset: -70]

- EP content shoot executed — Artist
- Set up the lead single pre-save link — Manager
- [Conditional] Lead single pre-save campaign goes live — Manager
- EP cover art first draft delivered — Designer
- Announce the lead single release date on socials — Artist
- Begin trimming shoot footage into promo edits — Marketing Lead

## Week 9 — Lead Single Ramp (Days 29–35) [offset: -63]

- Lead single teaser content begins — Marketing Lead
- Follow up on lead single curator outreach — Marketing Lead
- EP cover art final approved — Artist
- Snippet of the lead single on TikTok/Reels — Artist
- [Conditional] Brief PR contacts on the EP campaign — Manager

## Week 8 — Lead Single Release Prep (Days 36–42) [offset: -56]

- Schedule lead single release-day posts — Marketing Lead
- Confirm lead single playlist placements that came back positive — Marketing Lead
- [Conditional] Pre-save reminder to the mailing list — Manager
- [Conditional] Final push on lead single ads — Marketing Lead
- Confirm the EP upload window with the distributor — Manager

## Week 7 — Lead Single Countdown (Days 43–49) [offset: -49]

- Countdown content for the lead single — Artist
- Prepare lead single release-day stories and posts — Marketing Lead
- Set up Spotify for Artists / Apple Music for Artists access for release-day analytics — Manager

## Week 6 — LEAD SINGLE DROP (Days 50–56) [offset: -42]

- **Lead single drops: post across all platforms — Artist [event milestone]**
- **Monitor the distributor for lead single go-live across all DSPs — Manager [event milestone]**
- Thank-you content and early stats shared with the team — Artist
- Compile the lead single's first-48-hours numbers — Manager
- Engage with fan posts and tags from the single drop — Artist

## Week 5 — EP Delivery & Announcement (Days 57–63) [offset: -35]

- Upload the EP to the distributor — Manager
- Upload EP cover art to the distributor — Manager
- Announce the EP with the tracklist reveal — Artist
- Pitch the EP to editorial with lead single traction as evidence — Marketing Lead
- Review lead single performance and adjust the EP plan — Manager
- Identify priority playlists and curators for the EP — Marketing Lead
- Confirm the EP release date is locked at the distributor — Manager

## Week 4 — EP Pre-save & Editorial (Days 64–70) [offset: -28]

- Confirm the distributor accepted the EP with no metadata errors — Manager
- Set up the EP pre-save link — Manager
- [Conditional] EP pre-save campaign goes live — Manager
- Begin EP curator outreach with a private link — Marketing Lead
- [Conditional] Send the EP press release to contacts — Manager
- Plan a second focus track to push after release — Manager

## Week 3 — Marketing Ramp (Days 71–77) [offset: -21]

- EP teaser content rollout begins — Marketing Lead
- [Conditional] Track-by-track teaser plan executed — Marketing Lead
- Follow up on EP curator outreach — Marketing Lead
- Confirm any release-day events — Manager
- Schedule EP release-day social posts — Marketing Lead

## Week 2 — Final Prep (Days 78–84) [offset: -14]

- [Conditional] Listening party or IG live planned — Manager [event milestone]
- Increase teaser content frequency — Marketing Lead
- Prepare release-day stories and posts (queued in scheduler) — Marketing Lead
- Confirm EP playlist placements that came back positive — Marketing Lead

## Week 1 — Release Week (Days 85–91) [offset: -7 / -3 / -1]

- 7 days out: countdown content begins — Artist [offset: -7]
- 3 days out: final push, ads start (if budget allows) — Marketing Lead [offset: -3]
- 1 day out: pre-save reminder to mailing list and fans — Manager [offset: -1]

## Release Day [offset: 0]

- **Release day: post the EP drop announcement across all platforms — Artist [event milestone]**
- **Release day: monitor the distributor for go-live across all DSPs — Manager [event milestone]**
- **Release day: thank-you content, share early stats with the team — Artist [event milestone]**

## Week 0 — Release Week Wrap-Up [offset: 1]

- Compile first-week stats: streams, saves, playlist adds, top markets, per-track split — Manager
- Identify the standout track by streams and saves — Manager
- Debrief with the team: what worked, what didn't — Manager
- Identify the top-performing content from the rollout — Marketing Lead
- Thank-you posts to playlist curators, press, and collaborators — Artist
- Update the master release tracker — Manager

## Week +1 — First Follow-Up Push [offset: 8]

- Post follow-up content (lyric videos, BTS, alternate edits, fan reposts) — Marketing Lead
- Push the standout track as the second focus track — Marketing Lead
- Pitch secondary editorial playlists with first-week stats as evidence — Marketing Lead
- Engage with fan posts and tags from release week — Artist

## Week +2 — Sustained Momentum [offset: 15]

- Post follow-up content (live performance, acoustic versions, story behind the tracks) — Marketing Lead
- Pitch mood-based and genre-based playlists for the strongest tracks — Marketing Lead
- Review analytics: where listeners are coming from and which tracks hold them — Manager
- [Conditional] Review ads performance: spend efficiency, top creative, cost per save — Marketing Lead

## Week +3 — Long Tail Setup [offset: 22]

- Post follow-up content (milestone celebrations, listener stats, artist commentary) — Marketing Lead
- Decide the next cycle: next single, deluxe edition, or next project — Manager
- Document lessons learned in the master release tracker — Manager
- [Conditional] Submit to year-end / quarterly editorial considerations — Marketing Lead
