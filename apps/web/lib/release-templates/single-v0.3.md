# Single Release Rollout Template — v0.3

Default duration: 11 weeks (8 weeks pre-release + 3 weeks post-release follow-up)
Trigger to start: Master is finalized, or finalization is within 1 week
Default roles: Artist, Manager, Marketing Lead, Designer, Distributor Contact

## Week 8 — Pre-Production & Strategy (Days 1–7) [offset: -56]

- Lock the master file (final mix, final mastered version) — Artist
- Confirm release date and lock it — Manager
- Choose distributor (DistroKid, TuneCore, EMPIRE, Believe, etc.) and confirm account is active — Manager
- Brief the marketing lead on the song: theme, story, target audience, mood — Artist + Manager
- Set rollout budget (ads, design, PR, content) — Manager
- Identify 3 priority playlists/curators to pitch — Marketing Lead

## Week 7 — Asset Planning (Days 8–14) [offset: -49]

- Brief designer on cover art (concept, references, dimensions) — Manager
- Plan content shoot day (BTS, performance clips, promo) — Manager
- Write song metadata: title, ISRC if pre-assigned, songwriter splits, producer credits, language — Artist + Manager
- Draft split sheet and circulate to all contributors — Manager
- Decide whether to do a pre-save campaign — Manager
- [Conditional] If physical formats (vinyl/CD/cassette) are part of release: begin physical production now (manufacturing alone is 8–12 weeks) — Manager

## Week 6 — Asset Production & Distributor Upload (Days 15–21) [offset: -42]

- Cover art first draft delivered — Designer
- Content shoot executed (promo videos, photos, social clips) — Artist + team
- Finalize split sheet signatures — Manager
- Upload track and metadata to distributor — Manager
- Upload cover art to distributor — Manager
- Confirm release date locked at distributor — Manager
- Submit Spotify for Artists pitch (Release Radar & Discover Weekly consideration) with private link — Marketing Lead
- Set up pre-save link (distributor has now accepted) — Manager
- Begin curator/playlist outreach with private link — Marketing Lead

## Week 5 — Distribution Confirmation & Editorial Push (Days 22–28) [offset: -35; -33 for +2 day buffer task]

- +2 day buffer: confirm distributor accepted submission with no metadata errors. If rejected, resubmit immediately — Manager [offset: -33]
- Cover art final approved — Artist + Manager
- Editorial playlist pitch with final assets (4+ weeks pre-release is the editorial window) — Marketing Lead
- Pre-save campaign goes live — Manager
- Begin trimming content shoot footage into 3–5 promo edits — Marketing Lead

## Week 4 — Marketing Ramp (Days 29–35) [offset: -28]

- Announce release date on artist's socials — Artist
- Begin teaser content rollout (1 piece every 2–3 days) — Marketing Lead
- Brief any PR contacts on release — Manager
- Follow up on curator outreach — Marketing Lead

## Week 3 — Pre-Release Push (Days 36–42) [offset: -21]

- Increase teaser content frequency — Marketing Lead
- Schedule release-day social posts — Marketing Lead
- Confirm any release-day events (listening session, IG live, etc.) — Manager
- Send press release / blurb to any contacts — Manager
- Confirm playlist placements that came back positive — Marketing Lead

## Week 2 — Final Prep (Days 43–49) [offset: -14]

- Listening party or IG live planned (if doing one) — Manager
- Snippet release on TikTok/Reels — Artist
- Prepare release-day stories and posts (queued in scheduler) — Marketing Lead
- Set up Spotify for Artists / Apple Music for Artists access for release-day analytics — Manager

## Week 1 — Release Week (Days 50–56) [offset: -7 / -3 / -1 / 0]

- 7 days out: countdown content begins — Artist [offset: -7]
- 3 days out: final push, ads start (if budget allows) — Marketing Lead [offset: -3]
- 1 day out: pre-save reminder to mailing list / fans — Manager [offset: -1]
- Release day: post drop announcement across all platforms — Artist [offset: 0]
- Release day: monitor distributor for go-live across all DSPs — Manager [offset: 0]
- Release day: thank-you content, share early stats with team — Artist + Manager [offset: 0]

## Week 0 — Release Week Wrap-Up (Days 57–63) [offset: 1]

- Compile first-week stats: streams, saves, playlist adds, top markets — Manager
- Debrief with team: what worked, what didn't — Manager
- Identify top-performing content from the rollout — Marketing Lead
- Identify any DSPs where the release underperformed expectations and investigate — Manager
- Thank-you posts to playlist curators, press, and collaborators who supported — Artist + Manager
- Update master release tracker — Manager

## Week +1 — First Follow-Up Push (Days 64–70) [offset: 8]

- Post 2–3 pieces of follow-up content (lyric video, BTS, alternate edit, fan reaction reposts) — cadence is per day or per week based on artist scale — Marketing Lead
- Pitch to secondary editorial playlists with first-week stats as evidence — Marketing Lead
- Engage with fan posts and tags from release week (repost, comment, share) — Artist
- Check for blog/press pickup and amplify any that landed — Marketing Lead

## Week +2 — Sustained Momentum (Days 71–77) [offset: 15]

- Post 2–3 pieces of follow-up content (live performance, acoustic version, story behind the song) — cadence per artist scale — Marketing Lead
- Pitch to mood-based or genre-based playlists (less competitive than editorial) — Marketing Lead
- Review Spotify for Artists / Apple Music for Artists analytics: where listeners are coming from, who they are — Manager
- [Conditional] Review ads performance: spend efficiency, top-performing creative, audience that converted, cost per save/stream — Marketing Lead
- Identify the strongest market and consider geo-targeted content for it — Marketing Lead

## Week +3 — Long Tail Setup (Days 78–84) [offset: 22]

- Post 2–3 pieces of follow-up content (milestone celebrations, listener stats share, artist commentary, remix tease if applicable) — cadence per artist scale — Marketing Lead
- Decide on the next release cycle: when is the next single, what builds on this momentum — Manager
- Document lessons learned in master release tracker for the next rollout — Manager
- Submit to year-end / quarterly editorial considerations if timing is relevant — Marketing Lead
