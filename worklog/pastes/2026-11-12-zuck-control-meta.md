# Why Meta is hard to beat: Zuck answers to nobody

Summary: an X post by Alex Kwon (@startupoppa) arguing Meta's edge is that Zuckerberg has full control, so he can make long bets and unpopular calls. Saved by Oyin from a screenshot, 2026-11-12.

Source: X, Alex Kwon (@startupoppa), last edited 23/09/2026, 226K views. Text copied as written; one swear word left out.

---

why @Meta is so formidable boils down to one simple fact.

zuck is structurally engineered to sit on the throne forever til he decides to step away.

in short, nobody can [mess] with him at meta. no investors, no stock prices, not even his wife can stop him.

napoleon once said "men are only controlled by two things: fear and self interest."

well, zuck has no fear, and his self interest of world domination is vast.

he can be as nimble as a startup can be even at the cost of short term sacrifice.

VR was a flop? i don't think so. they probably learned far more about consumer appetite for VR space than any standing company in existence and we will know it when the time is right.

colossal loss from that direction? doesn't matter. even if he lost everything on a bet, instagram facebook whatsapp will generate infinite cash to stand again.

plus, nobody can [mess] with him. board, public, nobody. and he's not the shy and safe type.

remember when timeline rolled out? people were signing petitions to undo it. zuck did it anyway and it became the bread and butter of the company.

## Why it might matter for Urganize (my read, not a decision)

- Founder control lets you make calls users push back on at first (like Timeline). Worth keeping in mind when raising money: what control we give up.
- A cash engine pays for long bets. For us that would be paying teams funding Audience and Opportunities.
