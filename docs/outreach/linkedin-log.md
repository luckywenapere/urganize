## What the inbox says (measured 3 Nov 2026, 80 threads)

| Opener style | Replied | Rate |
|---|---|---|
| Asks who they work with, no product ("I see you manage artists. Who are you working with right now...") | 7 of 8 | 88% |
| Small talk first ("Hello X, trust you're well?") | 13 of 19 | 68% |
| Playbook opener ("I'm Oyin, building tools for... hardest part of running a release?") | 13 of 36 | 36% |
| Names Urganize or "a tool" in the first message | 2 of 5 | 40% |
| Sends a link in the first message | 0 of 2 | 0% |

Rule from this: no product name, no link, in the first message or connection note. Open with something from their profile or post, then ask who they work with and what's dropping. Small samples, and any reply counts, not just good ones.
