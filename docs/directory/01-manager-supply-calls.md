# Test 1: Manager supply calls

Question: do good managers want to hear from artists they do not know?

Pass bar (from the spec): **10 of 20 say yes with a filter they would use.** A yes with no filter does not count. A filter is something they would really use to decide who gets through, like "a release already planned" or "a short pitch".

Who: 20 managers from the 100 to 150 sales list. Owner: Wena.

## How to pick the 20

1. Take the sales list. Cross out anyone you have never heard of the work of. These calls are for managers with real artists.
2. Spread the 20. Aim for about 7 who run one or two artists, 7 who run a small roster, and 6 from small labels. A yes from only one kind of manager tells you little.
3. Skip anyone who has already asked for a favour from you this month. Do not mix this with another ask.
4. If someone does not answer after two tries (one DM, one follow-up three days later), swap them for the next name. Write the swap in the tracker so the 20 stays honest.

## The call script (10 to 15 minutes)

Keep it a chat. Do not read it out. The questions in bold are the ones that matter.

**Open (30 seconds)**

"Hi {first name}, it's Wena from Urganize. Thanks for taking this. I will keep it to ten minutes. I'm not selling you anything today. I'm trying to find out if something would be useful, and you are exactly the person to ask. Is it ok if I take a few notes? I won't share your name with anyone."

**Warm up (2 minutes)**

1. "How many artists are you working with right now?"
2. "When a new artist wants you to manage them, how does that usually reach you?"

Listen. Write down the channels they name (DM, a friend, a producer, a show). Do not help them finish the sentence.

**The core (5 minutes)**

3. "Imagine artists could send you a short request inside Urganize. Their next release, their links, what they need. You can say yes, say no, or ignore it. After 7 days it closes by itself. Would you want that?"

Wait. Do not explain more. Let the silence work.

4. **"If you said yes, what would you want to see before you read a request? What would make you open it, and what would make you skip it?"**

Write their words exactly. Then probe once:

5. "Some people say a release already planned. Some say streaming numbers. Some say a short pitch. Which of those would you actually use?"

Do not offer more than three. If they pick something not on the list, write that down. It is worth more than the list.

6. "Would you switch it off if it got noisy? What would make it too noisy?"

**The yes check (2 minutes)**

7. **"Would you actually turn this on for your profile, if it was ready this month? Yes, maybe, or no?"**
8. "Is there anyone on your team who would handle these, or is it you?"

**Close (1 minute)**

"Thank you. This helps a lot. Can I tell you the moment something is ready, and ask you to look at it first? Is it ok to follow up on WhatsApp?"

Write down their answer before the call ends.

## How to mark the answer

Use exactly these three marks so the count is fair.

| Mark | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Yes with a filter | They said yes or maybe-leaning-yes in question 7, AND named a filter in question 4 or 5 that they said they would use. Counts toward the 10. |
| Yes, no filter | They said yes but could not name a filter, or said "anyone is fine". Does not count. Note it. It matters later. |
| No | They said no, or they said they would never open it. Does not count. Write the reason in their words. |

A "maybe" counts only if you would honestly bet they would turn it on when it is ready. If you are unsure, mark it "Yes, no filter". The bar is meant to be hard to pass.

## What to write down after every call (2 minutes, same day)

- What they said in their own words for questions 4, 5 and 6.
- Whether they could name a filter, and which one.
- Whether they asked "does it cost money?" (it does not cost to be found; say so if asked).
- Whether they asked about data, privacy or being spammed. This feeds the open questions in `07-monday-review-memo.md`.
- Follow-up: did they ask to see it first? Put a date next to it.

## What the filters tell you

After all 20, count the filters. The one named most feeds open question 3 in the memo ("Filter on inbound"). Do not decide the filter until the calls are done. If three or more managers name the same one, that is the default. If they all name different things, the honest answer is "let the manager pick" and say so at the review.

## What a pass and a fail mean

- 10 or more Yes with a filter: managers want to be found by artists they do not know. Go read the decision tree with test 3.
- Fewer than 10: do not argue with it. The decision tree says what to do. A fail here is useful. It saves you from building a "message the manager" feature nobody wants.
