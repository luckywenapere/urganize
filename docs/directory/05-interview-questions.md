# Two questions for every ROI and Famously Notorious interview

From the spec. Add both to the end of every ROI interview and every Famously Notorious conversation from now on. They cost two minutes and they tell us what finding people costs today, in the person's own words.

## The two questions

1. **How did you find your current manager (or your last producer, director, or designer)?**
2. **What did it cost you to find them, in time or money?**

Ask them in that order. Ask question 1, listen, then ask question 2. Do not offer examples. If they ask "what do you mean", say "time wasted, money spent, favours owed, anything at all".

## Why we ask

It is not a poll. It tells us three things:

- Where good matches really come from today (a friend, a show, a DM, a producer).
- How painful it is. If it took them eight months, that is a reason to use us. If it took a week, it is not.
- What to say when we pitch. Their words become our words.

## How to ask it so the answer is true

- Say it as a story question, not a yes or no. "Tell me how you found them" gets a story.
- Wait. Count to five after they stop before you say anything.
- If they say "a friend introduced us", ask once: "How did the friend know to introduce you?"
- Do not argue with an answer. Do not say "we can fix that" in the interview. Say "that makes sense" and write it down.

## What to write down

For each interview, add one line to the sheet. Same columns as `trackers/05-interview-answers.csv`.

| Date | Who (first name, or "anonymous") | Interview (ROI or Famously Notorious) | Looking for (manager, producer, director, designer) | How they found them (their words) | Cost: time (days, months) | Cost: money (naira or dollars, or "none") | Anything surprising |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| | | | | | | | |

If they will not say a number, write "would not say". Do not guess.

## Before the interview

Ask permission: "Can I note your answers? I will not use your name unless you say yes." For Famously Notorious, where the interview is public, ask whether the two answers can be quoted. If not, keep them in the sheet only.

## What to do with them

- Put the first 10 answers in the Monday review memo as a short list of raw quotes.
- Count: how many found their person through someone they already knew, and how many found them cold. If it is nearly all "someone I already knew", that supports the directory as a way to meet people you would not meet. If it is nearly all "cold DMs that took months", that supports it even more. Either way, note it.
- Do not treat these as a test with a bar. They are the reason behind the tests.
