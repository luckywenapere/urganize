# Test 2: Artist demand post

Question: when offered, do artists ask for a manager?

Pass bar (from the spec): **50 artist requests in 14 days.** Owner: KB.

One Dear Creative post and one community post. Same ask, same short form, no app needed. Start the 14-day clock on the day the first one goes live. Write the start date at the top of the tracker.

## What counts as a request

A request is one finished form from a real artist, with a working way to reach them. Not a like, not a comment, not a "how do I join".

Count it as 1 request if:
- the form is complete (name, how to reach them, one link, next release, what they need), AND
- it is not a copy of another one (same person twice counts once), AND
- it is not a test from our own team.

Do not count: our own test entries, a friend sent by us to make the number look better, or a form with no way to reach the person. Count those in a separate "not counted" column so you can see them. The bar is 50 real requests.

## The Dear Creative post

**Title:** Looking for a manager?

> Looking for a manager? Tell us about your next release.
>
> A lot of artists tell us the same thing. They have good music, a release coming up, and nobody to run it with them. Finding a manager takes months of DMs that go nowhere.
>
> So we are asking. Tell us about your next release in two minutes. Who you are, where we can hear you, when it is coming out, and what you need help with.
>
> There is nothing to download and nothing to pay. We read every one.
>
> [Tell us about your next release]  (link to the form)
>
> If you already have a manager, that is great. Pass this to a friend who does not.
>
> Wena, Urganize

## The community post (shorter, for WhatsApp and group chats)

> Looking for a manager? Tell us about your next release.
> Two minutes, no app, nothing to pay. We read every one.
> {form link}

Use the same line in any group chat you are in. Say who you are. Do not post it in a group where you have not spoken before.

## The short form (no app needed)

Build it in any form tool that works on a phone (Tally or Google Forms). Keep it to **seven questions**. Each extra question loses people. Put it on one screen on a phone.

| # | Question | Type | Required |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Your artist name | Short text | Yes |
| 2 | Where can we hear you? (one link: Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, SoundCloud) | Link | Yes |
| 3 | Your next release: what is it? (single, EP, album) | Pick one | Yes |
| 4 | When is it coming out, roughly? (this month, 1 to 3 months, 3 to 6 months, not sure yet) | Pick one | Yes |
| 5 | What do you need help with? (a manager, someone to run the release, something else) | Pick one, then short text | Yes |
| 6 | How can we reach you? (WhatsApp number or email) | Short text | Yes |
| 7 | Your city | Short text | No |

At the bottom, one tick box:

> Can we share this with a manager who might be a good fit? We will only share it with people we trust, and only about this release.

Make the tick box required to submit. If they do not agree, we cannot use it for test 3. Write one line under it:

> You can ask us to delete your answers any time. Message us and we will do it within 3 days.

Do not collect streaming numbers, bank details, ID, or age. Nothing here needs them and it raises risk.

## Where to send the replies

Send form answers to one sheet owned by KB. Share the sheet with Wena only. It holds phone numbers. Do not paste them into a group chat.

## Say which post worked

Add the source for every request. At the review, say which one brought more. That tells you where to post next time, even if the bar is missed.

## What a pass and a fail mean

- 50 or more: artists do ask for a manager when offered. Test 3 can use 10 of them.
- Under 50: read it with the source column. If almost all came from one place, the problem may be where you posted, not the offer. Say so at the review and decide whether to post once more before reading it as a fail. The decision tree stays strict: under 50 is a fail, and what you do next is a choice you make out loud.
