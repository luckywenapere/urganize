# Test 3: Concierge matches

Question: does a match turn into work?

Pass bar (from the spec): **3 of 10 start a shared release in Urganize within 30 days.** Owners: Wena and KB.

No product is built for this. You are the directory. You pick 10 artists from test 2, pick a manager for each from the managers who said yes in test 1, introduce them on WhatsApp, and watch what happens for 30 days.

## Before you start

- Test 2 must have finished with at least 10 artists who ticked the share box. Test 1 must have at least 10 managers with a yes.
- Do not start before both are done enough to choose from. A bad match tests nothing.
- A "shared release in Urganize" means: the artist and the manager are both on one rollout in Urganize. That is the bar. A chat, a call or a handshake does not count. The bar is the product, not the friendship.

## How to pick the 10 artists

1. Take artists who ticked the share box and gave a release date within 3 months.
2. Spread them across the managers. Do not send all 10 to the same two managers.
3. Skip anyone who already has a manager (they said so in the form or you can see it). Note it.
4. Write the 10 in the tracker before any messages go out.

## How to match

For each artist, pick the manager from the test 1 list whose filter they meet. If a manager said "a release already planned", only send artists with a date. If a manager named streaming numbers, ask the artist first before sharing. The filter the manager named is the rule. Write the match reason in one line.

## The WhatsApp scripts

Use the person's first name. Send each message yourself. Do not use a group. Do not send both sides at once. Ask the manager first. A cold intro to someone who has not agreed is how trust is lost.

### Message 1: ask the manager first

> Hi {manager}, it's Wena from Urganize. You told me you'd like to hear from artists who {their filter, in their words}. I have one I think fits. {Artist name}, {one line about them and their release, and the link}. Their next release is {date}. Want me to introduce you? If not, no problem at all.

If they say no or do not answer in 48 hours, note it and pick another manager for that artist.

### Message 2: ask the artist, then make the intro

> Hi {artist}, it's Wena from Urganize. Thanks for telling us about your release. A manager who I respect, {manager name}, would like to hear from you. Is it ok if I introduce you two on WhatsApp?

### Message 3: the intro (after both say yes)

> {Manager first name}, meet {artist first name}. {Artist}, {manager} manages {their artists, or what they do}. {Manager}, {artist} is putting out {release} on {date}.
>
> I'll leave you two to talk. If you decide to plan the release together, you can do it in Urganize. Anyone can start a rollout and invite the other. Tell me if anything is stuck and I will help.

### Message 4: the nudge (day 10, only if no release has started)

To the artist:

> Hi {artist}, how did it go with {manager}? Did you two talk?

To the manager:

> Hi {manager}, did you hear from {artist}? Tell me if there is anything I can help with.

### Message 5: the check (day 25)

> Hi {name}, quick check. Did you start planning {release} together in Urganize? If not, what got in the way? I am trying to learn, not to push.

Always ask what got in the way. A "no" with a reason is worth as much as a "yes".

### If someone does not want to be matched

Reply: "No problem at all. I'll delete what you sent me." Then delete it within 3 days and mark it in the tracker.

## What to learn even if it passes

Write three lines at the end of each week:
- What did managers ask for in the intro that the form did not give them?
- What made an artist go quiet?
- Where did people try to use WhatsApp instead of Urganize?

The third one is the most important. If a match works but the work stays on WhatsApp, the loop is not closing, and that is not a pass in spirit.

## What a pass and a fail mean

- 3 or more of 10: matches turn into work. Build the artist request flow.
- Fewer than 3: the decision tree says to keep the directory for manager-to-crew hiring and drop the artist-finds-manager angle for now. Say it plainly to yourself and to the team. Do not read it as "needs one more try" without a new reason.
