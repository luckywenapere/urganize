1

Wider, in two columns. What the task is and what to do sit on the left; status, who, due, phase and "On the brief" sit on the right with a small label each. Today it is a 670px column where every line is just an icon and a value, so you have to guess which line is which.

2

A late task says so, with the two fixes beside it: "Move to this week" and "Mark done". Today it is a small red "58 days overdue" and you have to find the date field yourself.

3

"To Do" appears once, in Status. Today it shows twice: under the title and again on the first line.

4

Dates in words: "Wed 16 Sep", not "16/09/2026". "Pre-release (from its date)" becomes "Pre-release" with a short hint under it.

5

What it unlocks, not just what it needs. "Needs first" is today's "Depends on". "Waiting on this" is new: the tasks that cannot start until this one is done, which is the reason to do it now. Both come from the dependencies the plan already has.

6

Where it lives, at the top: artist, rollout and "Open rollout". Today the only clue is "Salt and Signal" in small grey text.

7

"Find graphic designer" becomes a short card that says how many are open to work, instead of a lone green link.

8

The key-activity pin, delete and close stay top right. Delete moves into the ⋯ menu so it is not one click from the close button.
