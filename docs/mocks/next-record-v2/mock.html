Decided today (27 Oct)

  1. The rollout button sits at the top of the record from day one, under the cover. The words under it follow the songs. You can plan before every song is done.
  2. Pressing it shows one "Here's what I'll use" sheet, then the planner with 4 questions. Unfinished songs become tasks.
  3. After planning the record becomes the rollout and leaves In the making. Its songs, sessions and people stay readable from the rollout.
  4. The top of the page is a cover and a tracklist. Nothing is required: the colour comes from the artist, the title starts as "Untitled", and cover, name and kind can be changed any time.
  5. Rare actions (rename, cover, kind, drop) go in a ⋯ menu on the cover. Drop is last, in red, and asks once.
  6. Screens 1, 4 and 5 stay. One change to 4: no separate box on the record. The app's own UrgAns box does the capture there.
