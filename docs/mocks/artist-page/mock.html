3 · Why it's laid out this way

No click data yet, so the order is our prediction. What to measure is in the last box.

PM: what goes where

  1. Status before content. A manager running five artists opens this page to answer "where is T3N at?". Two chips under the name answer that before any scrolling.
  2. New record first. Between releases, the next record is the work. Plan a rollout sits beside it.
  3. Up next is the one action. The countdown when a rollout is live, otherwise the record to finish.
  4. One Music section. A song moves from In the making to Rolling out to Out now. Three separate cards made people ask "is Kunu a rollout or not?".
  5. Posting plan goes quiet. Side column on desktop, bottom on phone. What UrgAns knows is gone from the page.

Designer: how it looks

  • Fewer boxes. Music sits on the page, not in a card, so the timeline line can run through it.
  • A timeline with a lit stage. The green dot marks where the artist is now, like a record moving through a pressing plant.
  • Words on buttons. The add icon (a cross through a diamond) read as an AI sparkle.

Engineer: what changes in code

  • Rewrite ArtistPageClient.tsx layout. Same data: records, rollouts, catalogue, posts. No new API, no migration.
  • Rows from the three music cards move into one Music section. Smart link, add code and cover edit keep working.
  • Imported catalogue rows and past rollouts both land in Out now.
  • Measure: taps on Up next and on New record from this page, before and after.
