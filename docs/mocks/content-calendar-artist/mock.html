Posting plan, on the artist

The content calendar, back in a lighter form. It lives on the artist, so it works between releases, and UrgAns nudges the person posting on WhatsApp on the day.

  • Four fields: what, day, where (optional), who posts.
  • No calendars to set up first, no month grid, no caption, format, pillar or link.
  • A post can belong to a release, but doesn't have to.
