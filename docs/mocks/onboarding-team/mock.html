Onboarding: account, team, then music

Clickable prototype for issue #854. Four screens for a new team, two for an invited teammate. Every button works. Use the controls to switch phone and desktop, dark and light, and to start either path. Built from spec.md and design-direction.md in this folder. Demo people: Kemi Bello (new), invited by Tobi Adeyemi of Northline Music.

Notes: what changed and why

Three questions for Oyin

  1. Unfinished music (Just an idea, Recording, Mixing or mastering) saves as a record in the making; Ready and Already out save as a rollout. One form either way. Oyin: yes, 29 Oct
  2. When someone who joined a team as a Manager adds music, it belongs to that team (the one in the pill). Needs a server fix. Oyin: yes, 29 Oct
  3. The card says "New music", not "project". Use the Card control above to compare. Oyin: New project, 29 Oct

Settled in the spec (change if you disagree)

  • The team is made on "Set up your team", prefilled "{First name}'s Team". Blank is fine. Names need not be unique.
  • "Ask to be invited" leads to a calm waiting screen that checks every 20 seconds and takes a pasted invite link.
  • The trial sheet can be closed. It opens once the plan is built (or 8 seconds after a record lands), never over the planner's questions.

PM review fixes (29 Oct)

  • The artist field no longer fills in your own name. A stage name is rarely the name on the account.
  • "Pick a day" with no day, or a day that has passed, now blocks Add it and says why.
  • On phone, Add it stays at the bottom of the screen once the form is long. It was a scroll away.
  • The preview shows only after a stage is picked, captioned "Preview". The "URG·001" number and "How it shows on Home" are gone: no such number exists, and a record lives under the artist, not on Home.
  • The waiting row sits under the WhatsApp button, so nobody waits before asking.
  • The invite sheet drops "What can they see?" here: with no rollouts yet there is nothing to choose.
  • Artist suggestions: after "Bring in released music", the artist shows as a one-tap suggestion.
  • "Not you?" now says "Not you? Log out", so it says what it does.
  • Going back to the team after it is made says "Save and continue", not "Create team" again.
  • The trial sheet waits for the planner's answer on a rollout with no date, instead of covering the question.
  • Trial plan lines match the pricing package (Manager: 3 artists, 5 people, three rollouts at a time). "Distribution included" was dropped: it depends on how they pay.
  • New states in Jump: email already used, invite-only, two invites with one run out, couldn't check for invites, still waiting after 30 minutes, no connection on Add it, joined a team as a Manager.

What changed from today, and why

  • 13 screens down to 4 (2 for an invited teammate). Nothing was saved until screen 10 before, so anyone who left early left with nothing.
  • Account first. No questions before it. Google works from the first second.
  • Join before you make. We check for a waiting invite before offering a new team, so invited people stop landing in an empty team of their own.
  • Music at any stage. No release day needed. "Just an idea" is the first stop on the list, drawn the same size as "Ready to release", so it never looks like the lesser answer.
  • A live preview card fills in as you type. It is the same card you meet on Home, so the first thing you see in the app is the thing you just made.
  • Price after value. The trial sheet comes after the plan, says what is true ("is on"), and closes.
  • "How did you hear about us" moved to the plan-building wait, one tap.

Visual fixes from the audit

  • Young Serif at its real weight. Today it is drawn in fake bold.
  • One main button colour: spring green, same as /auth and the app. Today onboarding uses cream.
  • Sentence case labels. Capitals only in Space Mono stamps and dates.
  • One radius scale: 8 tiles, 12 fields and rows, 16 cards, full for buttons and chips.
  • Outlined Google button. Today it is pure white.
  • No box around the question on phone, no "Welcome aboard" title, no 12-part progress bar.
  • Choice rows have a visible edge in light mode, and selected means a filled dot plus a green border, not a faint tint.
  • Back is a small text link, so it never competes with the main button.
  • Logo and avatar sit where the app puts them, so landing in the app does not jump.

Where the designer went against the spec (all accepted by the PM)

  • Spring green main button, not the cream one. The spec keeps today's buttons; the cream one is the odd one out across auth and the app.
  • No card around the step on phone. The spec says "dark card on the app background". On 375px the card costs 40px of width and a layer of chrome. Desktop keeps cards on the right, like the reference.
  • Stage asked before artist. The fields below depend on the stage, so asking it first means the form never reshapes under something already typed.
  • Stages as a list, not chips. Five chips of very different lengths wrap into ragged lines on a phone and look ranked. A list reads as one line of work, idea to out, each stop the same size.
  • "Who is this team for?" as three rows, not chips, for the same reason: equal targets for the thumb.
  • Release day as two chips (No date yet, Pick a day) instead of a date box plus a "No date yet" checkbox. A greyed-out date box above a ticked box read as broken, and it matches the Already out chips.
  • On the waiting screen, WhatsApp comes first, then the message preview, then the email. Once the invite lands, the ask disappears and only Join is left, so there is one green button on screen.
  • "How did you hear about Urganize?" sits above the plan while it builds, not under it, so it is seen during the wait it is meant for.
