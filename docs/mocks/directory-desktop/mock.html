1

The big initials tiles are gone. They repeated the first six people in the list at four times the size and gave no reason to pick them. One list, one row per person.

2

Roles you hire for, not three chips. Today the chips are All, Managers, A&R and PR, so you cannot find a designer or an engineer without typing. Each row also says what they delivered, from real rollouts, or "New here".

3

Open to work is a switch, on by default. Nobody hiring wants a list of people who are booked.

4

Ask is on the row. It opens the same ask sheet as today. You no longer have to open a profile to reach someone.

5

The right column is about you. What your rollouts still need (from the plan, the same gaps panel the rollout shows), the asks you sent and what came back, and your own listing.
