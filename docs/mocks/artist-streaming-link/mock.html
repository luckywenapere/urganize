How it works behind the scenes
  • You pick the artist once, on Deezer (it gives the codes for free). We then link the same artist on Spotify and Apple Music ourselves, by matching one of their songs' ISRC, so you never pick three times.
  • On release day we look at that artist's newest releases only, match the title, and read the UPC and ISRC from it.
  • Artists already linked from the old music import keep their link. Right now none of the 68 artists in the app have one, so everyone sees the popup once.
