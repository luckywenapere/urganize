UrgAns remembers each artist

Small and structured: one fact per line, each tied to an artist, with where it came from. UrgAns asks before it saves anything, reads the facts before it answers, and the artist page is where you check, change or remove them.

A clear decision about an artist gets one card. Nothing is saved until you tap Save. Chit-chat and one-off asks get no card.

The rules behind it

  • One fact, one line. Artist, topic, what it says, where it came from (said in chat or set by hand), who, and when.
  • Five topics. Playlist pitching, preferred channel, budget, who to contact, and something else. One line per topic per artist, except "something else", which can have up to 10.
  • Short. A fact is up to 200 characters. Longer thoughts belong in a note.
  • UrgAns never saves on its own. Every save or change from chat is a card you approve. You can edit or remove any fact on the artist page.
  • The whole team shares it. Anyone on the team who can see the artist sees and follows the same facts.
  • Logged. Every fact UrgAns saves, changes or removes goes in the UrgAns action log.
  • Not a memory of everything. Only facts about artists for now. No chat transcripts, no guesses about people.
