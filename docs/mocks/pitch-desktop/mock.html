1. You always see what you are about to spendToday you tick curators and scroll to find out the cost. A dark card on the right adds up the credits as you tick, shows what you have, and holds the send button.
2. The promise is said once, plainly"A listen and a written reply in 7 days, or your credits back" is the reason to pay. It sits under the heading and again under the button, where the decision is made.
3. The kit is a short checklist"What curators read" becomes three ticked lines (story, credits, bio) with Edit and Copy share link, in the right column, so it is there but not in the way.
4. Editors are clearly separateSpotify and Apple Music editorial pitches are their own card, free, under the curators, instead of a heading lower down the page.
5. Answers come back to the same pageA "Sent" card lists who you pitched and what they said, with a link to the curator inbox. The floating green "Your curator inbox" text at the top goes. The sample replies here are made up for the mock.
