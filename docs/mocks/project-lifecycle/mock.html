One lifecycle: a project becomes a rollout

Where a release starts before the music exists. A project has two stages, Idea and Making, with real tasks so the AI can suggest work. Plan the rollout turns it into the rollout you already know, with Pre-production holding whatever was still open. Names and songs are made up. Drawn in the app's light look at phone width, one computer frame at the end.

Stages live on the project

The record row gets an Idea stage and tasks. It is not a rollout row, so rollout counts, plan limits and the scoreboard stay honest.

Forward by itself, never back

Start making is a button. The stage also moves on its own the first time a song goes past Idea or a session is saved. Drop is the only way down.

Tasks are real tasks

Same rows as a rollout's activities, so chasing, assignment emails, the task pop-up and the AI all work on a project with no new plumbing.

One way to start the next one

Start the next one makes a project. The old pre-production rollout and its eight seeded jobs become suggestions in the Making stage.

What I am asking you to decide

  1. Start making as the big button in the Idea stage, with Plan the rollout quieter underneath. The other way round keeps your 27 Oct rule (rollout button on top from day one) but makes the idea stage look like it only exists to be skipped.
  2. Suggestions as a short list with + Add, on the page and from the AI, never auto-added. Taps add a real task. The list is fixed per stage, plus one per song from its stage, so the AI tailors and never invents.
  3. The idea text comes back on the page (it was left off the 27 Oct page). Shown in the Idea stage, folded under the cover once Making starts.
  4. Carried tasks say "from the project" on the rollout for a while, so a teammate understands why a task they were given on the project now lives on a rollout.
  5. Start the next one asks one question (Just an idea, or Already making it) and nothing else. Name, kind and cover come later, as on the record page today.

Not in these mocks, on purpose

  1. Dates on project tasks are optional and carry nothing until a rollout date exists. The rollout planner never reads them.
  2. WhatsApp check-ins for sessions (step 5 from 27 Oct) still wait on Twilio and your yes.
  3. No click data exists yet, so the order of everything here is a prediction. The measure stays the same: weekly active teams with no rollout in progress.
