1

The chat box is the first section, above Next drop and My tasks. It greets you by name and time of day.

2

Four suggestions, written from your own data: your nearest rollout, your late tasks, an artist with nothing planned. Click one and it is sent, the same as typing it.

3

It says "AI", not a product name, as everywhere since 3 Nov.

4

The + adds a file or photo, and "About" picks a rollout. See the next frame.

5

Next drop, My tasks, Delegated, Team and Artists are unchanged, one step lower. In Customise Home the chat box can be moved or removed like the other blocks.
