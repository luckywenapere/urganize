Urganize Music, version 2

Light theme. The same top tabs, green underline and swipe as the dashboard. DM Sans (the logo's font) on everything. shadcn icons (lucide). The dashboard's wallpaper behind everything. Analog touches: records instead of square covers, a turntable player, a dial for the song's position, cassette tiles for the coming-soon playlists, and a faint paper grain.

What changes, in short
  • Two tabs, Home and Collection. Search is an icon beside the circle. Settings is no longer a tab; it lives in the circle menu.
  • The loud Dashboard and Pitches buttons go; the circle holds them.
  • A headphones circle on the dashboard brings you back to Music, and the avatar menu gets an "Urganize Music" row too.
  • Upload is in the circle menu, on dashboard Home, and still inside each rollout.
  • The dashboard's wallpaper and theme clock on every music screen, so the two sides feel like one app.
  • Everything in DM Sans, the logo's font. Icons from lucide, the set shadcn uses, on the music side only.
  • The analog touches move only to say something true: the record spins only while a song plays.
