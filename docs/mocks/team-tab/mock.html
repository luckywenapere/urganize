What changed this round

  1. Desktop People is a card grid. Each card: face, role, when they were last active, Open / Late / Done 7d, and their next task (red if late). Teams here are 2 to 8 people, so the whole team fits on one screen.
  2. Empty seats show as an "Invite someone" card and pending invites as dashed cards in the same grid. You see the full shape of the team, including the room left.
  3. One person opens in a side panel over the grid instead of a fixed column, so the grid keeps its full width.
  4. The access limit shows on the card as a green pill ("2 rollouts", "Zara only"), so you can tell who sees what at a glance.
  5. Phone stays a list, only light mode changed. Cards on a phone would show 1 to 2 people per screen and hide the team.
  6. "Transfer" is now "Hand over": plainer.

Still standing from last round (say if any is wrong)

  • Order: online first, then most recently active, then never seen. Same as Home's "Your team" card.
  • You can only hand the workspace to a manager on this team who does not own another workspace.
  • When someone leaves or is removed, their open tasks go back to the owner.
  • Someone limited to chosen rollouts sees teammates' tasks only on those rollouts. If all their rollouts are deleted they see nothing, not everything. New rollouts are not added to them on their own.
  • My People stays hidden. Settings → Team goes away; its link opens this tab. Billing stays in Settings.
