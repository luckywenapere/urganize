How the staff PM and designer settled it

  1. Two parts, not five. People (who, when, what they hold) and Workspace (name, seats, links, leave). A third "Activity" part was cut: "last active" and "Done 7d" already answer "who has been working".
  2. One click to someone's tasks. The panel opens beside the list, so a manager can go down the team in ten seconds. Late tasks lead, in red, because that is why you open it.
  3. Order: online first, then most recently active, then never seen. Same rule as Home's "Your team" card, so the two never disagree.
  4. Pending invites sit at the bottom with "Copy link", because on WhatsApp the link is what actually gets them in.
  5. No new tracking. "Last active" is the ping we already keep. "Done 7d" counts tasks marked done in the last 7 days.
  6. Everyone sees everyone's tasks, as you asked. Tapping a task opens it in its rollout. Ticking it from here is left out on purpose: you work tasks in the rollout.

Decisions I made for you (say if any is wrong)

  • Transfer only goes to a manager on this team who does not own another workspace. Each person can own one workspace today. If nobody qualifies, the button explains why.
  • When someone leaves or is removed, their open tasks go back to the owner so nothing is left with no one.
  • Access has three choices: all rollouts (default), one artist (what artist invites do today), or chosen rollouts (new, for any role). Managers always see everything. The server enforces it, the same place the artist limit is enforced today.
  • A limited person sees teammates' tasks only on rollouts they can open. Otherwise the Team tab would leak names of rollouts kept from them.
  • If every chosen rollout is deleted, they see nothing, not everything. (The artist limit today falls back to "everything" when its artist is deleted; chosen rollouts will not.)
  • New rollouts are not added to a limited person automatically. You add them from "Change role or access".
  • My People stays hidden (paused since 27 Oct). Nothing deleted.
  • Settings → Team goes away; its link opens this tab. Billing stays in Settings.
