What happens to the chats people already have

  • Every saved session is folded into the one chat, oldest first, each under its date and its old title, like "From 'Pitch ideas' · 3 Oct".
  • Nothing is deleted. A copy of every old session is kept before they are folded in.
  • Rollout chats start fresh from today, because old sessions do not say which rollout they were about.

How the brief works

  • 8am Lagos time, every day, for people who used Urganize in the last 14 days.
  • The facts come straight from your rollouts and tasks. AI only writes the opening line and picks the one next step. If AI is down, a plain version still arrives.
  • It does not use any of your AI credits.
  • You can turn it off in Settings, under Notifications.
