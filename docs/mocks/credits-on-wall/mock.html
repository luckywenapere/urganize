Try the assistant for ₦1,500, right on the wall

Someone without a plan can already buy credits and use the assistant. Today that option is one small line at the bottom of the plan wall. This puts it on the wall itself, with its price, as a clear second choice under the plans. Nothing else changes: credits still open the assistant only. Names and numbers are made up.

What’s better than today
Seen without scrolling
Today the credits line is the last thing on the wall, under the label line and the small print. Now it sits right under the plans, inside the first screen.
A price, not a vague offer
“Get credits” becomes “Try it for ₦1,500”. People know what it costs before they tap.
Plans still lead
The plan button stays the one filled button. The ₦1,500 card uses an outline button, so it is the second choice, not a rival.
Pay in one step
Today the line opens a separate page, then you pick a pack. Now a sheet opens on the wall with ₦1,500 already picked.
Clear about what it opens
The sheet says it is the assistant only, so nobody pays expecting rollouts.
A road back to a plan
After paying, the assistant shows the balance and one quiet line pointing to plans.

What I need from you

  1. 1The words. “Not ready for a plan? Try the assistant for ₦1,500.” Keep, or change?
  2. 2Bigger packs in the sheet. Show all four packs, or only ₦1,500 to keep it simple?
  3. 3Where else. Also put the same card on the pricing page and the plans screen in onboarding, or the wall only for now?

Measured vs assumed

  • Measured: the packs and prices (₦1,500 buys 50 credits without a plan), that buying is switched on in production, and where the credits line sits on today’s wall. A quick question costs 1 credit and a longer answer about 3.
  • Assumed: that most people never see today’s line. There is no click data for it.
