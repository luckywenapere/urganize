Mark a rollout as a test

Of 70 rollouts outside our team on 28 Nov, only 2 were marked as tests, and 20 more look like tests. This lets the person who made one say so in one tap, with one tap to undo. It reuses the test banner and the Test tag the app already shows for "Try a test release". Dashed outline = what is new. Issue #1442.

1. The rollout menu

One new row at the bottom, next to Archive and Delete, where people go to tidy up a rollout. No confirm step: marking a test deletes nothing and is undone in one tap.
