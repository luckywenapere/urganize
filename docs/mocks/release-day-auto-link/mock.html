How it works behind the scenes

  • Linking fills in the past. When someone links an artist in the popup, the app brings in their old releases (the same import that exists today) and matches every rollout already in Urganize to its streaming release: UPC, ISRC and a smart link, filled in silently.
  • Our hosting checks once a day, early on release day (about 08:00 Lagos). Songs that land later are found the next time anyone on the team opens the app, and again the next morning.
  • Artists not linked yet keep today's flow, including "Type the code yourself".
  • If a linked artist's song still isn't found by the day after release, the card shows "Type the code yourself" so nobody is stuck.
  • Email cost: one email per team member per release. Our email plan allows 100 a day.
