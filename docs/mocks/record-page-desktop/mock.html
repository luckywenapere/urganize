1. The page uses the desktop widthToday it is one narrow column in the middle of the screen. The idea and the tracklist sit on the left, tasks on the right, so tasks are visible without scrolling.
2. The cover is a square, not a bannerA cover is square everywhere else in music. With no cover it is a plain tile that says so, with "Add cover". No colour-wash placeholder.
3. One main button, at the top"Plan the rollout" moves from the middle of the page to the top right, where the main action sits on the other pages. The line under it says why it is ready.
4. The stages read as a pathIdea, Making, Rollout become three steps on one line with a tick for done, in place of three boxes that look like buttons.
5. The tracklist explains its barsEach song's four bars sit under their names: Idea, Recorded, Mixed, Done. Today the names are in a corner and the bars are unlabelled.
