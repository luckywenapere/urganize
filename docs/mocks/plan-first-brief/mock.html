Plan first. The brief is one tap away.

A rollout with a plan opens on Work the plan again. The brief moves into a button on the plan's title line, so it stays there even when the menu folds. Tapping it opens the brief on the whole screen until you go back to the plan. Before there is a plan, the brief is still the step: the team reads and approves it, and that builds the plan.

What changes, what stays

  • Changes: a rollout with a plan opens on Work the plan. Read the brief leaves the tabs (still in •••). The brief opens on the whole screen from the Brief button and closes with Back to the plan.
  • Changes: right after an ideas round, the rollout opens on the brief to approve, until someone approves it.
  • Stays: the brief board itself, Arrange, the view link, the ideas round, approve and New version. No data changes.
  • Measure: Brief button taps, time on the brief, and how people leave it (Back, Esc, browser back).
