How it works

  • Runs in the browser. No outside service, no cost per image, and the art never leaves Urganize.
  • Every upload path gets it. The release page and the "upload your cover art" sheet after a task is ticked both use the same upload step.
  • Saves as a JPEG at high quality, about 2 to 4 MB assumed. Well under the 25 MB limit.
  • The 1500px line between "ready" and "may look soft" is my judgement, not a store rule assumed.
  • The original is not kept. Only the 3000 × 3000 file is saved.
