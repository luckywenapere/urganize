How it works behind the screens

  • First payment of an add-on goes through Paystack like any checkout. That also saves the card.
  • Each month we charge add-ons plus any credits used past the allowance on that card, on the plan's renewal day, in one charge. Bank transfer payers can't take add-ons until they add a card.
  • Money we owe TRM: 80% of every single (₦1,600) and every distribution add-on (₦5,680 a month, ₦20,400 a year) goes on the TRM ledger, same as today's extra releases.
  • Credits already bought in a pack stay and are used before any paid credits.
