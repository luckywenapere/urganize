1. Out now is one lineToday it is a tall card with a full-width button. It is a small status, so it takes one line, and the action is a link.
2. Each row says when it dropsThe date is the first thing a manager checks. Today the rows show no date at all.
3. Soonest drop firstThe list follows the same order as the Next drop card. Artists with nothing planned sit last.
4. Columns sit closerToday the eye travels across empty space from the name to the rollout to the tasks. The page is 1120 wide and the columns are joined.
5. Late is a number, not a red pillWith 28 to 37 late on every row, red pills everywhere stop meaning anything. One red number per row keeps it readable.
7. Tasks on Home (new)Two lists under the Next drop card: My tasks, and Delegated, which is work you gave to someone else. Late first, then soonest. You can tick your own off here. A delegated task shows who has it and when they finish it. The tasks and names in this mock are samples.
8. Your team (new, desktop only)One row of people under the tasks. Each person shows their role and how much is on them, with late work in red. Click a person to see their tasks. On a phone this row is left out and the Team page stays where it is. Names and counts here are samples.
9. Customise Home (new)A quiet button at the bottom of Home opens this mode. Drag a block to move it, press Remove to hide it, and add it back from "Add to Home". The layout is saved for you only, not your whole team. Your artists cannot be removed, so Home is never empty. Reset puts everything back.
6. A way to add an artistHome with artists has no Add artist today. It gets a quiet button at the top of the list and a row at the bottom.
