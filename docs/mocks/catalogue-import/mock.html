Released music: how it comes in

Issue #882. Today "Import past rollouts" sits under the Artists list as a separate job. That's the wrong idea: old music is not a rollout, and it belongs to one artist. The rethink:

  1. You find the artist, you don't type them. Adding an artist starts with a search of Deezer and Apple Music. Pick the right one and Urganize brings their photo and every release, marked as already out.
  2. It stays up to date. Urganize remembers the artist's streaming profile. When they drop something new, it shows up under Out now by itself.
  3. Released music never counts as a rollout. It doesn't use up plan limits or show up in rollout counts. It just fills the artist's Out now and can get a smart link.
  4. The button leaves the Artists list. Artists already added by name get one "Find on streaming" card on their own page.

Covers, photos and names are sample data.

What this changes under the hood
  • The artist remembers their streaming profile (one new field on artists, no new table). That's what lets new drops come in by themselves: a check at most once a day, when someone opens the artist.
  • Released music stays in its own place, as it does today, so it never counts as a rollout, a plan limit or a scoreboard number.
  • "Import past rollouts" is removed from the Artists list on desktop and phone. The import inside onboarding stays; it's the same search.
  • Not in this mock: a team-wide page of all released music. It could come later as an "Out now" filter under Rollouts.
