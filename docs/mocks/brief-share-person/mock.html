What it does, in one place

  • Who can share: the rollout's owner and team admins, like the view link.
  • The guest: a team guest limited to this one rollout. They read the brief and the plan, and can only mark their own activities done (your call, 31 Oct).
  • Seats: guests invited from a brief take no paid seat, up to 5 per rollout (your call, 31 Oct). A 6th is refused with a plain message.
  • Their part: the pieces picked are assigned to them when they join, so the owner and due date stay in the plan.
  • Removing a guest takes away their access; their activities stay in the plan, unassigned.
