The rules
One question a day, at most
Starts on day 2. The first brief is just useful, no asking.
Every answer changes tomorrow
If an answer would change nothing anyone can see, we don't ask it.
Skipped means quiet
No answer yesterday, no question today. Two skips in a row and it waits a week.
Nothing hidden
Every answer is listed in Settings, with a way to change or clear it. One switch turns the questions off.
No streaks, no counters
People come back because tomorrow's brief is shaped by what they said.
No AI needed
The questions and what each answer does are written in code. AI can reword, never invent a question.
Better than today's brief
The brief asks, not only tells
Today the brief is one-way. Now it ends with a question you can answer in one tap.
The reason is on screen
A small line under the opening says why the brief looks this way, with Change next to it.
Tap targets you can hit
Answer buttons are 40px tall, full width on a phone, and the picked one is clearly marked.

Measured vs assumed

  • Measured (in the code today): the brief already arrives at 8am Lagos, once per person per day, with Needs you, Since yesterday, Coming up and One thing first. It has an off switch in Settings.
  • Assumed: that a question people can answer in one tap brings them back more than the brief alone. We have no numbers on brief opens yet, so we would count opens and answers from day one.
  • New to build: a place to keep each person's answers, the question list, and the brief reading those answers. No new screen besides the Settings list.
