How the plan is chosen

  1. Hit a limit: the cheapest plan above theirs that lifts that limit (more artists, more people, more rollouts at once, or more AI). The button that sent them here says which limit.
  2. Trial ended or first plan: start from who they said they are at sign-up (artist starts at Solo, manager or label starts at Pro). If they picked a plan on the sign-up screen, start there instead.
  3. Then make sure it fits: if the team already has more artists, people or rollouts in progress than that plan allows, move up to the smallest plan that holds them all. Nobody is shown a plan that would lock part of their team out.
  4. Everything else stays one tap away behind "See all plans", which opens today's four plans. Label stays a "Talk to us" line.
