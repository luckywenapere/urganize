Change a rollout's release date

Today the date can only be changed from a late Spotify pitch warning, a Distribute checklist row, or by asking UrgAns. This adds two plain doors on the rollout page, both opening one sheet that says what will move before you save. Owner only, same as today. Rollouts with no date keep their "Set your release date" row and are not changed.

1. Phone: tap the date new

The line under the title already says when it drops. It becomes a button with a small calendar mark. Teammates who are not the owner see the same line as plain text.
