Artists list: three directions

Issue #878. Today the page is a plain table: face, name, a rollout count. Each direction takes a cue from Spotify and adds facts we already keep: the next rollout and its cover, days until it drops, tasks due this week and late. No new tables. Drawn with your real roster, Dorian and T3N. Titles, dates and covers are sample data.

B. Spotlight and list · recommended

Spotify cue: the big banner at the top of an artist page, then a tracklist. Riff: the banner goes to whichever rollout drops next. The eye lands on the cover, reads the title, then lands on the countdown, then drops to the list. Every row reads left to right in the order a manager asks: who, what is next, when, what needs doing.

The same in all three
  • One status word per artist, always the same three: Rolling out (green), Making a record, Quiet. Red is only for late tasks.
  • A missing photo or cover is a flat tile, never a gradient. Tapping a face still changes the photo.
  • "Import past rollouts" moves up beside "Add artist", so the top right holds every action.
  • Task counts are what the viewer can see, the same rule as the artist page.
