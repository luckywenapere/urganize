1. One page for getting a song to storesToday cover art is its own page with one card on it, and release details is three closed rows on an empty page. Both become rows in the same checklist the Distribute tab already has, so there is one place to look.
2. What is missing is obviousEach row has a green tick or a red mark, and the dark card on the right says how many things are left. The send button stays grey until they are done.
3. It uses the desktop widthThe checklist is on the left. The send button, the distributor and your plan's allowance are on the right. Today it is one centred column.
4. Rounded corners and plain textThis page has square boxes and typewriter-style capital letters ("WE'LL TELL YOU THE MOMENT IT MOVES"). It gets the same rounded cards and normal sentences as the rest of the app.
5. After sending, you can still see what wentThe five items stay listed with "View", so you can check the cover or the credits that were sent without hunting for them.
