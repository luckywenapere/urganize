# Feature cuts (2026-10-16, founder brief, URG-319)

> Moved out of CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-22 (verbatim). "This file", "above", "below" and "the X section" in here refer to the old single CLAUDE.md: find the named section in docs/notes/README.md.

The founder approved a keep/cut brief: Urganize does one job, getting a manager's next release out on time with their team, then giving them a reason to open the app before the next one. Fourteen features left the nav and every entry point. **Hidden, not deleted:** no table, row or migration was touched, and the code stays until 60 days pass with nobody asking for it, then goes only with the founder's OK.

- **One switch for the flagged ones:** `apps/web/lib/cut-features.json` is spread into `next.config.js` `env`, which Next ranks ABOVE the process environment, so these flags read `'false'` in every literal `process.env` read whatever Vercel says: EVENTS (shows, setlists, run of show, DJ packs, the /pack page, Home and artist-page shows), CAPTURE, RELEASE_CHAT, CONTENT_CALENDAR, URGANS_VOICE and URGANS_REALTIME (dictation and Talk to UrgAns), MCP_CONNECT (founder's own /api/mcp still answers), INVITE_GATE (the freeze is gone, nothing replaces it), SHARE_REWARD (claimed bonus days stay; resolveTeamAccess reads the columns, not the flag), NOTES, PITCH_PAYMENTS (curator earnings, payouts, pricing; the free curator list stays). `lib/cut-features.test.ts` pins the list. To bring one back, delete its line in both files.
- **In code:** Audience is gone from `lib/shell-nav.ts` and the G-then-A shortcut, and `/dashboard/audience` redirects to Home. The Spend scene has no menu row and `resolveActiveScene('spend')` is null; the payback email sweep is off (the credits sweep still writes its lines). `/dashboard/shows` and `/dashboard/shows/[id]` redirect to the Catalogue. UrgAns is no longer offered the show (except list_songs/create_song), content-calendar, notes and capture tools, though they stay resolvable by name (`CUT_CHAPPI_TOOLS` in `lib/chappi/tools.ts`) so an old confirmation card fails as an ordinary error. The homepage feature grid lost its Shows tile.
- **Owned elsewhere:** the Promo tab folds into Pitch this! (the Pitch This workstream owns the pitch, pitchkit and promo rows), the Home spend card is the Home workstream's, and Notes data is absorbed by the release brief. The release-scoped EPK owner page already redirected to /dashboard/press-kits.
