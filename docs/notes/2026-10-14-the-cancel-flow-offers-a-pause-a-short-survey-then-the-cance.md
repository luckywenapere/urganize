# The cancel flow: offers, a pause, a short survey, then the cancel (2026-10-14, v1.130.0, on by default)

> Moved out of CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-22 (verbatim). "This file", "above", "below" and "the X section" in here refer to the old single CLAUDE.md: find the named section in docs/notes/README.md.

Founder-approved brief: cancelling must stay as easy as signing up (every screen has a plain "Continue to cancel"), minimal words, and a light branded last button. Built from the Churnkey study and three reference screens (Disney+ pause, a one-pick reasons list, MoonPay's "We're sorry to see you go, {name}"). Settings > Billing shows a quiet "Cancel plan" / "Cancel trial" link to the team OWNER only; it opens `/dashboard/cancel` (`components/billing/CancelFlow.tsx`).

- **Rules** (`lib/cancel-flow.ts`, pure, tested in `cancel-flow.test.ts`): `buildCancelSteps` is the order. 1 discount ("10% off this month" = `CANCEL_DISCOUNT_BONUS_DAYS` 3 days, once per team: `paid_bonus_days` for a paying team, `trial_ends_at` for a trial, the share reward's exact mechanism so Paystack is never touched), 2 trial-only invite (one fresh invite through the existing `/api/workspace/members/invite`, then +7 days, once per team, only with a free seat), 3 paid-only pause (2 weeks, 1, 2 or 3 months, from the end of the paid period, at most one per 6 months counted from `subscriptions.paused_at`), 4 the five reasons (exact founder wording and order, one pick, no Other; picking is optional so the button never blocks), 5 one matched follow-up (budget bands + offer, or a short text box; "I'm taking a break" gets none because they just turned the pause down), 6 "Anything else?", 7 the "Drop the mic" button (brand ground, mic tilts on hover and drops on press, `.cancel-mic-button` in globals.css, nothing moves under reduced motion; the button also says "Cancel plan" and carries an aria-label so it is never ambiguous), 8 "Thank you, {first name}" with "Come back anytime" to Settings > Billing.
- **Budget bands** (`budgetBands`): 5/11, 15/22 and 10/11 of the plan's monthly price, rounded to ₦500 / ₦1,000 / ₦5,000 steps by price size. Manager ₦22,000 gives the founder's under ₦10,000 / ₦10,000–₦15,000 / ₦15,000–₦20,000; Solo ₦2,500 / ₦3,500 / ₦4,500; Label ₦25,000 / ₦35,000 / ₦45,000. Offers (`matchBudgetOffer`): the lowest band offers the dearest cheaper plan under the band (Manager to Solo, Label to Manager, Solo none) through the existing change-plan route (a downgrade waits for the renewal); middle and top offer the current plan at the band's top price for 3 months, ONLY when `PAYSTACK_PLAN_<TIER>_RETENTION_<PRICE>` is set (missing = offer hidden). Trial teams see Manager's bands and only the "start on the cheaper plan" offer (to `/locked?intent=reactivate`).
- **A retention price is a founder swap, not an automated one.** Accepting it writes a `retention_offer_accepted` billing event and emails the founder "SWAP NEEDED (retention price)" with the Plan code, the plan-change precedent ("Paystack has no plan-change endpoint"): their subscription keeps running at full price until the founder moves it, and back after 3 months. Nothing reminds anyone at month 3 yet.
- **Paid cancel** (new; before this only card trials could cancel in-app): Paystack `/subscription/disable` via `lib/paystack-subscription-server.ts` (the cancel-trial logic lifted out; `cancel-trial/route.ts` itself is unchanged), status `cancelled`, `expires_at` kept, so access runs to the period end (+ any bonus days) and then the team is read-only through `profiles.paid_at` as before.
- **Cancelled trials are read-only, not walled (founder call).** `workspaces.trial_cancelled_at` is written on a trial cancel; `resolveTeamAccess` puts such a team in `read_only` with reason `trial_cancelled` once the trial (and the server grace) is over, below credits-only and the paid lapse. Never-cancelled unpaid trials still meet the wall. The resolver reads the column on its own tolerant query (`readTrialCancelledAt`, the `readPaidBonusDays` pattern) so a missing column reads as "never cancelled". **Known consequence, worth the founder's eye:** an unpaid trial is now better off cancelling (permanent read-only) than letting the trial run out (the wall). Known gap, same as the share reward's: `lib/admin-team-access.ts` (admin Teams console, email audiences) does not read it, so a cancelled trial shows there as walled.
- **Pause**: disables the Paystack subscription at once (so the period-end renewal never fires), status `cancelled`, and stores `paused_at / paused_from / paused_until / pause_option`. During the pause the team is read-only (the existing paid-lapse path). A pause is in force only while the status is still `cancelled` (`isPauseActive`), so paying again ends it whether or not the sweep has run. **Resume, the safe option:** while the paid period is still running, "Resume now" calls Paystack `/subscription/enable` (its next payment date is still ahead, so it charges on the day it always would have) and sets status `active`. After the period, the app never charges by itself: resume is the normal checkout (`/locked?intent=reactivate`). The daily sweep (`runPauseSweep`, a passenger on `/api/cron/notifications` because Hobby allows two schedules; also its own `/api/cron/subscription-pauses` with `CRON_SECRET`) emails "Your Urganize pause ends soon" 3 days before (`pause_reminder_sent_at`) and on the day clears the window and emails "Your Urganize pause has ended" with a one-tap resume (`team_admin_notice` kinds `pause_ending` / `pause_ended`). Billing shows "Paused until {date}" and Resume now.
- **Route** `app/api/billing/cancel-flow/route.ts` (open route like cancel-trial and change-plan, owner only, every refusal a 4xx with a plain message): GET context (`{ available:false }` for everyone else and whenever a read fails, including before the migration, so Billing keeps the old card-trial control), POST `discount | invite_extension | pause | resume | retention_offer | cancel | event`. Bare `event` posts may not claim money outcomes (`cancelled`, `resumed`, `offer_accepted` except the switch-plan one, which the change-plan route itself executes).
- **Data**: `cancel_flow_events` (flow id, event, offer, reason, budget band, free text capped at 1000, metadata; RLS on, owner reads own rows, only the service role writes), `billing_events` (`cancel_discount_taken`, `trial_invite_extension`, `subscription_paused`, `subscription_resumed`, `retention_offer_accepted`, `subscription_cancelled`), founder emails for cancelled / paused / retention, and the server PostHog event `cancel_flow_event` (never the typed text).
- **Flag** `NEXT_PUBLIC_FEATURE_CANCEL_FLOW`, ON unless `'false'` (founder rule: flags ship on). In turbo.json and .env.example with the six retention Plan vars.
- **Activation checklist**: (1) apply `supabase/migrations/20261014150000_cancel_flow.sql` (safe in either order; until then the flow simply does not offer itself); (2) optionally mint the six monthly retention Plans in Paystack (Solo ₦3,500 / ₦4,500, Manager ₦15,000 / ₦20,000, Label ₦35,000 / ₦45,000) and set `PAYSTACK_PLAN_{SOLO,MANAGER,LABEL}_RETENTION_<price>` in Vercel Web; (3) walk it on our own team: discount, pause + resume in period, cancel, the thank-you screen.
- **Not verified**: nothing has run in a browser, against a real session, database or Paystack. Paystack `subscription/enable` behaviour on a re-enabled non-renewing subscription is from the API docs, not observed. Type-check and unit tests only.
