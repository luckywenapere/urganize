## The TRM fee, locked prices and the margin view (2026-10-22, v1.165.0, no flag)

Built on the self-serve checkout (same day, `2026-10-22-self-serve-checkout.md`): its prices (Solo ₦8,000, Manager ₦30,000, Label ₦60,000), its `semiannual` period and its Paystack Plan vars are untouched here. Founder approved the mocks (`docs/mocks/pricing-trm/index.html` on branch `claude/pricing-trm-distribution-4ed781`, PR #843, which this replaces; their prices are the superseded ones); the migration and anything at Paystack each need his separate yes.

- **Distribution included** (`packages/pricing`: `includesDistribution`, `distributionIncluded`, `distributionHighlight`). Manager, Label and Group say "Distribution included" on the pricing cards; Solo says nothing about it. The old "Distribution through our partner" line on every tier is gone. With `DISTRIBUTION_REQUIRES_PREPAY=true` (default false) the monthly view says "Distribution on 6-month and yearly plans". **Only true once TRM confirms** the wholesale fee (TRM-REQUIREMENTS.md).
- **TRM fee** (`lib/trm/partner-fee.ts`, pure; `lib/trm/partner-ledger-server.ts`, the only writer of `trm_partner_ledger` and `workspaces.trm_registered_at` / `trm_plan`). Manager to TRM Single Artist, Label to TRM Label, Solo and legacy ids never. Fixed yearly fee (₦20,100 / ₦101,000) owed a twelfth per month covered (`monthly_prorata`), or the year upfront (`annual_upfront`), or a percent (`TRM_COST_BASIS=percent` + `TRM_REVENUE_SHARE_PCT`). An unset `TRM_FEE_*` falls back to the default, never zero. First paid period stamps the team registered and sends a REGISTER ON TRM alert (TRM's API has no account creation).
- **One hook for every payment path**: `recordSubscriptionCharge` (`lib/referrals-server.ts`) now records every payment whether or not referrals are on, with `payment_channel`, `billing_interval` and `plan_version`, and writes the TRM fee when the caller names the period and the source is not `plan_change`. Callers: card first charge and renewal (webhook), transfer periods (`applyTransferPeriod`), admin Mark paid (period from `billingInterval` or `intervalCoveringPeriod` on the end date). The period is never guessed from the plan: `resolveBillingInterval` reads a Label row with no interval as yearly (legacy rule).
- **Locked prices**: a new card checkout and a paid transfer period write `subscriptions.price_locked_kobo` and `plan_version` (`PRICE_LIST_VERSION`); `applyRenewal` never touches them (a test reads its body).
- **Distribute step** (`lib/distribution-entitlement.ts`, `/api/billing/distribution`, `DistributionPlanCard`): included / needs_prepay / not on plan. needs_prepay sends a transfer team to `/checkout?...&period=semiannual&method=transfer`; a card team gets "Ask us to switch you" (self-serve checkout keeps a card plan's period, and plan-change refuses interval changes). No in-app period switch was built.
- **Admin `/admin/billing`** (`lib/billing-margin.ts` pure, `lib/billing-margin-server.ts` reads, internal accounts left out): per paying team: plan, period, paid by (latest charge, else `workspaces.payment_method`), came in (`acquisition_path`), cash per month, estimated Paystack fee, TRM owed, credits x `CREDIT_COST_KOBO`, contribution; totals and break-even from `FIXED_COSTS_MONTHLY_KOBO`. TRM CSV per month at `/api/admin/billing/trm-ledger?month=YYYY-MM`.
- **Scoreboard fix**: `monthlyAmountMinor` counted a 6-month payment as one month's MRR; it now uses `monthlyEquivalentKobo`.
- **Migration** `20261022170000_trm_fee_and_price_lock.sql`: additive, backup and rollback in its header. Its billing-fields trigger is the LIVE body read from production plus the two TRM columns (an earlier draft would have dropped the self-serve protections). **Applied to production 2026-10-22** after backups (`subscriptions_backup_20261022b`, `subscription_charges_backup_20261022b`, `workspace_billing_fn_backup_20261022b`); existing rows locked at their amount as `legacy`. Code still tolerates it missing.
- **Not verified**: no real checkout, webhook, ledger row or CSV against a real database; the in-app screens only in the mocks.
