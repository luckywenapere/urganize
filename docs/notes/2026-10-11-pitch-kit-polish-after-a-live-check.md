# Pitch kit polish after a live check (2026-10-11, v1.116.0)

> Moved out of CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-22 (verbatim). "This file", "above", "below" and "the X section" in here refer to the old single CLAUDE.md: find the named section in docs/notes/README.md.

A read-only walkthrough of production found rough edges in v1.108.0's simple pitch kit. Fixed: RolloutTabs wraps its places in a ScrollRow that fades an edge and shows an arrow button when tabs hide past it (the pitch kit tab was off-screen with no hint); the pitch kit progress is three segments on a visible track (bg-content-tertiary/25) instead of a bar whose empty track vanished; the credits cost line is text-sm secondary; the story save-status row collapses when empty (empty:hidden) so step spacing is even; PublishPanel's Turn off the link sits behind a divider; a kit on the legacy 'public' visibility shows it as "Only people I send it to" and hides link_only (same behaviour, so never both, no "(old setting)" label); RolloutBanner's hand-written notice is secondary text, not warning colour. No data, route or action changes. Status words beside press kit marks are sr-only on purpose; don't remove them.

Same release, from a founder screenshot: the activity pop-up opened with its title and checkbox half under the sticky Delete/Close bar. TaskUIProvider now resets the scroll body to the top on open, per activity and when loading ends (useLayoutEffect + one rAF), with overflow-anchor: none; TaskDetailView's title auto-grow re-fits on width change and after document.fonts.ready and pins its own scrollTop to 0. Also dropped the internal "(Artist + Manager in v0.3)" note from five single-template activity descriptions (new rollouts only; existing tasks keep their stored text).

v1.119.1 correction: the real cause of the hidden activity title was the sticky Delete/Close bar using top-0 inside a padded scroll body. Browsers stick at the padding edge, so the bar sat 24px down over the title. Use -top-5 sm:-top-6 (matching p-5 sm:p-6) for any sticky header inside a padded scroller.
