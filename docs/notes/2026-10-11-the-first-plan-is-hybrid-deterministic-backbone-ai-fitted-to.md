# The first plan is hybrid: deterministic backbone + AI fitted to the user's answers (2026-10-11, v1.107.0, founder-approved, no flag)

> Moved out of CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-22 (verbatim). "This file", "above", "below" and "the X section" in here refer to the old single CLAUDE.md: find the named section in docs/notes/README.md.

Two changes, shipped together, to the onboarding-to-first-plan path that v1.105.0 built.

### A. "What's already done?" is asked once, in onboarding

Onboarding's Step 3 ("Where is this rollout in its lifecycle?", six stage radios) and the planner's "What's already done?" asked the same thing two screens apart. Onboarding now asks the planner's question with the planner's own options and values (`READY_TODAY_OPTIONS` / `ReadyItem`: mixed and mastered, cover art, video, distributor, none yet; "None of it yet" is exclusive), so `isTaskPreCompletedByReady` and the presets work on it unchanged. `lib/onboarding-readiness.ts` (pure, tested) holds the toggle, the normalizer and `deriveOnboardingStage`.

- **The stage is derived, not asked.** `profiles.onboarding_stage` is still written (the admin onboarding tallies read it) and "Your first move" (`lib/onboarding-ai-moment.ts`) still reads `draft.stage`, so the draft keeps a `stage` computed from `readyToday`: none → `just_starting`; not mastered → `recorded`; mastered + art + distributor → `date_set`; otherwise `preparing`. **`promoting` and `post_release` can no longer come out of onboarding**: nothing in a readiness checklist says a release is already being promoted, both only changed which single suggestion "Your first move" showed, and the planner plans ahead of a date anyway. A compact stage question was not kept because nothing essential depends on those two values.
- **Hand-off:** `markOnboardingPlannerHandoff` carries `readyToday`; `WorkspaceView` passes it as `prefill.readyToday`; `decideIntakeSkips` skips the ready step when it holds at least one valid answer ("None of it yet" counts), and the modal uses it as the answer. An onboarded user's planner asks three questions: what matters most, budget, what to cover. Every other entry point passes no readiness and keeps the question. The onboarding step count (7) and progress are unchanged: the step's content changed, not its slot.

### B. The first plan is hybrid

**What the investigation found first.** Both doors (`runUrgansAction` → `release.draft_plan` → `createReleasePlan`, and `POST /api/releases/[id]/plan` → `createReleasePlan`) run the same pipeline; the first-plan rule (`lib/credits/first-plan.ts`) only skipped the credit hold and charge, so the AI layer DID run for the first plan whenever `AI_API_KEY` was set. Production rows (`release_plan_drafts.doc.ai`, last 30 days, read 2026-10-11) show the tailoring applied on 6 of 11 plans; 3 were thrown away whole as `invalid_output` (the all-or-nothing validator refusing a response over one bad item), and both plans built on 2026-10-11 (the first onboarding plans after v1.105.0) failed `unavailable` because the gateway answered 5xx for both `openai/gpt-5.4` and the router's fallback (`ai_usage_events`). The prompt was ~10k tokens for a 64-task single (pretty-printed JSON). Budget unchanged: 40 s per attempt, 2 retries, 52 s deadline, inside the 60 s route ceiling.

**What is deterministic (the backbone, unchanged guarantees, holds with no model):** the playbook for the release type, every date computed from the release date, readiness pre-completion, the user's cover/exclusion choices and "no budget rules out paid ads", presets, release-day and distribution work that no scope or drop can remove, the owner-history assignments, and the schedule warnings. This plan is built and saved whatever the model does.

**What the AI layer does (`PlannerAiDelta`, every item checked by `sanitizePlannerDelta`):** exclude scopes the notes rule out, drop non-essential optional tasks, re-time (`adjust_offsets`), assign roles, rewrite why lines in terms of the user's goal and situation, mark at most 5 `focus` tasks (they land as priority `high`, `metadata.planner.focus`), and **add at most 5 tasks** (`add_tasks`). The prompt now carries every selection: type, title, artist, date and days out, what is done, goal (+ their words), budget, what the plan covers, notes, plus the onboarding answers read from the profile (`onboarding_where_stuck` labels, `onboarding_planning_preference` label; `readPlannerOnboardingContext`, best effort). Task JSON is compact and why lines are trimmed to 140 chars in the prompt.

**Invariants the gate enforces (each item refused on its own, the rest of the delta stands; the count is stored as `doc.ai.rejected_items`):**
- ids must be ones we sent; a required task may only be dropped inside a left-out scope; a task tagged `release_day` or `distribution` is never dropped, optional or not;
- offsets stay in -120..60, and a move may not push work that was still ahead of today into the past;
- adds: title 4–80 chars, why 1–160, offset inside the playbook's own date range and not before today, role from `PLANNER_ROLE_VALUES` or null, tags from `AI_ADD_TASK_TAGS` (the playbook vocabulary minus the keep tags), nothing tagged with a left-out scope, nothing paid (tag or wording) on no budget, no adds at all on a preset, no near-duplicate of a playbook title or another add (`isDuplicateTaskTitle`), at most 5;
- added tasks are `origin: 'ai'`, optional, one day long, no dependencies, owned by the role's assigned member or the owner, and become normal editable rows with `metadata.planner.origin = 'ai'`.

`validatePlannerDelta` still exists as the yes/no form (ok only when nothing would be refused) for callers that want it; the pipeline sanitizes.

**Fallback:** a model error, timeout or unparseable answer leaves the backbone, with `doc.ai.failure` recorded. **A team's first plan is never refused** (`createReleasePlan({ firstPlan })`, computed by both doors from the same facts as the free-plan rule): with typed notes it is built anyway with "UrgAns could not read your notes this time…". Later plans keep the 2026-09-12 rule (typed notes + a one-off failure → 503 `tailoring_unavailable`, nothing written). Credits unchanged: the first plan is free, others are metered as before. Model and provider unchanged (`lib/ai/planner-model.ts`, routed).

**Surfacing:** `PlanBuiltBanner` shows "Shaped for: reach new listeners · under ₦100k" when the tailoring ran, a "Start with:" line naming the focus tasks, and "Added by UrgAns (n)" with a small UrgAns mark per added task. No new pop-ups. Task rows themselves carry no new mark yet (only `priority: high` for focus and the metadata origin).

**Tests:** `lib/hybrid-first-plan.test.ts`, `lib/release-planning-actions-server.pipeline.test.ts` (the whole pipeline against a fake database and model: backbone on error, first plan with notes, onboarding answers reach the prompt, per-item refusal), `lib/onboarding-readiness.test.ts`, plus updates to `release-planning.test.ts`, `planner-intake-steps.test.ts`, `onboarding-draft.test.ts`. **Not verified against the live model or in a browser**: whether the model actually uses `add_tasks`/`focus` well, and the onboarding walk, rest on unit tests and the type-check.
