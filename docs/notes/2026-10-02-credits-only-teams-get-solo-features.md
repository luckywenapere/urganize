# Credits-only teams get Solo features (2026-10-02)

> Moved out of CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-22 (verbatim). "This file", "above", "below" and "the X section" in here refer to the old single CLAUDE.md: find the named section in docs/notes/README.md.

The strategy doc (section 8): "Credits only | None | None | ₦30 a credit | Anyone who wants to pay by use. Solo features." Until this, a no-plan team could use UrgAns on credits and nothing else ("Credits-only access" above). Now it gets the whole app, under Solo's limits. No migration, no new flag: it rides `NEXT_PUBLIC_FEATURE_URGANS_CREDITS`, which is on.

**Founder call: only while the team has credits.** At zero the team falls back to the wall (or read-only, if it paid before), and the UrgAns gate still lets it in to buy more.

- **A new access state, `credits_only`** (`lib/team-billing.ts`): `hasAccess` and `canWrite` true, reason `credits_funded`. `resolveTeamAccess` returns it only where the answer would otherwise be read-only or the wall, so a paid, comped, trialing or grace team is never narrowed to Solo. It is checked BEFORE read-only, so a lapsed plan with credits keeps writing (the doc's "drops to Solo features as a credits-only account").
- **Solo limits:** `resolveTeamTier` maps `credits_only` to `solo` first, ahead of the founding deal and every fail-open branch. One seat, one artist, one rollout at a time, UrgAns as chat only. Members and rollouts already over the limits stay; new ones are refused, as for any downgrade.
- **What counts as funded: monthly and bought credits, never trial credits** (`isFundedForAccess`). Trial leftovers are removed lazily when a trial ends with nothing bought, so counting them would open the app to every expired trial until that ran. **An unreadable balance is NOT funded**: the one counter here that fails closed, because the open way gives the app away to every walled team for as long as the read fails, and closed is exactly the behaviour before this existed.
- **The server reads a balance only for a team that would otherwise be walled or read-only** (`couldBeCreditsOnly`), once per such team, in parallel, inside `getTeamAccessForUser` and `getWorkspaceTier`. A team on a plan or a trial pays nothing extra. The answer is cached 30s like every access answer that grants access, so a team that spends its last credit can keep working for up to 30s.
- **The known gap, accepted by the founder:** credits are only spent on AI, so a team that buys a pack and never uses UrgAns keeps Solo features indefinitely. Watch for it on the scoreboard; a small daily charge is the fix if it matters.
- **Settings → Billing** says "Using credits" for such a team, with "Pick a plan" for the owner (`reactivate`, since the team has access and a bare `/locked` would bounce). **`/admin/teams`** shows "Credits only" with "While credits last" in place of a wall date, and has a filter for it. The scoreboard and the email audiences are unchanged: they read such a team as walled or read-only, as before, and it is not a paying team.
- Three deliberate breaks (counting trial credits, dropping the state, leaving it on Label's limits) were each caught by a named test. Not verified against a real database or session: no team has been put into this state for real.
