# Home spend card, a bare top bar, the phone's rollout menu sheet and a messenger chat (2026-10-11, v1.112.0, founder)

> Moved out of CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-22 (verbatim). "This file", "above", "below" and "the X section" in here refer to the old single CLAUDE.md: find the named section in docs/notes/README.md.

No flag (ships on). Not verified in a browser or on a device: type-check and unit tests only.
- **Where the money went is actionable.** Tapping the card opens a rollout's Spend scene (`/dashboard?release=<id>&scene=spend`); "Add spend" adds `field=add`, which `ReleaseScenePanel` turns into `SpendSection startAdding` (the form opens on arrival). Which rollouts: Home's in-flight rollouts whose spend the person may see, asked through `useSpendAccessStore` (the same probe the menu's Track spend row uses), first 8, ordered by `orderSpendCardRollouts` (`lib/home/spend-card.ts`: next release day ahead first, then most recently out, then undated). Several: a native picker on the card. `orderHomeBlocks` input `hasSpend` is now `showSpend`: a manager with spend OR a rollout to track it on, so the card shows empty (`SPEND_COPY.homeEmpty`) with Add spend. Spend only on rollouts no longer in flight leaves the card display-only. Track spend in the rollout menu is unchanged.
- **The top bar lost its left block on every screen**: no Urganize mark, no team/artist label (`ArtistSwitcher` deleted). That label was the ARTIST view filter (Home and Catalogue read `useShellStore.selectedArtistId`), not a rollout switcher; it now lives in the account menu on a desktop and in More on a phone (`components/shell/useArtistFilter.ts`; `useArtistFilterSync` in TopNav keeps the roster and saved choice loaded). Desktop: pills left, icons right. Phone (below lg): search only, in a 56px row plus the safe-area top; credits, the bell button and the account menu are hidden there. The bell stays MOUNTED on a phone (it runs the realtime toasts) and More's Notifications row opens its panel through `stores/useNotificationsDoorStore.ts`, which also gives More an unread dot. On `/dashboard/urgans` the phone has no row at all, only the safe-area padding, and `.chappi-shell-height-nav` plus the keyboard-open pin in globals.css were re-derived without the old 72px.
- **Phone rollout menu** (`TasksPanel`): a portalled bottom sheet (`RolloutMenuSheet`, z-[170] over the bottom bar, scrim, close button, Escape, max height = screen minus safe areas, own scroll, opens scrolled to the bottom since rows are drawn `orderForThumb`). The pill is hidden on the Chat scene.
- **Rollout chat** (`ReleaseChatSection`): a fixed-height box measured by `usePinnedChatHeight` (ends above `.mobile-nav-bar`, the keyboard via `useKeyboardViewport`, or the desktop scroll column), list is the only scroller and jumps to the newest message, composer pinned under it; Send is 44px.
- **Pitch**: subtitle gone, 20px rhythm (title, chips, grid), platform chips 44px. Song link placeholder is `SONG_LINK_PLACEHOLDER` / `_SHORT` in `lib/pitching.ts` (short below 640px). `isValidSongUrl` already took any http(s) link; tests now pin SoundCloud secret-token, on.soundcloud.com and untitled.stream links.
