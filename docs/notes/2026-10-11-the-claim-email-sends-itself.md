# The claim email sends itself (2026-10-11, v1.115.0, founder switched it on Oct 11, 2026)

> Moved out of CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-22 (verbatim). "This file", "above", "below" and "the X section" in here refer to the old single CLAUDE.md: find the named section in docs/notes/README.md.

Founder ask, through the Credits -> Claim loop: a credited collaborator should get their claim link without the owner pressing "Email" for each person. Behind `NEXT_PUBLIC_FEATURE_CLAIM_AUTO_EMAIL` (default off), which **also needs `NEXT_PUBLIC_FEATURE_METADATA`**, because the credits panel is where the owner sees that it went out. One migration, `20261011140000_claim_auto_email.sql`, **not applied anywhere yet**. No changelog entry or version bump: they land at the flip (the ledger is the record of when a flag was flipped). This deliberately reverses Lane A's "the invite email is owner-initiated, never automatic" once the flag is on, so flipping it is a founder call.

**What it does.** A passenger on `/api/cron/due-date-notifications`, right after the unclaimed-profile sweep (`runClaimAutoEmailSweep` in `lib/claim-auto-email-server.ts`, never throws). For each person with an unclaimed page, an attested, visible credit with an email, on a rollout that counts as delivered (`deliveredReleaseIds`, now exported from `lib/claim-sweep-server.ts` so the rule has one home: `lib/recap-eligibility.ts`, not archived, not a test rollout), it makes a claim link and emails it ONCE. Seven days after that email went out, if the page is still unclaimed and nobody else made a link, it sends ONE reminder with a fresh link. Never more.

**Rules worth not undoing.**
- **Once is a row.** `person_claim_emails` is unique on `(person_id, kind)`, and the row is inserted BEFORE the link is made or the email sent, so two runs at once lose the insert and send nothing (a test runs two sweeps in parallel). Per PERSON, not per credit: three credits, one email. The newest ready credit is the one named.
- **Only a send that certainly reached nobody is tried again**: email not configured, or Resend's 429. A timeout or a 5xx is final (status `failed`), because it may have gone out and "never twice" outranks "always once". The owner can still send by hand. An unsubscribed or bounced address is `suppressed`, final. A row stuck at `sending` is never retried.
- **Never** to someone who asked to be taken off, has claimed, has an account on the page, or is a founder-seeded profile, and **never once anybody made a link by hand** (owner Email or Share, another team, the founder). Urganize's own links have a team and no creator (`created_by` null), which is how the rules tell them apart. An owner link made after the first email stops the reminder. A used, turned-off or locked link stops the reminder; an open or run-out one gets a new link (the token is never stored, so a reminder always carries a new link, and making it turns the first one off: one live link at a time. The reminder says "We made you a new link, so use this one.").
- **One path for the link and the email.** `mintClaimLink` and `emailClaimLink` in `lib/claim-owner-server.ts` are used by the owner's button and by the automatic step. `claim-guard.test.ts` holds every claim template to being sent from that file only, holds the automatic step to never calling `sendEmail` itself, to checking the flag before anything else, and to reserving before it mints or sends. `person_claim_emails` is written only by `lib/claim-auto-email-server.ts`.
- **Its own email category, `claim`**, unsubscribable on its own (these people never signed up). That is half of the migration: `email_log.category` and `email_suppressions.scope` gain it (the `chat` precedent). The owner's own "Email" stays `transactional`. Templates `claim_auto_invite` and `claim_reminder`, one component (`ClaimPageEmail.tsx`); every word is in `lib/claim-email-copy.ts` ("Tems' team credited you as producer on Free Mind. Your page is ready.", one "Claim your page" button, one line on what the page does for them). The plain-text part is rendered from the same component and carries the link.
- **Inside the day's email budget.** The Resend free plan is 100 a day for everything. The step keeps back `BULK_RESERVE` (25) for everyday mail, sends at most 25 a run, and sends nothing when today's count cannot be read. Left-over people wait for tomorrow (`deferred` in the cron's JSON).
- **The owner sees it.** `listCreditClaims` adds `autoEmail` (`invitedAt`, `remindedAt`) and `invite.automatic`. The panel says "We emailed Ada their link on 3 Oct" (and "and reminded them on 10 Oct"), adds "No need to send it yourself. If they do not claim it, we send one reminder a week later.", and the owner's button reads "Email Ada again".
- PostHog: `claim_email_sent` and `claim_reminder_sent`, server-side, against the rollout owner (the collaborator has no account), with no name, email or role text.
