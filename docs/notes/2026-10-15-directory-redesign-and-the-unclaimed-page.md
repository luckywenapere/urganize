# Directory redesign and the Unclaimed page (2026-10-15, URG-304, v1.145.0, no new flag)

> Moved out of CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-22 (verbatim). "This file", "above", "below" and "the X section" in here refer to the old single CLAUDE.md: find the named section in docs/notes/README.md.

Relationships rebuild, approved mocks (bare-minimum text, app dark tokens, Young Serif names, Space Mono meta).

- **Directory** (`/dashboard/directory`): one Search field, four chips (All, Managers, A&R, PR; `lib/directory-chips.ts`), one Rising shelf of faces, then compact rows: face, name + tick, role · city. Managers is the profile TYPE (a manager's own work is never a delivery record); A&R and PR are roles. A role a link brings in (role gaps, `?role=`) shows as one extra clearable chip; `?availability=` still applies. The tick means at least one delivered record, never a self-typed role. A row opens `/p/{slug}` when public, otherwise the work request sheet when the person can be asked. The old helper text, the dropdown filters (type, city, open to) and the "most recently updated" note are gone. Invites moved under the list.
- **Rising shelf** (`DirectoryShelves`): Featured then On the rise, merged into one row, each person once (`risingShelf`), no heading, default view only, nothing drawn when empty.
- **Unclaimed rows**: `GET /api/directory` also returns `unclaimed` from `lib/directory-unclaimed-server.ts`: unclaimed, slugged, not removed or asked to be, AND at least one attested, visible credit. That is narrower than what `/p/{slug}` already serves, and the credit rule keeps consent-pending seeded and Directory-invite profiles out. Only name, handle and credited role leave the server. They show under All or a typed name, never under a role, type, city or availability filter.
- **Unclaimed page** (`UnclaimedProfileView`): dashed monogram, name + Unclaimed pill, credited role, Claim this profile, Invite <first name> (share sheet, else WhatsApp, sharing the page), small Request removal (the existing form; its link and the claim email now say "Request removal").
- **Claim this profile** hands off to the existing `/claim/{token}`. Signed out it goes through `/auth?next=/p/{slug}`. Signed in, `POST /api/claim-start` mints a link (`mintClaimLink`, no team, earlier links kept) ONLY when the account's confirmed email is an email identifier on that profile; anyone else gets 403 "Sign in with the email on your credits." A typed email is never enough here, because no team sent this link.
