# The side menu comes back; the top bar calms down (2026-10-14, URG-254/255, v1.126.0, no flag)

> Moved out of CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-22 (verbatim). "This file", "above", "below" and "the X section" in here refer to the old single CLAUDE.md: find the named section in docs/notes/README.md.

Design review with Femi (2026-10-13): the top pills, the tab row and the page were three stacked horizontal layers, and the top bar's credits chip, `?` button, bell and search all competed for the eye. Live at merge, no flag (the overhaul's rule). Two earlier cuts (areas down the side; then areas down the side with the tabs in the top bar) were rejected by the founder the same day: "home, catalogue etc still at the top", "i even liked the central layout of the top menu". Do not move the areas into the side menu again.

- **The top bar is the overhaul's bar** (`TopNav.tsx`): the mark left, the six area pills truly centred (the `1fr auto 1fr` grid), and on the right only search, a plain outline bell with an unread dot, and the account menu. CreditsChip and the `?` HelpMenu button are deleted.
- **The side menu is one level down** (`components/shell/Sidebar.tsx`, `lg` and up): the open area's pages as parent rows (Catalogue: Rollouts, Artists, Press kits, Notes; Relationships: People, Directory, Shows), from `shellSideMenu` in `lib/shell-nav.ts` (tested in `lib/shell-sidebar.test.ts`). An area with one page (Home, Pitching) has no side menu and the page takes the width; Catalogue always has one, because an open rollout's menu nests under Rollouts. The rollout screen draws that menu itself: `WorkspaceDesktopFocused` portals `RolloutTabs variant="side"` into `SIDEBAR_SLOT_ROLLOUT` (`useSidebarSlot`), so every scene, the Notes link and the three dialogs work as before; with no slot on screen it falls back to the old row of tabs. A rollout is the only page with pages inside it today; a future one nests the same way (a slot under its parent row).
- **The page bar** (`PageBar.tsx`, `lg` and up, inside `<main>`): the side-menu fold button, the page's name (`shellPageTitle`) and the page's own slots (`SUBNAV_SLOT_START/TOOLS/END`, names kept so no page changed). On an area without a side menu it shows only when a page put tools in it. It is `h-14`, the old tab row's height, so the focused rollout's viewport-locked `calc(100dvh-10.5rem)` is unchanged. The tab row (`SubNav`) is `lg:hidden`.
- **Folding** the side menu hides it entirely (the rollout's menu goes back to tabs), remembered per device in `useSidebarStore` (the pre-overhaul store; `SIDEBAR_COLLAPSED_VERSION` went to 2 so an old menu's choice does not fold this one).
- **The account menu** (`WorkspaceMenu`) holds the credit balance as one row to `/dashboard/credits` (warning tone when low, a warning dot on the avatar only then, nothing when credits are unknown or off) and the help rows (`useHelpMenuGroups` in `HelpMenu.tsx`), behind AppShell's `showHelpMenu`.
- **Phone unchanged.** One tablet change: below `lg` the rollout count and Add rollout stay on the Catalogue card (the slots are desktop only now), where at `md`–`lg` they used to sit in the tab row.
- **Verified:** tsc clean, lint no new findings, vitest, and screenshots at 375, 1024 and 1440 (side menu open and folded) with no horizontal scroll, from a local dev server with a stubbed session (pages show their loading state). **Not verified:** an open rollout's menu in the side menu (needs a real account's data), and both themes.
