# Home becomes the chat on a computer; stacks; sessions (2026-10-07, v1.88.0, no flag)

> Moved out of CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-22 (verbatim). "This file", "above", "below" and "the X section" in here refer to the old single CLAUDE.md: find the named section in docs/notes/README.md.

Founder brief from the Hark app screenshots, decided through four rounds of questions. Plan: `C:/Users/penta/.claude/plans/the-home-screen-on-wiggly-aho.md`. Live at merge, no flag (the overhaul's rule).

- **Stacks.** Every group on Home with more than one item is a stack (`components/home/HomeStack.tsx`): the first item in full, up to two card edges peeking behind it, and a chip on the top edge ("Tasks · 5") that opens and closes it. The peeking edges also open it. One item: no peeks, no toggle. Empty groups are not drawn. Stacks start closed every visit. Pure rules: `lib/home/stack.ts`, `lib/home/sections.ts` (order, empty drops, Up next in the tasks slot when there are no tasks, the daily line only when nothing is late), `lib/home/stack-items.ts`. "Do this first" is the top card of the tasks stack. `HomeBlocks` renders the order for both the quiet and the busy Home. Deleted: BriefCard, HomeSectionShell, the six section files, `.home-two-col`.
- **Phone: the pinned box** (`HomeAskBar`, phone only). A real field pinned above the bottom bar on every Home state, with + (attach) and the mic. It is portalled to `<body>` because `.app-layout`'s transform anchors a fixed element to the page, not the screen. The text lives in `useChappiStore.input`. Send hands the message over with `setSendInputOnOpen(true)` and slides into the full chat page. It publishes `--home-dock-height` and `data-home-dock` on `<html>`, so `.home-main-padding` and the toasts clear it. While the keyboard is open the box rides on it and the bottom bar hides **on every page** (`:root[data-keyboard-open='true'] .mobile-nav-bar`). The field is `text-[16px]` (`text-base` is 14px here and would zoom iOS).
- **Computer: Home is the chat** (`HomeDesktopSplit`, mounted only at lg+ via `useIsDesktopViewport`, so the conversation is never in the page twice). Home's column on the left (26rem, 28rem at xl), the conversation on the right; the page never scrolls (`.home-split-height`). "+ New session" sits right after the last stack, not pinned. With history on, a "Chat | Sessions" switch swaps the right side to the sessions list (`useHomeChatStore.pane`, put back to Chat when Home is left; a module counter survives React's development double mount). The conversation stays mounted, hidden, while Sessions shows. The right side carries `data-tour="nav-urgans"`.
- **The full chat page is a phone's only.** On a computer `/dashboard/urgans` replaces itself with Home and shows Home's skeleton while it goes. The Today panel, `useChatSideStore` and `lib/chat-side.ts` are deleted. The drawer's expand closes the drawer and opens Home. The top bar's More item is "UrgAns sessions" (Home, Sessions pane; dropped with history off). The search bar's "Ask UrgAns" goes through `lib/ask-urgans.ts`: on Home it fills the box and asks for the cursor (80ms later, after the bar hands focus back to its opener); elsewhere the drawer on a computer, the chat page on a phone.
- **`ChappiConversation`** gained `headingLevel` (h2 on Home, whose greeting is the h1) and `focusRequest`. Its ten-second auto-focus now checks `mayAutoFocusComposer` (`lib/composer-focus.ts`): never over an open dialog, never from a control in use on the page, and it stops at the first click or key.
- **Making a rollout** from Home and from the chat page is one component, `components/releases/RolloutCreateFlow.tsx` (type-first create, team-login step, share offer, first-win, test rollout). Home's copy had fallen behind.
- **"Session" is copy only.** `CHAT_TITLE_FALLBACK` ('New chat') is a stored marker and keeps its value; `displayChatTitle` shows it as "New session". Identifiers, routes, storage keys and the rollout team chat are untouched.
- **Guards:** `home-ask-bar-guard`, `home-layout-guard` (rewritten), the new `home-desktop-chat-guard`, and the new Home files on the rollout-vocabulary list.
- **The task tick circle uses the control outline now** (`TaskCompletionCheckbox`, the clickable state only: `border-stroke-control`, darker on hover). It was a 6% hairline and vanished on Home's cards, which are the same colour as the circle. This reaches every task row in the app. Locked and waiting-to-sync circles stay faint, since they cannot be clicked.
- **The phone box renders only after hydration** (`useSyncExternalStore`, false on the server), so its portal can never make the server and browser disagree, whatever page mounts it.
- **Not built:** stage panels (no plan yet, release day, after release) are the follow-up; no rollout picker in the phone box.
- **Verified:** 6,715 tests, `tsc` (only the 8 known `confirmed-action.test.ts` errors), lint (nothing new), `next build` with `/auth`, `/dashboard`, `/dashboard/home`, `/dashboard/urgans`, `/onboarding` and `/settings` still static. Looked at in the Browser pane through a throwaway page with made-up data (deleted), with voice and history switched on locally to match production. On a phone (375×812), the box is pinned, the field is 16px, and +, mic and send all fit with 210px left for typing. A simulated keyboard lifts the box to 8px above it. Send fades Home and opens the chat page. On a computer (1440×900 and 1024×768), there is no sideways overflow and the split fills the screen exactly. The Chat | Sessions switch, + New session and the "Ask UrgAns" focus request all put the cursor in the box. A stack opens in place.
- **Not verified:** nothing with a real session (localhost was not signed in), so the real top bar, a real reply arriving on Home, the tour's highlight, the create modal from the chat, and a phone's real keyboard. Worth one pass on the preview: send from Home on a computer, switch to Sessions mid-reply and back, open a saved session, and on a real iPhone and Android type in the box with the keyboard up. A banner strip above the top bar (trial ending, offline) makes the computer page scroll by its own height, the same as the chat page always has.
