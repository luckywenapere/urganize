# Profile photos for artists and directory people (2026-10-11, v1.111.0, founder)

> Moved out of CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-22 (verbatim). "This file", "above", "below" and "the X section" in here refer to the old single CLAUDE.md: find the named section in docs/notes/README.md.

Founder ask: "a slot and option for profile photos" on the Artists list and in the directory. No flag (ships on).
- **Artists:** `artists.photo_path` (`20261011170000_artist_photo.sql`, applied to prod 2026-10-11). Upload: `POST /api/workspace/artists/[artistId]/photo` signs a PUT under `artist/<id>/photo/` in release-assets; `PATCH` same route `{ photoPath | null }` saves (prefix-checked, roster-authorized like rename/brand colors) and drops the old object. `GET /api/workspace/artists` now carries `photoUrl` (signed per response, own tolerant query in `lib/profile-photo-server.ts`). Faces on the Artists list (40px, picker laid OVER the row link as a sibling, never inside it) and the artist page header (72px, Change/Remove).
- **Directory people:** reuses the long-dormant `person_profiles.avatar_storage_path`. `POST /api/directory/profile/photo` signs under `person/<user_id>/avatar/`; `PATCH /api/directory/profile` takes `photoPath`. Directory list and shelves return `avatarUrl`; cards lead with a 48px face; Settings > Your directory record has the picker.
- **Rule change in `lib/profile-kit.ts`:** photo is the one field where the person's OWN upload beats the published press kit (a kit's first shot is often a wide press photo); kit photo is the fallback. Tested in profile-kit.test.ts.
- Shared pieces: `components/shared/ProfileFace.tsx` (was pitching's CuratorFace, which now re-exports it), `components/shared/ProfilePhotoPicker.tsx`, `lib/profile-photo.ts` (+test), `lib/profile-photo-client.ts`. Account deletion collects both photo kinds. Analytics: `profile_photo_saved`, upload failures under surfaces `artist_photo` / `person_photo`.
- **Follow-up v1.113.0 (same day, founder "do the things in not built"):** crop step before upload (`components/shared/PhotoCropDialog.tsx`, maths in `lib/photo-crop.ts` + test: drag, zoom 1-4x, clamped so no empty corners, square JPEG <=1024px); artist faces in the account-menu / More-sheet "Showing" artist filter (`useArtistFilterOptions` now carries `photoUrl`), the press kits list, the press kit editor header (editable picker), the public `/k/{slug}` header (`PublicPressKit.artistPhotoUrl`), and rollout headers desktop + phone (`components/shared/ArtistFace.tsx`, reads the roster store by artist id).
