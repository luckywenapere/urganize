# Directory referral invites (2026-10-11, v1.119.0, GitHub #834, no flag)

> Moved out of CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-22 (verbatim). "This file", "above", "below" and "the X section" in here refer to the old single CLAUDE.md: find the named section in docs/notes/README.md.

From the Oct 11 demo: a manager offered to introduce his mix engineers, video directors and producers once he had evaluated Urganize. Any signed-in team member can now invite the people they work with into the Directory from the Directory screen. **No new feature flag** (the founder's standing "always ship them on"); it sits behind the Directory's own `NEXT_PUBLIC_FEATURE_DIRECTORY`, which is on. One migration, `20261011210000_directory_invites.sql`, **not applied to production when this shipped**: until it is, `GET /api/directory/invites` answers `{ ready: false }`, the invite row is not drawn, a POST answers 503 "Invites are coming soon." and nothing crashes.

**It is founder seeding run by a team member, not a second claim flow.** An invite makes the same three things `/admin/seeding` makes: a consent-pending `person_profiles` row (unclaimed, private, `roles: [role]`, the name the manager typed), a `person_claim_invites` link through `lib/seeding-server.ts`'s `mintClaimLink` (now with optional `workspaceId`, `stampSeed: false` and `revokeEarlier`), and the invitee lands on `/welcome/{token}`, which already does sign up or sign in, pre-filled name and role, edit, and publish-only-if-they-say-so. The inviter's record is the new `directory_invites` table (the per-user `seeded_profiles`), RLS on with no policies, written only by `lib/directory-invites-server.ts` (create, list) and `lib/directory-invites-claim-server.ts` (hooks the welcome flow calls). A guard test holds both.

- **Where:** a row at the top of `/dashboard/directory` ("Invite your people", `components/directory/DirectoryInvites.tsx`), above the filters, on phone and desktop. "Invite" opens a portalled sheet: Name, What they do (the Directory's 16 roles, default Producer), Email (optional). With an email the button is "Send invite", without it "Get link". After: the link, Copy, WhatsApp (`wa.me/?text=` with `directoryInviteShareText`, in the manager's voice, private-first like the seeding message), "Invite someone else". Below the row, a fold "N invited · M joined" lists each invite with its status: Invited, Opened, Joined, Listed (`directoryInviteStatus`).
- **The welcome page names the inviter.** `openClaimLink` returns `invitedBy` (the inviter's `profiles.name`) when a `directory_invites` row points at the profile; the page says "{name} invited you to the Urganize Directory." and expired / replaced links say "Ask {name} for a new one". Founder seeding links find no row and still say Wena.
- **Statuses are stamped by the existing flow.** `recordClaimOpened` (the browser's report, never the render) stamps `opened_at`; `claimProfile` stamps `joined_at` + `joined_user_id`, and `published_at` when they publish. Every invite pointing at the profile is stamped, so two managers who invited the same person both see it.
- **People Urganize already knows** (email in `person_identifiers`): a claimed person is refused ("They are already on Urganize. Search for them above."); somebody who asked to be taken off is refused; an unclaimed page (a credit profile, or another manager's invite) is reused and its earlier links are NOT revoked. A link-only invite has no email to check, so an existing user who opens one hits seeding's "You already have a profile" refusal; their invite stays "Opened".
- **Email:** template `directory_invite` (`DirectoryInviteEmail.tsx`), category `transactional` (the manager typed the address and pressed Send, the owner claim email's precedent), from "{inviter} via Urganize", idempotency key `directory-invite:<id>`. Sent only when asked; a failed or unconfigured send still hands back the link.
- **Limit:** 20 invites per inviter per rolling 24 hours (`DIRECTORY_INVITE_DAILY_LIMIT`), counted from `directory_invites.created_at`; an unreadable count refuses. It keeps one user from eating the shared Resend day.
- **Analytics** (server-side, all against the INVITER's id, no names or emails): `directory_invite_sent` (role, channel, emailed), `directory_invite_opened` (role), `directory_invite_joined` (role, published).
- **No reward, deliberately.** The share reward is a once-per-team grant keyed on `workspaces.share_reward_claimed_at`, and referral credits pay only on an invited team's first payment; both exist precisely so nothing pays out on a signup, which costs nothing to fake with a throwaway email ("Referral credits", item 1). A per-signup bonus here would be the credit-farming machine that section warns about, and a creative who joins usually never pays. `joined_user_id` is recorded, so a reward on a verifiable event (for example the invitee's team making its first payment) can be added later without a migration.
- **Not built:** a generic, reusable personal link (each link is per invitee, so it can carry the name and role); reminders; marking `published_at` when somebody publishes later from Settings rather than on the welcome page; a lighter sign-up for creatives (the welcome flow still sends a new account through onboarding, which makes a team and a first rollout; the seeding section's open founder call).
- **Verified:** `lib/directory-invites.test.ts` (rules, limit, statuses, copy), `lib/directory-invites-server.test.ts` (the real create, list and the real welcome claim against an in-memory database: private profile, link hashed, email only when asked, failed email keeps the link, 21st invite refused, 24h window per inviter, not_ready before the migration, known person refused or reused without revoking, open/join/publish stamps, founder links still say Wena), `lib/directory-invites-guard.test.ts`, `lib/email/templates/directory-invite-render.test.ts`. Deliberate breaks (limit off by one, the join stamp dropped) were each caught. Full suite green, `tsc --noEmit` clean. **Not verified:** nothing in a browser (375px or desktop) or against the real database; no real email sent.
- **Activation:** apply `supabase/migrations/20261011210000_directory_invites.sql` to production (founder go-ahead). Nothing else.
