# Reward the first-rollout share: one month free (2026-10-11, v1.103.0, on by default)

> Moved out of CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-22 (verbatim). "This file", "above", "below" and "the X section" in here refer to the old single CLAUDE.md: find the named section in docs/notes/README.md.

Founder call: the share-to-socials sheet offers "Share your rollout and get 1 month free", and the FIRST tap of any share, copy, save or send button in it (any tab: Instagram, status, X, Snapchat, Message) gives the team 30 days free. A tap is enough (honor system). Once per team, ever. Rules in `apps/web/lib/share-reward.ts` (pure, tested in `share-reward.test.ts`); claim and offer check at `app/api/workspace/share-reward/route.ts` (GET `{ eligible, claimed }`, POST claims); client hook `components/share/useShareReward.ts`; banner `ShareRewardBanner` in `ShareSheet.tsx`.

- **Who sees it:** the owner or an admin of the person's PRIMARY team as the paywall resolves it (`getTeamAccessForUser`, so the same team the wall talks about), team not yet claimed, at least one real (non-test) rollout among the team members' rollouts, and access not already free (comped, allowlisted owner). Shown on every share sheet until claimed, including the auto-opened first-rollout one. Members never see it.
- **Once per team, server-enforced:** `workspaces.share_reward_claimed_at`, written by a conditional update (`.is('share_reward_claimed_at', null)`), so two taps on two phones give one month. The client claims on the next tick after the tap (`setTimeout(claim, 0)`), so nothing runs inside the gesture before Web Share's own call.
- **Trialing teams, and every team without access of its own** (walled, read-only, credits-only, grace, an unstamped trial): `workspaces.trial_ends_at` moves to max(now, current end, the owner's own per-account trial end) + 30 days, `computeExtendedTrialEnd`'s arithmetic. A trial outranks read-only and credits-only in `resolveTeamAccess`, so that is full access for 30 days in each. An unstamped trial (which fails open) becomes stamped at now + 30, which is still longer than the 3 days provisioning would have given.
- **Paying teams (owner's snapshot `accountStatus === 'paid'`): `workspaces.paid_bonus_days += 30`.** `resolveTeamAccess` treats the days after the owner's paid period (`ownerPaidThrough` = snapshot `expiresAt`, i.e. `subscriptions.expires_at`) as `state: 'paid'`, `reason: 'share_bonus'`, keeping the owner's tier. **Paystack is never touched.** Why this and not the alternatives: (1) Pushing `subscriptions.expires_at` would also carry through renewals (`advancePeriodEnd` extends from the current end), but it rewrites the money row, makes Settings' renewal date disagree with when Paystack actually charges, and inflates the days-remaining credit `quotePlanChange` gives on an upgrade. (2) Skipping a Paystack charge needs the subscription disabled and recreated with a new `start_date`, which is exactly the path that produced the 2026-09-10 card-trial incident. (3) A fixed bonus DATE on the workspace would be overtaken by the next renewal and the month silently lost. Days counted from the LIVE paid-through date avoid all three: the team keeps paying on schedule (no double charge, no broken renewal), the month rides behind every renewal, and it is used when they stop paying (cancel, or a failed renewal: past_due keeps access to `expires_at`, then the 30 days, then read-only). **Say this plainly if the founder asks "did paying teams get a free month": they got 30 extra days at the end, not a skipped charge.** Claims by an owner with no expiry (the `is_subscribed` back-compat accounts) are recorded with no date to show.
- **Comped / allowlisted:** no offer, nothing written.
- **Copy:** offer "Share your rollout and get 1 month free" (Gift icon, how-it-works behind an `InfoTip`, per the no-stacked-helper-lines rule); after the tap "1 month free added. Your team now has access until November 10, 2026." (or "1 month free added to your team." with no date). `lib/share-reward.ts` is on the rollout-vocabulary guard. Captions and posters never mention it, and a test reads `lib/social-share.ts` and `lib/social-share-card.tsx` to hold that.
- **Flag: `NEXT_PUBLIC_FEATURE_SHARE_REWARD`, ON BY DEFAULT whenever `NEXT_PUBLIC_FEATURE_SOCIAL_SHARE` is on**, deliberately breaking "flags start off" (the invite gate precedent: the founder asked for it live, and a Vercel env save is itself a deploy). Kill switch: set it to `false` in Vercel Web, then redeploy. Added to `turbo.json` and `.env.example`.
- **Migration `20261011120000_share_reward.sql`:** three columns on `workspaces` (`share_reward_claimed_at`, `share_reward_kind` in ('trial_extended','paid_bonus'), `paid_bonus_days` int not null default 0, 0..3660) and the `prevent_workspace_billing_self_update` guard extended to all three (an owner can otherwise write their own row with the anon key). It re-adds `legacy_rate_expires_at` with `if not exists` only because the guard names it. **Safe in either order:** before it is applied the GET fails and nobody is offered anything, and the paywall resolver reads `paid_bonus_days` in its OWN query (`readPaidBonusDays`, only when some owner lacks access) whose failure reads as "no bonus", because folding it into `WORKSPACE_COLUMNS` would fail the main read and that resolver fails OPEN. Applied by hand in the Supabase dashboard, like every migration here.
- **Ledger and analytics:** a `billing_events` row `share_reward_claimed` (kind, days, access_until, team_state) and the server PostHog event `share_reward_claimed`.
- **Known gap:** the admin Teams console and email audiences (`lib/admin-team-access.ts`) do not read `paid_bonus_days`, so a paying team inside its bonus month shows there as read-only. Deliberately not added to their selects, since that would break those pages before the migration is applied.
- **Not verified:** nothing has run in a browser or with a real session or database; the copy, banner and the claim have only been type-checked and unit-tested.
