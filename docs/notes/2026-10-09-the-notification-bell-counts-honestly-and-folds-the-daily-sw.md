# The notification bell counts honestly and folds the daily sweep (2026-10-09, v1.92.7, no flag)

> Moved out of CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-22 (verbatim). "This file", "above", "below" and "the X section" in here refer to the old single CLAUDE.md: find the named section in docs/notes/README.md.

Found by a live-app review: the bell's badge read "9+" while its spoken name said "20 unread", and the dropdown filled with near-identical "An activity is overdue" rows stamped "13h ago". **No flag, no schema change, no server change.**

- **One function for the badge and the spoken name.** `bellBadge` (`lib/notification-groups.ts`, pure and tested) returns the drawn label and the `aria-label` together. `GET /api/notifications` is capped at 20 rows, so the unread count is "at least this many" past the badge's cap of 9: the badge says "9+" and the spoken name says "more than 9 unread", and a test asserts the two can never speak a number the screen does not show.
- **A run of sweep rows is one line.** The daily due-date sweep writes one row per task, all within minutes of each other, in four tiers (`task_overdue`, `task_due_soon`, `task_due_3d`, `task_due_7d`). `groupNotifications` folds a run of the same tier (neighbours at most 30 minutes apart, so a 20-row sweep chains together while the day between two sweeps splits them) into a digest ("12 activities are overdue", the first titles, the newest time) that opens in place to the individual rows. Tiers never mix, a different notification landing in the middle does not split a run, assignments, comments, status changes and pitch reminders are never grouped, a lone sweep row stays an ordinary row, and a row with no usable date is never grouped. A digest sits where its newest row was, and its id depends only on that row, so it stays open as older rows are added behind it.
- **The bell is otherwise unchanged:** row click (mark read, navigate, open the activity), Mark all read, Clear, the realtime toast and peek-on-open all behave as before. The digest header is a real `<button aria-expanded aria-controls>`, and nested rows are indented.
- **Tests:** `lib/notification-groups.test.ts` (badge cap and spoken name, grouping rules, copy under the house rules, and source scans that the bell builds its badge through `bellBadge` and its list through `groupNotifications`). Five deliberate breaks, each caught.
- **Seen in a browser** through a throwaway page (deleted) with 12 overdue rows, an assignment and a comment, at 375px and 1440px: one digest line, no sideways scroll, expands to the twelve rows.
- **Not changed, and logged in `notes/needs-lucky.md`:** the API's 20-row cap can push older non-sweep notifications out of the list when a big sweep lands, and an app left open during a sweep can show a toast per inserted row.
