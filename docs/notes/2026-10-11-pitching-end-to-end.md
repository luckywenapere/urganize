# Pitching, end to end (2026-10-11, v1.106.0, founder)

> Moved out of CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-22 (verbatim). "This file", "above", "below" and "the X section" in here refer to the old single CLAUDE.md: find the named section in docs/notes/README.md.

Follow-up to v1.104.0: the founder asked for a holistic pass over the whole pitching flow. Faces lead everywhere, through one shared `components/pitching/CuratorFace.tsx` (photo or a deterministic gradient with initials; `size="fill"` for tiles, a number for round avatars). `SentPitch` gained optional `curatorPhotoUrl` (signed, from `loadCuratorPhotoUrls`, on all three routes that build one). Team side: the Pitching page is "Pitch / Send your song to a curator.", release choice is a chip row (hidden with one rollout), the sent list is called Answers and a reply renders as a message bubble under the curator's face; after a free send the composer shows a "Sent, {name} will answer here" card until the next pick; the first pick scrolls the send card into view on phones. Checkout shows the curators being paid; CHECKOUT_DISCLOSURE and every spec string are unchanged and still shown in full. Curator side (free pitches): tapping Listen opens the song and records Listened, then two large buttons, "Add it" and "Pass"; New and Listened are no longer buttons. Paid cards keep their flow. Public /pitch/{slug}: centred hero photo, follower total, one CTA, playlists as square tiles, footer links to urganize.app.
