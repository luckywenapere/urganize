## Pricing v3: 6-month and yearly prepay, the TRM fee, the margin view (2026-10-22, v1.164.0, no flag)

Founder approved the mocks (`docs/mocks/pricing-trm/index.html`) and the migration plan on 2026-10-22. The migration and the Paystack Plans each need his separate yes.

- **Prices** (`packages/pricing`, the only source): Solo ₦7,500, Manager ₦30,000 (`MANAGER_MONTHLY_NAIRA`), Label ₦100,000 a month. 6 months = 5 months' price (`SIX_MONTH_MONTHS_BILLED`), a year = 10 (`ANNUAL_MONTHS_BILLED`). `PRICE_LIST_VERSION = '2026-10-22'`. `BillingInterval` gained `six_month`.
- **One Paystack Plan per tier per period**: `PAYSTACK_PLAN_{SOLO,MANAGER,LABEL}_{1M,6M,12M}_NGN` (`lib/billing-plans.ts`). A period with no var is HIDDEN (`purchasableIntervals`, `purchasableIntervalsByTier`), and a tier is offered when any period has a Plan. **Do not merge before the vars are set**: with none set the wall offers nothing. Solo's 1M and 12M copy the existing ₦7,500 / ₦75,000 codes; the other seven are new Plans.
- **Grandfathering**: Paystack binds a subscription to its Plan, so renewals keep charging what they always did. A new checkout writes `subscriptions.price_locked_kobo` and `plan_version`; `applyRenewal` never touches either (a test reads its body). Plan changes still refuse a team whose `amount` is not today's list price (`isGrandfatheredRate`).
- **Prepay switch** (`quotePrepaySwitch` in `lib/plan-change.ts`, `/api/billing/change-plan`): monthly to 6 months or a year on the same tier. Credit = what they paid × unused part of a 30-day month, new period starts today. Paystack still needs the founder's manual swap (SWAP alert), like every plan change.
- **TRM fee** (`lib/trm/partner-fee.ts`, pure; `lib/trm/partner-ledger-server.ts`, the only writer of `trm_partner_ledger` and `workspaces.trm_registered_at` / `trm_plan`, guard test in `lib/pricing-v3.test.ts`). Manager → TRM Single Artist, Label → TRM Label, Solo and legacy plan ids never. Fixed yearly fee (₦20,100 / ₦101,000) by default, owed a twelfth per month covered (`monthly_prorata`), or the year upfront (`annual_upfront`), or a percent of what the team paid (`TRM_COST_BASIS=percent` + `TRM_REVENUE_SHARE_PCT`). An unset `TRM_FEE_*` falls back to the default, never zero (a test caught it reading 0). Plan-change top-ups write no ledger row. A team is "registered" on TRM (stamp + founder alert) only after a successful payment. TRM-REQUIREMENTS.md lists what TRM must confirm.
- **Distribution step** (`lib/distribution-entitlement.ts`, `/api/billing/distribution`, `DistributionPlanCard`): included / needs_prepay (only when `DISTRIBUTION_REQUIRES_PREPAY=true`, default false) / not on plan (Solo, none, lapsed). Lapse rules unchanged: releases already out stay out.
- **Admin `/admin/billing`** (`lib/billing-margin.ts` pure, `lib/billing-margin-server.ts` reads, internal accounts left out): per paying team this month: plan, period, how they paid (card / transfer / direct transfer), cash spread per month, estimated Paystack fee (card 1.5% + ₦100, ₦100 waived under ₦2,500, cap ₦2,000; transfer 1% cap ₦300), TRM owed, credits × `CREDIT_COST_KOBO` (684), contribution; totals with MRR, contribution per team, break-even teams from `FIXED_COSTS_MONTHLY_KOBO`. TRM CSV per month at `/api/admin/billing/trm-ledger?month=YYYY-MM`.
- **Analytics**: `plan_price_viewed`, `checkout_period_selected`, `prepay_upsell_shown`, `prepay_upsell_accepted`, `billing_checkout_started`; each carries `acquisition_path` (`lib/acquisition-path.ts`), which also rides the payment row and the charge ledger.
- **Cancel flow** budget bands follow the new prices, so the Manager and Label retention vars changed: `PAYSTACK_PLAN_MANAGER_RETENTION_20000/27000`, `PAYSTACK_PLAN_LABEL_RETENTION_70000/90000` (new Plans; a missing one hides that offer).
- **Migration** `20261022130000_pricing_v3_trm_fee.sql`: additive, backup and rollback in its header. Code tolerates it missing.
- **Not verified**: no real checkout, webhook, ledger row or CSV against a real database; screens checked only in the mocks.
