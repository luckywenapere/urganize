# A Released rollout stops asking you to fix things before distribution (2026-10-09, v1.92.5, no flag)

> Moved out of CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-22 (verbatim). "This file", "above", "below" and "the X section" in here refer to the old single CLAUDE.md: find the named section in docs/notes/README.md.

Found on the live app: a rollout dated Sep 9 2026 with 58 of 64 activities done read **Released** in the Catalogue (the derived status from v1.91.2) but its page still showed the collapsed "Fix these before you order distribution" banner (count 7). `DistributionStatusStrip` was mounted on every rollout except an empty shell (v1.91.1), and showed its readiness list until a hand-off was recorded; nothing looked at whether the rollout had come out. **No flag, no schema change, no status written.**

- **One rule, three surfaces.** `distributionStripMode` (`lib/distribution-strip-visibility.ts`, pure and tested) returns `none` for an empty shell (the v1.91.1 rule, moved here from an inline `isEmptyShell` in `WorkspaceView`), `rejections` when `displayStatus` (`lib/catalogue-table.ts`) says `released`, and `full` otherwise. Because it reads the same `displayStatus` as the Catalogue row and the desktop focused header, the header and the banner cannot disagree. Not a third definition of "has this happened": `displayStatus` is `lib/recap-eligibility.ts`'s rule.
- **Released hides the readiness list only.** `WorkspaceView` passes `showReadiness={distributionMode === 'full'}`, and the strip drops both "Fix these before you order distribution" and "Worth a look". The newest distributor answer (`DistributionRejectionList`) still shows on a Released rollout, because it is not a before-release check and may still need acting on. No post-release replacement banner was built; none existed to reuse. The Distribute scene is unchanged and still lists every check.
- **Archived is unchanged.** `displayStatus` returns `archived` before `released`, so an archived rollout gets the `full` strip it had before.
- **Tests:** `lib/distribution-strip-visibility.test.ts` (empty shell; drafts not yet out; the live regression case, past date with 58/64 done; release day itself; every activity done with no date; stored `released`; archived; agreement with `displayStatus` across cases; source scans that the rule imports `displayStatus` with no hand-rolled date check, that `WorkspaceView` mounts through it and passes `showReadiness`, and that the strip gates `showIssues` on it). Mutations: making the rule never return `rejections` fails 5 tests; restoring `main`'s `WorkspaceView.tsx` fails the wiring test.
- **Not verified in a browser or with a real session.** Preview pass: open a past-dated rollout that the Catalogue shows as Released and confirm no "Fix these…" banner on its main view; open a future-dated draft with missing details and confirm the banner is still there; open an archived rollout and confirm it looks as it did. Known edge (inherited from the shared rule): "today" is the browser's local midnight.
