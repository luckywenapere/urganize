# The founder cockpit at the top of /admin (2026-10-09, internal, no flag)

> Moved out of CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-22 (verbatim). "This file", "above", "below" and "the X section" in here refer to the old single CLAUDE.md: find the named section in docs/notes/README.md.

Founder ask: make the admin dashboard help the company succeed at its stage. The plan, the research behind it and the ordered backlog live in **`docs/admin/dashboard-roadmap.md`**; each later pass ships the top item under its "Next" and moves it to "Done". Internal (the `/admin` precedent), so no flag, no migration, no changelog entry and no version bump.

- **What it is.** `/admin` (the Overview) now opens with "This week": the North Star (weekly active paying teams) against last week, weekly active teams (paid or not) with its 4-week compounded weekly growth judged against YC's 5% / 10% bars, MRR and paying teams, activation, time to first rollout (median days, last 90 days of sign-ups), week-4 retention pooled over the newest 4 complete cohorts, Runway shown as "Not tracked" (cash and spend are not in the database; **superseded by iteration 6**: the founder types them in), an 8-week chart of weekly active teams, and a "Needs a message" list (paying teams under 2 active people this week, then teams that were active last week and are quiet this week). The dashboard links follow under "All dashboards", grouped Numbers / Operations / Content since iteration 4.
- **One set of definitions.** `lib/founder-cockpit.ts` is pure and takes the Scoreboard's own `ScoreboardInput` and the board built from it, so it counts nothing a second way; what it adds is time. This week's numbers equal the Scoreboard's exactly (a test holds that). `lib/founder-cockpit-format.ts` is the client-safe words half. `GET /api/admin/cockpit` reads through `loadScoreboardInput` behind `verifyAdmin` and writes nothing; `components/admin/FounderCockpitView.tsx` is display only. The hub fetches it AFTER its own gate, so the links show at once and a cockpit failure never hides them.
- **Honesty rules.** Growth from zero has no percentage (null, shown as the plain change). Empty data reads "No data yet" / "Not tracked", never a zero rate. Past weeks use TODAY's paying status and members, because neither is stored over time; the page says so. A team made after a week ended is left out of that week.
- **Iteration 5 (2026-10-10): churn, Draft in Emails, one read.** Details in the roadmap's Done list; the four things not to undo. (1) **Churn uses `subscriptions.started_at`, never `profiles.paid_at`, as the start of paying**: the renewal webhook rewrites `paid_at` on every charge, so it is the LATEST payment. Logo churn and gross MRR churn are worked out in `lib/admin-churn.ts` from the Scoreboard's own input; **net MRR churn and NRR are deliberately null ("Not tracked")** because a plan change overwrites `subscriptions.amount`, so upgrades and downgrades are not stored. Do not fill them with 1 minus gross churn. (2) **`/admin/emails/new` accepts `?to=&subject=&body=`** (`lib/email/compose-prefill.ts`): `to` must be one plain address or it is dropped (the form then opens on its usual audience, never aimed at a list), text is capped and stripped of control characters and only ever shown as input values; `page.tsx` wraps the client in `<Suspense>` so the route stays `○ Static`. A prefilled link never sends: Send and its confirm are unchanged, and every backing route keeps ADMIN_EMAILS. The cockpit's Needs a message rows link there first (`messageComposeHref`) and keep the mailto as "Or use my mail app". (3) **The cockpit and the Scoreboard share one read** (`scoreboardReadCache` in `lib/scoreboard-cache-server.ts`, 60 seconds, in-flight requests share it, failures are not kept, `?fresh=1` bypasses it and the Scoreboard's Refresh sends it). It is per server instance, so it helps only when both requests land on the same warm one. Every builder that reads the cached input must stay pure and leave its input alone; `lib/scoreboard-cache-server.test.ts` builds every number both pages send from a fresh read and from the cache twice and requires them identical. The UrgAns rows are now read after the shared read, at its `input.now`, so a cached read and the UrgAns windows end at the same moment. (4) **Stage owners live in `LIFECYCLE_STAGES`** (`lib/admin-message-list.ts`); a test reads `docs/strategy/company-strategy.md` section 6 and fails on drift. Verified: full Vitest in five batches (7,770 passing, 69 skipped), `tsc --noEmit` 0 errors, eslint clean on every touched file (one pre-existing warning in `ScoreboardAdminPageClient.tsx`), `next build` exit 0 with `/admin/emails/new` still static. **Not verified:** no browser or real admin session, so the 375px layout and the real churn numbers need a founder look.
- **Iteration 6 (2026-10-10): the founder's answers.** Details in the roadmap's Done list and its section 8. Four things not to undo. (1) **Our own accounts are left out of the cockpit AND the Scoreboard**, with the one list `/admin/metrics` uses: `internalAccountEmails()` in `app/api/admin/_shared.ts` (ADMIN_EMAILS + METRICS_INTERNAL_EMAILS; the metrics route's private copy was removed). The pure `withoutInternalAccounts` (`lib/admin-internal-accounts.ts`) drops teams one of us owns (with their releases and referrals), our people's activity and memberships elsewhere, and our own subscriptions, and returns a NEW input (the shared read cache's rows are never changed). Both routes call it, so the "cockpit equals Scoreboard" tests still hold; `?team=1` brings us back. User emails come from `extras.emailByUserId` (profiles the read already loads). (2) **Runway is typed in by the founder** on the cockpit and kept in `public.app_settings` key `founder_runway` (RLS on, no policies: service role only; the table's documented home for such settings, so no migration was needed or written). `GET/PUT/DELETE /api/admin/runway` (verifyAdmin on each method; `parseRunwayInput` checks every field, NGN or USD, Naira per dollar required with USD because billing, and so MRR, is Naira only). This is the ONLY production write the admin loop has added. The maths is pure in `lib/admin-runway.ts`: runway = cash ÷ (spend − MRR) from the as-of day; default alive projects weekly with spend flat and MRR at its own 4-week compounded rate rebuilt from subscription stretches (`payingStretch`), flat when there was no MRR 4 weeks ago. (3) **Churn gives a renewing subscription (active / past_due) Paystack's 3 retry days** (`PAYSTACK_RETRY_GRACE_DAYS`) after its last paid day before it counts as lost (`inRenewalGrace` in `lib/admin-churn.ts`); cancelled gets none. (4) **The UrgAns share counts every usage-log feature**, status updates and task outcome replies included, because both are model calls logged with a provider cost (no credits charged); `lib/admin-urgans-features-guard.test.ts` reads both routes. NRR / plan-change logging was NOT approved and is not built.
