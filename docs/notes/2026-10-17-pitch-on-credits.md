# Pitch on credits (2026-10-17, v1.157.0, URG-345 Phase 1, founder approved v2 mocks)

> Moved out of CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-22 (verbatim). "This file", "above", "below" and "the X section" in here refer to the old single CLAUDE.md: find the named section in docs/notes/README.md.

A pitch to a curator is paid from the team's ONE credit balance (allowance, top-up and trial all count, drawn trial → allowance → top-up like every other charge) instead of a Paystack checkout per pitch. Behind `NEXT_PUBLIC_FEATURE_PITCH_CREDITS`, which ships ON: `isPitchCreditsEnabled()` in `lib/pitch-credits.ts` is true unless the env says exactly `false`, and it also needs `NEXT_PUBLIC_FEATURE_PITCHING` and `NEXT_PUBLIC_FEATURE_URGANS_CREDITS`. The old Paystack paid flow stays cut (`NEXT_PUBLIC_FEATURE_PITCH_PAYMENTS` in `cut-features.json`); its tables and rows are kept, and its Payments section and cancelled notice were removed from PitchScene.

- **Tiers are Urganize's, never the curator's:** `curator_playlists.credit_price`, Free 0 / Standard 100 / Top 250 (`PITCH_CREDIT_TIERS`, config; the DB only checks 0..10000). Every playlist starts Free. Set them at `/admin/curator-invites` → Playlist tiers (`/api/admin/playlist-tiers`).
- **One function charges and creates:** `send_credit_pitches()` (migration `20261017120000_pitch_credits.sql`) takes a per-team advisory lock, reads each tier itself, checks the balance, the 10-waiting cap, paused curators and the one-waiting-per-playlist index, then writes the pitches (`payment_kind 'credits'`, `credits_charged`, `response_due_at` = now + 7 days; Free-tier pitches stay `'free'` with no deadline) and one ledger spend row per source, `spend:pitch:<id>:<source>`. Pitch ids are `md5(requestId || playlistId)`, so a retry or double tap with the same request charges once. Short balance answers `insufficient` and writes nothing. `planCreditCharge` in `lib/pitch-credits.ts` mirrors it for tests.
- **Routes:** `POST /api/pitching/send` (the picker), `GET /api/pitching/credits` (balance, account, canBuy). The free `POST /api/pitching/pitches` refuses a paid-tier playlist (409 `curator_credits`) so it is never a way around the charge. A paid-tier pitch to a curator on the sender's own team is refused (`curatorsOnTheTeam`, fails closed); Free-tier to your own playlist stays allowed.
- **Expiry:** `runCreditPitchSweep` (`lib/pitch-credits-server.ts`) rides the 15-minute pg_cron `/api/cron/pitch-payments?task=windows` and the daily backstop. `expire_credit_pitch()` refunds each spend row to its own source (`refund:pitch:<id>:<source>`), marks the pitch expired and adds the strike (3 in 90 days pauses, the paid rule). Not behind the flag.
- **Curator side:** a credits pitch is a paid pitch to the curator (15+ words, once, in the window, same PATCH rules), shown in naira only ("₦1,000 for your reply · N days left"). The trigger `record_credit_pitch_earning` writes `curator_earnings` (₦10 a credit, status `due`) on an on-time reply. Payouts are Phase 2: nothing moves money yet.
- **Top-up:** short on credits opens the sheet with the smallest covering pack (`smallestCoveringPack`), uses `/api/credits/checkout` with `returnTo: '/dashboard/pitching'`; the waiting send is kept in localStorage (`lib/pitch-credits-client.ts`, 2 h TTL), the bridge forwards `reference` to the rollout's Pitch tab, the picker verifies and sends with the same request id. iOS shows "Open Urganize in your browser to buy more credits."
- **Before the migration** every read falls back on 42703/PGRST202 to "everything is Free" and the send route writes free pitches the old way, so a push ahead of the migration breaks nothing.
- **Deviations from the mocks:** Boomplay is not a platform in the schema, so the segments are Spotify / Apple Music / Audiomack / YouTube; the song link and note (required, kept from the Oct 16 design) sit in a grouped block under the list once something is picked; the curator card keeps Accept / Pass rather than one Send reply; the curator earnings page (mock 10) is Phase 2.
