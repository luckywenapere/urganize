# A free look at the plan before credits are spent (2026-10-09, v1.92.0, no flag)

> Moved out of CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-22 (verbatim). "This file", "above", "below" and "the X section" in here refer to the old single CLAUDE.md: find the named section in docs/notes/README.md.

Found by using the live app: the UrgAns planner's footer says "This uses about 12 UrgAns credits. You have N credits." from its first question, and a new person had seen nothing of what those credits buy. **No flag**: it is read-only display inside an already-live surface, in line with the design overhaul's "nothing behind a flag" call and the v1.91.x precedent. No pricing, credit amount, hold or entitlement changed.

- **Built from the playbook, nothing else.** `lib/planner-template-preview.ts` (pure, client-safe) turns the hand-written template into the card's numbers: activity count, weeks, a count per phase (`derivePlanPhase`, the plan's and the timeline's own boundaries) and up to four example names (the first required activity of each phase). No request, no model, no credits, no database. `components/planner/PlannerTemplatePreview.tsx` draws it.
- **Same playbook as the plan.** The preview repeats `getPlannerTemplate`'s rule (EP and album fall back to the single playbook while `NEXT_PUBLIC_FEATURE_EP_ALBUM_TEMPLATES` is off, and the card says so); that function lives in a server file, so a test pins the two to the same template id in both flag states.
- **Where it shows.** In `PlannerIntakeModal`, on the first question the person actually sees (`step === firstStep`) once a type is known: below the type choice when the planner asks it, below "Who's the artist?" when type-first create already picked it. It is not on the create screen's type cards, because the founder dropped the playbook numbers from those cards on 2026-09-29. The footer's cost-and-balance line is untouched and sits right under it, so the balance shows beside the preview on a phone too, where the top bar has no credits chip.
- **Labelled as the start, not the result.** "Free preview" / "Your starting plan" / "This is our single playbook. UrgAns tailors it to your answers." UrgAns may drop activities, so the number is the playbook's, never promised as the final count.
- **Tests:** `lib/planner-template-preview.test.ts` (counts, phases and examples read from each real template; flag on and off; agreement with `getPlannerTemplate`; no `fetch` during a build; a source scan that the preview's two files reach no request, model, credits or database; the modal still mounts it beside `embeddedCostLine`; house copy rules). Two mutations (EP and album forced to the single playbook; a `fetch` added to the build) were each caught.
- **Not verified in a browser.** Preview pass: open the planner from Add rollout (type-first create on) at 375px and 1440px in both themes, confirm the card under Question 1 of 6 reads the single playbook's numbers and the footer still shows the cost and "You have N credits"; open it with no type (an UrgAns chip or a note) and confirm the card appears only after a type is picked.
