# Accessibility and copy pass (2026-09-30, v1.53.0, no flag)

> Moved out of CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-22 (verbatim). "This file", "above", "below" and "the X section" in here refer to the old single CLAUDE.md: find the named section in docs/notes/README.md.

Origin: the `better-interface` skill (with the `shadcn/ui` and `jakubkrehel` skill packs, installed globally) was run over every screen by six read-only agents. Every area came back Block. What follows is what was fixed and the rules that keep it fixed. **No flag and no migration.** It shipped as v1.53.0 because people can see it: clearer field outlines, pinch-zoom, one password rule and the wording fixes.

**Everything decidable was pulled into `lib/` and tested first**, because vitest reaches `lib/` and nothing else. The pure halves are `text-contrast`, `dialog-focus`, `dialog-stack`, `radio-group-keys`, `row-activation`, `toast-timing`, `oauth-consent`, `onboarding-next-hint`, `auth-callback-error`, and `chappi/{confirmation-tone,transcript-announcement}`.

### Rules worth not undoing

1. **Text contrast is a test, not a taste.** `lib/text-contrast.test.ts` parses the shipped `globals.css` and asserts 4.5:1 for secondary and tertiary text on every ground in all three theme blocks, plus the dark-locked island faint tokens. The tokens were deepened to meet it (light secondary `75 71 64`, tertiary `106 102 95`; dark tertiary `135 136 129`).
2. **One dialog contract.** `useDialogFocus` (with `lib/dialog-focus.ts` and `lib/dialog-stack.ts`) moves focus in, traps Tab, restores focus to the opener, and lets only the **topmost** dialog trap. A dialog yields if focus is inside a different `[aria-modal]` it does not contain. Mark the control to focus with `data-autofocus`, never React's `autoFocus`: `autoFocus` fires during commit, before the hook has noticed which element opened the dialog, so focus could never go back. About 25 overlays use it; `components/shared/DialogPanel.tsx` is the wrapper for the simple ones. The old `components/events/focusTrap.ts` is deleted.
3. **A button-based radio group needs the keys too.** `lib/radio-group-keys.ts` gives arrows, Home and End with a roving tab stop. Native `<input type="radio">` already has them.
4. **Do not claim a role you did not build.** `role="menu"` and `role="listbox"` promise arrow-key movement. `HelpMenu`, `NotificationsBell`, the Show page menu and the three rollout/assignee pickers only ever had Tab, so they are disclosures now (`aria-expanded` + `aria-controls`, a `role="group"` panel, `aria-current` plus a check mark on the chosen row). `WorkspaceMenu` and `CalendarOverflowMenu` really implement the keys and keep `role="menu"`. `ReleaseCombobox` and `SongPicker` are real comboboxes.
5. **State is never colour alone.** Selected chips, the timeline tones, thumbs and the recording mic all carry an icon or a word.
6. **Live regions exist before their text.** `ToastProvider`'s container is always mounted; the UrgAns transcript has an always-mounted sr-only status; about 80 inline error messages now carry `role="alert"`. Toast lifetime is `toastDismissMs` (longer for errors and long messages).
7. **Landmarks.** `AppShell` renders a skip link and `<main id="main-content" tabIndex={-1}>`; `WorkspaceView`'s nested `<main>` became a `<div>`. The auth pages have a `<main>`, one `<h1>` and a real tab title (a `layout.tsx` beside each client page).
8. **A disabled button says why.** The onboarding wizard shows `describeNextBlocker`'s sentence beside a disabled Next and points `aria-describedby` at it.
9. **The OAuth consent screen shows where the approval goes (`describeRedirectTarget`), not the app's registered name.** That name is whatever the client typed at open dynamic registration, so showing it would lend borrowed trust to anyone who calls themselves "Claude". The redirect host is where the code is actually sent, and the server has already validated it.
10. **The dark public pages get their own focus ring** (`.epk-island`, `.credits-island`, `.capture-island`, `.kit-island`): the global ring follows the viewer's theme, which is forest green for a light-theme visitor and about 2.6:1 on the locked ink ground.
11. **Public pages no longer show a blank tab or Next's default 404.** `app/not-found.tsx` is new, and `/k`, `/p`, `/pack` got `loading.tsx`.
12. **`Button isLoading` keeps its own name** (`aria-busy`, and the label stays as sr-only text) instead of turning into "Loading". `Input` drops the square inner focus ring that the app-wide `:focus-visible` rule drew inside its rounded pill. `HelpTip` opens on keyboard focus only (a tap used to open it and then toggle it shut on Android) and has a 44px hit area.
13. **A sign-out on the paywall page.** `TeamWall` has no chrome, and gave someone on the wrong account no way off it.
14. **A form field's outline is a token, and the token holds 3:1.** `border-stroke-control` (`--color-stroke-control`; the legacy `--border-control` is derived from it) clears 3:1 against every ground in all three theme blocks (WCAG 1.4.11), checked by `lib/text-contrast.test.ts`. The hairline tiers (`stroke-subtle`, `stroke-default`, `stroke-strong`, about 1.1 to 1.4:1) are for cards and dividers, and had been copied onto about a hundred inputs, so a field was close to invisible on cream. `lib/field-border-guard.test.ts` reads every `<input>`, `<select>` and `<textarea>` in `app/`, `components/` and `packages/ui` through `lib/jsx-scan.ts` (a small tag scanner; it follows a class const in the same file, not one imported from another file, so it is a floor and not a proof) and fails on a hairline, on the calendar's `--cc-border` used as a field border, and on a hover that fades. A field that is bare until hovered (`border-transparent`, the task pop-up's inline rows) is exempt on purpose. The two dark-locked public forms (credits, capture) and the calendar have their own `--credits-`, `--capture-` and `--cc-field-border` tokens, held to 3:1 by the same contrast test. Hover is `hover:border-content-secondary`, which gets stronger.
15. **One password rule, `lib/password-rules.ts`, and it is 8.** Sign-up, accepting an invite, joining as a curator, resetting and Settings all read `MIN_PASSWORD_LENGTH`, so raising it is one edit. Before, the first three took 6 and the last two demanded 8, so someone could make a password and then be unable to type it back on the reset page. **Signing in is never held to it**: accounts made before this can have a 6-character password, and a `minLength` on the login form would lock them out of their own work. `lib/password-rules-guard.test.ts` fails on a literal length comparison, on a printed character count, on a password field with a literal `minLength`, on a new-password form missing from its list, and on the login form importing the rule. No trimming and no character-mix rule (a space is a character, and forced symbols only breed predictable substitutions). **Supabase keeps its own minimum** (Dashboard, Authentication, Providers, Email): raise it to 8 there so the rule also holds for anything that skips these forms. That is a dashboard setting and not something code can do.
16. **Pinch-zoom is open; one-finger sideways drift and double-tap zoom stay closed.** `touch-action: pan-y pinch-zoom` on `html` and `body`, and `pan-x pinch-zoom` on `.overflow-x-auto`. The swipe-drift fix (2026-08-17) only needed horizontal panning closed, but `pan-y` alone also switched off pinch, which failed WCAG 1.4.4 for a reader who needs to enlarge the page. `lib/touch-zoom-guard.test.ts` holds the exact values, refuses `manipulation`, `auto` and `pan-x` on the document, and fails on any viewport that sets a scale limit. **Still do not put an `overflow` property on `html`**: it breaks `position: sticky` app-wide (the #318/#321 landmine).
17. **Corners are rounded, and the tokens now say so.** `--radius-sm` through `--radius-2xl` were `0px` from 2026-05-03 while almost every component asked for rounded corners through Tailwind, so 'the square Brand Bible look' was a stale variable and not a rule: the Bible (all 17 pages read, 2026-09-30) sets nothing about corner shape, and the July restyle kept the rounded scale. The variables are 4, 6, 8, 12, 16 and 9999 px now, equal to the Tailwind preset, and `lib/radius-tokens.test.ts` fails if they drift or go back to zero. **`.input` no longer sets `border-radius`.** It read `--radius-lg`, which won over every caller's `rounded-*` (it is plain CSS after the Tailwind layers) and drew square fields nobody asked for. Every caller found sets its own `rounded-*`, so they now get the corners they asked for; the visible changes are `TaskQuickCreateModal` and the `TaskMemberSelect` trigger, which ask for 18px and were square.
18. **The legacy text tiers are derived, and the `.input` states are readable.** `--text-secondary` and `--text-tertiary` (the set `.field-label`, `.saas-eyebrow`, `.kbd` and `.icon-btn` read) are `rgb(var(--color-text-*))` in all three theme blocks. They were a second hand-kept hex set that was missed when the rgb tokens were corrected, so light tertiary sat at 2.6:1 and dark at 3.5:1 after rule 1 said the job was done. `.input::placeholder` uses tertiary (it used the decorative quaternary tier, about 2:1 on ink) and `.input:hover` uses the secondary text colour (it used `--border-strong`, so the outline faded when you pointed at the field). Both are pinned in `lib/text-contrast.test.ts`.

### Copy: the sweep and its ratchet

- An audit found 186 em dashes, 34 uses of the banned product word, and sentences that assumed a person's gender, all in screens nobody had listed in a guard. They are fixed, along with the object word on the pitching, onboarding and date screens and the stale "draft the plan" wording.
- **`lib/house-copy-ratchet.test.ts` sweeps every component and page** for the em dash (including `&mdash;`), "ship", "workspace" and gendered pronouns, so a new file is covered the day it is written. Its `BASELINE` is frozen and can only shrink (a second test fails if an entry becomes clean). Out of scope on purpose: `app/api` and `app/admin` (server log lines look like copy to this reader), and `lib/` (tool schemas, prompts and playbooks are read by a model or are versioned data). `lib/press-kit-copy-guard.test.ts` still holds the named surfaces to the stricter invented-number and job-title rules.
- **`lib/copy-scan.ts` was widened.** It used to miss JSX text bounded by `{` or `}` (`Not you?{' '}`, `{count} items left`), which is where an em dash was hiding in a sentence a guard listed as clean. `adjacentJsxText` reads those runs with `isProseRun`, which rejects code and blanks HTML entities first (an entity ends in the `;` that marks a statement). Mutation-tested through all four rules against a real sentence.
- **A mutation must be confirmed to have applied.** The em dash mutation first "survived" because bash's ANSI-C quoting of the character's escape did not produce it on this platform. The tool layer also collapses a doubled backslash inside inputs, which broke two scripts, and truncates a command past about 8k characters; build such text from a file write, not a long heredoc.

### Still open (not done, deliberately)

- **Founder follow-ups from the four decisions** (made 2026-09-30 on the founder's delegation; rules 14 to 17): raise Supabase's minimum password length to 8 (Dashboard, Authentication, Providers, Email), and try pinch-zoom on a real phone. The one new gesture allowed is two-finger pinch; a one-finger sideways swipe must still not move the page.
- **Not built:** 44px targets on the `TaskDetailView` icons, the tasks-panel dock icons and `TourOfferCard` (they clear the 24px AA minimum); `<form>` wrappers on the Settings sections; a save cue and cap message on `DirectoryProfileSection`; the UrgAns system prompt still carries about 26 em dashes (it is instruction to a model, and rewriting it is a mechanical follow-up the structural test makes safe).
- **Metadata, capture and the content calendar** are still switched off in production (v1.21.0).

### Verified, and what is not

5415 tests pass (run alone: a run with `tsc` and `eslint` going at the same moment timed out 9 tests in 6 files that all pass alone), ESLint holds the 3-error / 83-warning baseline (the three are the known `react-hooks/refs` reads in `WorkspaceView.tsx`), `tsc` is clean apart from the 8 documented `confirmed-action.test.ts` errors, and `next build` is green with `/auth`, `/onboarding`, `/settings`, `/dashboard`, `/locked`, both password pages and the OAuth screen still `○ Static` (and `/k`, `/p`, `/pack` `ƒ`). The compiled CSS was read and not assumed: `border-stroke-control`, `hover:border-content-secondary`, `--color-stroke-control`, `pan-y pinch-zoom`, `pan-x pinch-zoom`, `--radius-lg:8px` and the `.input` border, placeholder and hover rules all survive minification. **Twenty mutations, each killed by the test written for it** (a hairline on a field, a literal password length, a dropped `pinch-zoom`, zero radius, the old placeholder tier, and so on). Rendered in a browser through a throwaway page (deleted): a hairline field beside a control-border field, `.input`, `<select>`, `<textarea>`, the shared `Input` and a 16px-corner modal, in dark and light, plus the real `/auth` email field at 375px (resting outline `rgb(110, 111, 105)`, 16px text, no horizontal scroll).

**Never exercised, because no authenticated session can be minted here:** every dialog's focus trap and restore, the disclosure menus, the radio keys, the toast and error announcements, the skip link, the onboarding hint and the paywall sign-out. The roughly 100 swept field class strings are covered by the guard and the compile check and not by looking at each screen: only the harness and `/auth` were rendered. Pinch-zoom was checked as a computed style and a guard, not with two fingers on a device. No screen reader was used at all. Two source-scanning tests (`body-scroll-lock-guard`, `icons-registry`) each failed once in a full run and passed alone and on three re-runs; the cause was not found and is likely load, which is also why the timeouts above happened.
