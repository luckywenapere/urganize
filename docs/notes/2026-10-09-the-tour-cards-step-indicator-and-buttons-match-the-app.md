# The tour card's step indicator and buttons match the app (2026-10-09, v1.92.4, no flag)

> Moved out of CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-22 (verbatim). "This file", "above", "below" and "the X section" in here refer to the old single CLAUDE.md: find the named section in docs/notes/README.md.

Found by a live-app review: the guided tour's pagination looked unlike the rest of the design system. Before: a bare "1 of 3" in 11px tertiary mono at the bottom left, a "Skip" text link about 24px tall (`px-2 py-1 text-xs`), and Next as `Button size="sm"` (32px). No Back control exists and none was added (the store has no `prev`; that would be a feature). Change is markup only, in `components/tours/TourOverlay.tsx`; no schema, no AI, nothing about pricing.

- **Step indicator follows the stepped-flow pattern already in the app.** `TeamBriefSection`'s guided mode says "Step N of M" in `font-mono text-[11px] uppercase tracking-[0.14em] text-content-tertiary` beside a `h-1.5 rounded-full bg-stroke-strong` track with a `bg-brand` fill (the meter rule in "Theme tokens" above). The tour card now does the same, on its own row above the title, so a long last-step label ("Go to Team") never squeezes it. The bar is `aria-hidden`; the words carry it. Tertiary on `bg-bg-elevated` measures 4.7:1 (dark) and 5.3:1 (light), above AA for small text.
- **The label and bar width come from `tourStepProgress` in `lib/tours.ts`** (pure, tested, clamped so a stale index never reads "Step 4 of 3"), the same "logic in lib/ so vitest can hold it" reason the tour copy lives there.
- **44px controls built from tokens.** Skip is `motion-focus min-h-11 rounded-full px-4 text-sm text-content-secondary hover:bg-bg-hover` (the `OnboardingBar` close-button idiom). Not `Button variant="ghost"`, because that variant hovers with a hard-coded `bg-white/[0.04]` that does nothing in light mode. Next is `Button size="md" className="min-h-11"`. The action row is `justify-end` and `flex-wrap`, so at 375px (card is `min(20rem, 100vw - 1.5rem)`) the two buttons wrap rather than overflow.
- **Tests:** `lib/tours.test.ts` (`tourStepProgress`: labels, clamping, empty tour) and `lib/tour-overlay-guard.test.ts` (a source scan of the returned JSX: label from the helper, meter tokens, `min-h-11` on Skip and on the primary `Button`, no `size="sm"`, no hex/rgb literal or white/black utility). Dropping `min-h-11` from Skip, or putting Next back to `size="sm"`, each fails it.
- **Not verified in a browser or with a real session.** Preview pass: start the tour on Home (Settings → Account → replay) at 375px with the bottom bar showing and at 1440px, in both themes; confirm "Step 1 of 3" and the bar read clearly, Skip and Next are easy to tap, the card still flips above anchors in the bottom bar, and the last step's "Go to Team" / "Done" fits.
