# Opportunities at the top of Home (2026-10-14, v1.123.0, URG-266, live at merge, no flag)

> Moved out of CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-22 (verbatim). "This file", "above", "below" and "the X section" in here refer to the old single CLAUDE.md: find the named section in docs/notes/README.md.

From the Oct 14 build walkthrough: Opportunities is the one live area that gives a team a reason to open the app between releases, so Home now opens with it. Rules in `lib/home/opportunities-card.ts` (pure, tested in `opportunities-card.test.ts`); card in `components/home/OpportunitiesCard.tsx`, drawn first by `HomeBlocks` (so it shows on the quiet and the busy Home, not on the brand-new Home, which stays one line and one button), fenced on its own and outside `orderHomeBlocks` because it loads its own data.

- **What it shows:** up to three items, each with its title, its kind (Grant, Brand, Residency...), "Suggested" when it is a matched suggestion, and the deadline in words ("Closes in 12 days", "No fixed deadline" for a rolling grant, "Deadline passed"), plus the match reason or who it is with. Every item and "See all" open `/dashboard/opportunities`; there is no per-item deep link there yet.
- **How matching works, and why the card does not match on its own:** first the team's new grant suggestions (`GET /api/opportunities/grant-suggestions`, the weekly run's picks against the team's grant profile: country, genres, career stage, who applies, needs), newest first, closed rounds dropped. Then the team's own open opportunities (`GET /api/opportunities`, status not won/lost/passed), newest added first, as the fallback and the filler. Never the same title twice. Suggested grants are paid-only (founder, 2026-10-11), so the card deliberately does NOT run `matchProgramme` against the curated list itself: that would hand the paid feed to every team through Home. The onboarding persona (URG-262) is not read; the grant profile's applicant type already carries "who applies".
- **No new table, no migration, no new route.** The strip's fetch moved into `components/opportunities/load-grant-suggestions.ts` so the strip and the card read the list the same way; the card shares `useOpportunitiesStore` with the page.
- **Empty state, unlike the other Home groups:** "No open opportunities yet" with a Find opportunities button. Hidden altogether (no empty card) when the team's list cannot be read: an artist-scoped teammate gets a 403 there. A skeleton holds the space while both reads settle, so Home does not jump.
- **Analytics:** `home_opportunity_clicked` (browser) with `target` (item, see_all, empty), `source` (suggested, saved), `kind`, `position`, `shown`. No titles or names. With page views of `/dashboard/opportunities` this answers the issue's measure (card clicks and opportunity opens per weekly active user).
- **Not verified in a browser or with a real session.** Worth one pass at 375px and 1440px in dark and light: a paid team with suggestions, a team with only saved opportunities, a team with none (empty state), and an artist-scoped teammate (no card).
