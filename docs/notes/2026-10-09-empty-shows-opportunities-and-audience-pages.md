# Empty Shows, Opportunities and Audience pages (2026-10-09, v1.92.3, no flag)

> Moved out of CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-22 (verbatim). "This file", "above", "below" and "the X section" in here refer to the old single CLAUDE.md: find the named section in docs/notes/README.md.

Found by a live-app review: the three dashboard areas looked bare to a team with nothing in them. Each was checked with zero records; only Opportunities needed work. No schema change, no AI, nothing about pricing or credits.

- **Opportunities: changed.** The bespoke "Nothing here yet" card had no button in it (the only Add was the header's, which on a phone sits full width above it). It is now the shared `EmptyState` with one line on what the page is for and an "Add opportunity" button that opens the same form. The words are `OPPORTUNITIES_EMPTY_COPY` in `lib/opportunities.ts`, held to the help-copy fifth-grader bar (`findHelpCopyProblems`) by `lib/opportunities.test.ts`. **One Add button at a time**, the Shows page's rule: the header carries it only once something is saved, and the empty state steps aside while the add form is open. A failed first load now offers "Try again" (`load({ force: true })`), the Relationships pattern, instead of a bare red line.
- **Shows: left as it was, on purpose.** `ShowsPageClient` already has three empty states that each explain and act: no artists ("Add an artist first" with a link to Settings → Artists), can book ("No shows yet" with the Book a show button, the header's button hidden until a show exists), and a teammate who cannot book (a sentence, no button, because there is nothing they can do). Its "can book" sentence is 19 words, one over the help-copy bar; that bar covers new copy only, so it was not rewritten.
- **Audience: left as it was, on purpose.** It is not built, so there are no records to be empty: the page is the shared `ComingSoon` screen (what it will do, three lines, and "Tell me when it is ready"). There is no create action to offer, and fan capture (the nearest live thing) is switched off in production, so no "meanwhile" link was added.
- **Tests:** the copy check plus a source scan of `OpportunitiesPageClient.tsx` (uses `EmptyState` with that copy and an `openNew` button; header action only when `opportunities.length > 0`; empty state hidden while the form is open; Try again on a failed load). Restoring `main`'s page fails four of them.
- **Not verified in a browser or with a real session.** Preview pass: open Opportunities on a team with none at 375px and 1440px in both themes, confirm one Add button, tap it, confirm the form opens and the empty panel goes, save one, and confirm the header button comes back.
