# EPs and albums to stores (#1115, v1.256.0, 2026-11-07)

Founder approved the mocks (`docs/mocks/eps-and-albums/mock.html`) on Nov 7, 2026. Extends Send to TRM (`2026-10-19-send-to-trm.md`), which was singles only.

- **Songs:** table `release_tracks` (migration `20261107180000_release_tracks.sql`, applied Nov 7; RLS on, service role only). A rollout with no rows is a single whose song is the rollout itself, exactly as before. The first "Add a song" writes two rows: song 1 copied from the rollout (title, ISRC, explicit, featured, lyrics) and an empty song 2. `releases.release_type` follows the row count (1 single, 2 to 6 EP, 7+ album) on every add, remove and reorder (`lib/trm/tracks-server.ts`). Songs can't change while the release is with TRM.
- **Per song:** title, featured artists, explicit (required), ISRC (optional, TRM assigns), lyrics, credits `rollout` (uses the rollout's credits) or `own` (`credits` jsonb: `[{name, roles, masterSplit}]`). Genre, language, label, cover art and rights stay per rollout.
- **Rules (pure, `lib/trm/release.ts`):** `songsOf`, `releaseKindOf` (count wins once there are 2; with one, the rollout's own type, and a 1-song EP/album blocks with "Only 1 song"), per-song blocking rows keyed `song:<id>` with `trackId`, `buildTrmTrack` per song.
- **Plan gate:** `distributionAccess` from `packages/pricing` on the kind the songs make. Checked on the payload (UI) AND server-side in prepare and submit (`storeAccessRefusal`, 402 `needs_monthly_plan` / `needs_yearly_plan`). Pro/Label monthly = singles + EPs; yearly = albums too. Solo/Manager get "Move to Pro" until the Distribution add-on (billing-addons session) changes `loadTeamDistribution`'s decision; the gate then picks it up with no change here.
- **Masters:** one per song, browser straight to TRM with `track_sequence`. Kept in `distribution_submissions.track_audio` `[{trackId, sequence, assetId, fileName}]`; song 1's also in `audio_asset_id` (singles unchanged). A master counts only for the same song at the same place (`mastersFor`), so a reorder after upload asks for the moved songs again.
- **/pricing:** EPs "Included" on Pro/Label, Albums "Included when you pay yearly"; Solo/Manager "From your own TRM account" until the add-on ships.
- **Not verified end to end:** TRM has no sandbox, so the first real EP send is the live test. Open with TRM: whether a re-presign at the same `track_sequence` replaces the old audio asset.
