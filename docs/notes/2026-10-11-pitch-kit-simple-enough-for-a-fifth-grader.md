# Pitch kit, simple enough for a fifth grader (2026-10-11, v1.108.0, founder)

> Moved out of CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-22 (verbatim). "This file", "above", "below" and "the X section" in here refer to the old single CLAUDE.md: find the named section in docs/notes/README.md.

Founder brief: "look at it from the point of a fifth grader that wants to derive value from that tool. It is too overwhelming." Holistic pass over the rollout pitch kit scene and the standing press kit editor; no data, route or action changes. `components/releases/PitchKitSection.tsx`: a hero (cover, one line, a 0 to 3 progress bar, one "Write it for me" button with an optional hint, cost line) then three numbered steps that turn into checks: Tell the story, Who made it, Share it. The story and the credits autosave on blur (no Save buttons); "Use what my team sent" resets credits to automatic. Share it is the artist kit's state as one action (Make / Open the press kit, or Copy link + See it when published). The old checklist row and the kit-status card are gone from the screen (`pitchKitChecklist` is still computed server-side). Press kit editor (`PressKitPageClient.tsx`): rail grouped "Start here" (`PRESS_KIT_ESSENTIAL_SECTIONS`: header, bios, assets, contact) and "Extras", status as a check / half ring / empty ring instead of mono chips, "N of 4 must-haves done" in the header, the section hint moved under the section title, a "Next: …" button walking the rail order, history renamed "Undo changes". `PublishPanel` leads with one state ("Ready to share?" / "Your kit is live") and one action (Share it / Copy link), with visibility, embargo, interviews and Turn off the link under a `<details>` "More options"; the `public` visibility option is only listed for kits already set to it. Bios open on the short box; medium and long behind "Add longer versions" unless already filled. `PRESS_KIT_SECTION_LABELS` renamed (About, Bio, New release, Photos, Videos, Big numbers, Shows...) and these also title the public `/k/{slug}` page and Chappi's lines. Copy in AutofillSummary, RolloutBanner and SectionDraftCard shortened. **Not verified in a browser**: type-check and unit tests only.
