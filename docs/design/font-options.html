Urganize font options

Same Home screen content in each card, only the interface font changes. Headlines stay in Fraunces here so you judge the body font on its own; the headline options are lower down. Dates and small labels stay in Space Mono. Open this on your phone as well: type is judged at the size people actually read it.

Where we are today: Space Grotesk for body and interface, Fraunces for headlines, Space Mono for dates and counts. Space Grotesk is the brand-bible font, and it is also one of the most common "designed" grotesques on the web right now, which is why it can read as generic.
